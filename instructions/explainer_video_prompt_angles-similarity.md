# Vox-Style Video Explainer Prompt & Script: Geometric Similarity (Class 10 Math)

## 📌 Video Overview & Production Guidelines
* **Target Audience:** Class 10 Students (CBSE / High School Geometry)
* **Visual Style:** Modern Vox-style motion graphics, minimalist high-contrast aesthetics, 2D/3D visual overlays, kinetic typography, glowing ray projections, highlighted geometric boundaries, and dashed vector lines.
* **Tone:** Energetic, clear, intuitive, and highly visual.

---

## 🎬 Scene-by-Scene Script & Motion Graphics Breakdown

### **Scene 1: Hook & Intuition (0:00 – 0:10)**
* **Voiceover:** 
  > "When you zoom in on a photo on your phone, does the shape of the photo change? No! It gets bigger, but every angle and proportion stays exactly the same. That’s the core idea of **Similarity** in geometry."
* **Visuals & Vox-Style Overlays:**
  * Clean dark slate background. A photo of a polygon floats in 3D space.
  * A pinch-to-zoom vector icon appears; the polygon scales up smoothly while glowing grid lines appear behind it to visually demonstrate proportion preservation.

---

### **Scene 2: The Shadow Projection Activity (0:10 – 0:30)**
* **Voiceover:** 
  > "Imagine a single light bulb hanging from the ceiling at point $O$. Directly beneath it, we place a cardboard quadrilateral $ABCD$, and below that, a table where its shadow $A'B'C'D'$ falls."
* **Visuals & Vox-Style Overlays:**
  * 3D vector scene of a glowing hanging light bulb labeled **$O$**.
  * A semi-transparent plane represents the cardboard polygon **$ABCD$**.
  * Four bright dashed light rays shoot out from point $O$, passing directly through vertices $A, B, C, D$ to project onto the table, drawing the enlarged shadow **$A'B'C'D'$**.
  * Neon yellow vector arrows mark the light rays $OA \rightarrow A'$, $OB \rightarrow B'$, $OC \rightarrow C'$, $OD \rightarrow D'$.

---

### **Scene 3: Angle Matching & Side Ratios (0:30 – 0:50)**
* **Voiceover:** 
  > "Notice two key facts: First, every angle in the shadow matches the original shape exactly. Second, every side length expands by the exact same multiplier!"
* **Visuals & Vox-Style Overlays:**
  * High-contrast split screen showing $ABCD$ on the left and $A'B'C'D'$ on the right.
  * **Angles:** Glowing neon-green arc overlays highlight $\angle A$ and $\angle A'$, accompanied by a dynamic digital protractor reading identical degree values ($\angle A = \angle A'$). This rapidly repeats for $\angle B, \angle C, \angle D$.
  * **Sides:** A ruler vector animation measures side $AB$ and side $A'B'$, displaying the ratio fraction $\frac{AB}{A'B'}$. A Vox-style formula card animates into view:
    $$\frac{AB}{A'B'} = \frac{BC}{B'C'} = \frac{CD}{C'D'} = \frac{DA}{D'A'}$$

---

### **Scene 4: The Core Mathematical Rule (0:50 – 1:00)**
* **Voiceover:** 
  > "So remember: Two polygons are **similar** if and only if **all corresponding angles are equal**, and **all corresponding sides are in the same ratio**."
* **Visuals & Vox-Style Overlays:**
  * Kinetic typography highlights the two fundamental criteria in bold neon yellow and cyan:
    1. **Equal Corresponding Angles:** $\angle A = \angle A', \angle B = \angle B', \angle C = \angle C', \angle D = \angle D'$
    2. **Proportional Corresponding Sides:** $\frac{AB}{A'B'} = \frac{BC}{B'C'} = \frac{CD}{C'D'} = \frac{DA}{D'A'}$
  * Clean summary card transition with subtle geometric grid animation.
