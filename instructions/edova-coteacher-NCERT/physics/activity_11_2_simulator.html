Think about it: Are the ammeter readings different for the three components? What does that tell you about each one's resistance?

Yes — the same four cells push a different current through each component. A higher reading means the component opposed the current less (lower resistance); a lower reading means it opposed the current more (higher resistance). The torch bulb's thin filament resists current the most, the nichrome wire the least, and the 10 W bulb sits in between.
⚠ Always remove the key from the plug right after noting the ammeter reading — this matches the real lab instruction and prevents the wire/filament from overheating.
