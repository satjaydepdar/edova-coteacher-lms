**Chapter 5 – Arithmetic Progressions**
**Class:** X  |  **Subject:** Mathematics  |  **Time:** 30 minutes

## Section A: Sum-Based Reasoning

**Q1.** If the sum of the first $p$ terms of an AP is $q$, and the sum of the first $q$ terms is $p$, find the sum of the first $(p+q)$ terms.

**Q2.** How many terms of the AP $-6, -\frac{11}{2}, -5, \ldots$ are needed to give a sum of $-25$?

---

## Section B: Digit and Number Problems

**Q3.** The digits of a three-digit number are in AP, and their sum is 15. The number obtained by reversing the digits is 594 less than the original number. Find the number.

---

## Section C: Application

**Q4.** 200 logs are stacked so that the bottom row has 20 logs, the next has 19, and so on, decreasing by 1 log per row. In how many rows are the logs placed, and how many logs are in the top row?

**Q5.** Find the sum of all three-digit numbers that leave a remainder of 2 when divided by 5.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** Working through the two sum equations gives $d = -2$ and, on substitution into $S_{p+q}$, the result $S_{p+q} = -(p+q)$.

**Q2.** $a = -6$, $d = \frac{1}{2}$. Solving $S_n = -25$ gives $n^2 - 25n + 100 = 0 \Rightarrow n = 5$ or $n = 20$ (both valid due to the terms crossing zero).

**Q3.** Let the digits be $a-d, a, a+d$. Sum $= 3a = 15 \Rightarrow a = 5$. Original $-$ Reversed $= -198d = 594 \Rightarrow d = -3$. Digits: $8, 5, 2$. Number $= 852$.

**Q4.** $a = 20$, $d = -1$, $S_n = 200$. $n^2 - 41n + 400 = 0 \Rightarrow n = 16$ or $25$; $n = 25$ is rejected (logs cannot go negative). **16 rows**, top row has $a_{16} = 5$ logs.

**Q5.** Numbers: $102, 107, \ldots, 997$. $a = 102$, $d = 5$, $n = 180$. $S_{180} = \frac{180}{2}(102 + 997) = 98910$.

</details>
