| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 90–100% | Excellent; nth term and sum formulas applied correctly; clear working |
| 75–89% | Good; minor calculation or sign errors |
| 60–74% | Satisfactory; understands concepts but needs more practice with multi-step problems |
| Below 60% | Needs remediation; review common difference, general term, and sum formula |
