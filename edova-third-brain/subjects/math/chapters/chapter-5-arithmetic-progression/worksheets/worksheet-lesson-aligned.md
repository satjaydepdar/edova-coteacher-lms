## Based on Lesson 1: Introduction to Sequences and APs (NCERT Sections 5.1–5.2)

**Q1.** Define an arithmetic progression and its common difference.

**Q2.** Which of the following are APs? State the common difference for those that are.

(i) $2, 7, 12, 17, \ldots$

(ii) $1, 4, 9, 16, \ldots$

(iii) $-5, -1, 3, 7, \ldots$

## Based on Lesson 2: $n$th Term of an AP (NCERT Section 5.3)

**Q3.** Find the 10th term of the AP $2, 7, 12, 17, \ldots$

**Q4.** Which term of the AP $3, 8, 13, 18, \ldots$ is 78?

## Based on Lesson 3: Sum of First $n$ Terms of an AP (NCERT Section 5.4)

**Q5.** Find the sum of the first 22 terms of the AP $8, 3, -2, \ldots$

**Q6.** Find the sum of the first 10 multiples of 5, using the formula $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a+l)$.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** An AP is a list of numbers where each term (after the first) is obtained by adding a fixed common difference $d$ to the preceding term.

**Q2.**
(i) Yes — $d = 5$.
(ii) No — differences are $3, 5, 7, \ldots$, not constant.
(iii) Yes — $d = 4$.

**Q3.** $a = 2$, $d = 5$. $a_{10} = 2 + 9(5) = 47$.

**Q4.** $3 + (n-1)5 = 78 \Rightarrow n = 16$.

**Q5.** $a = 8$, $d = -5$. $S_{22} = \frac{22}{2}[16 + 21(-5)] = -979$.

**Q6.** $a = 5$, $l = 50$, $n = 10$. $S_{10} = \frac{10}{2}(5 + 50) = 275$.

</details>
