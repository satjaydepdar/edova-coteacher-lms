## Section C: Combined Reasoning

**Q6.** The 3rd term of an AP is 16, and the 7th term exceeds the 5th term by 12. Find the AP.

**Q7.** If the sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $S_n = 3n^2 + 5n$, find its first term and common difference.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** $3 + (n-1)5 = 78 \Rightarrow n = 16$. It is the 16th term.

**Q2.** $5 + (n-1)6 = 301 \Rightarrow n - 1 = 49.33\ldots$, not a whole number. So 301 is **not** a term.

**Q3.** $a = 12$, $d = 3$, $l = 99$. $n = 30$.

**Q4.** $a = 8$, $d = -5$. $S_{22} = \frac{22}{2}[16 + 21(-5)] = -979$.

**Q5.** $a = 2$, $d = 2$, $n = 20$. $S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}[4 + 19(2)] = 420$.

**Q6.** $a + 2d = 16$; $(a+6d) - (a+4d) = 12 \Rightarrow d = 6 \Rightarrow a = 4$. AP: $4, 10, 16, 22, \ldots$

**Q7.** $a_1 = S_1 = 8$; $a_2 = S_2 - S_1 = 22 - 8 = 14$. So $a = 8$, $d = 6$.

</details>
