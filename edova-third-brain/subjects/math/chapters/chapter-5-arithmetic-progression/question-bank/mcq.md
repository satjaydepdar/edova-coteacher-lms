# Multiple Choice Questions

**Q1.** The first term and common difference of the AP: $5, 1, -3, -7, \ldots$ are:

(a) $5, 1$
(b) $5, -4$
(c) $1, -4$
(d) $-4, 5$

**Q2.** The 10th term of the AP $2, 7, 12, 17, \ldots$ is:

(a) 38
(b) 42
(c) 47
(d) 52

**Q3.** Which of the following is an AP?

(a) $1, 3, 6, 10, \ldots$
(b) $2, 4, 8, 16, \ldots$
(c) $7, 4, 1, -2, \ldots$
(d) $1, 4, 9, 16, \ldots$

**Q4.** Which term of the AP $3, 8, 13, 18, \ldots$ is 78?

(a) 14th
(b) 15th
(c) 16th
(d) 17th

**Q5.** The sum of the first 10 terms of the AP with $a = 5$, $d = 3$ is:

(a) 150
(b) 175
(c) 185
(d) 195

**Q6.** The sum of the first $n$ natural numbers is:

(a) $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$
(b) $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$
(c) $n^2$
(d) $\frac{n(n+2)}{2}$

**Q7.** If the $n$th term of an AP is $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$, this relation is used to find:

(a) the common difference from the sum
(b) a term from consecutive partial sums
(c) the first term only
(d) whether the AP is increasing

**Q8.** The 30th term of the AP $10, 7, 4, \ldots$ is:

(a) $-77$
(b) $-67$
(c) $77$
(d) $67$

**Q9.** If the sum of first $n$ terms of an AP is $S_n = 2n^2 + 3n$, its common difference is:

(a) 2
(b) 3
(c) 4
(d) 5

**Q10.** For an AP, the alternative sum formula using the last term $l$ is:

(a) $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$
(b) $S_n = n(a + l)$
(c) $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a - l)$
(d) $S_n = 2(a + l)$

---

## Answers

**A1.** (b) $5, -4$

**A2.** (c) 47

**A3.** (c) $7, 4, 1, -2, \ldots$ (constant difference $-3$)

**A4.** (c) 16th

**A5.** (c) 185

**A6.** (b) $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$

**A7.** (b) a term from consecutive partial sums

**A8.** (a) $-77$

**A9.** (c) 4 — since $a_2 - a_1 = (S_2 - S_1) - S_1$; here $a_1 = 5$, $a_2 = 8 + 6 - 5 = 9$, $d = 4$.

**A10.** (a) $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$
