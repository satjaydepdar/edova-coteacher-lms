# Short Answer Questions

**Q1.** Define an arithmetic progression.

**Q2.** Find the common difference of the AP: $15, 12, 9, 6, \ldots$

**Q3.** Find the 15th term of the AP: $7, 13, 19, 25, \ldots$

**Q4.** Is 301 a term of the AP: $5, 11, 17, 23, \ldots$? Justify your answer.

**Q5.** Find the sum of the first 10 multiples of 5.

**Q6.** Write the general form of an AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$.

**Q7.** Find the sum: $2 + 4 + 6 + \ldots + 40$.

**Q8.** Which term of the AP $21, 18, 15, \ldots$ is $-81$?

**Q9.** Find the 25th term of the AP whose $n$th term is $a_n = 7 - 3n$.

**Q10.** How is the $n$th term of an AP related to the sum of its first $n$ terms?

---

## Answers

**A1.** An arithmetic progression is a list of numbers in which each term (after the first) is obtained by adding a fixed number, called the common difference, to the preceding term.

**A2.** $d = 12 - 15 = -3$.

**A3.** $a = 7$, $d = 6$. $a_{15} = 7 + 14(6) = 91$.

**A4.** $a = 5$, $d = 6$. $5 + (n-1)6 = 301 \Rightarrow n - 1 = 49.33\ldots$, not a whole number, so 301 is **not** a term of this AP.

**A5.** $a = 5$, $d = 5$, $n = 10$. $S_{10} = \frac{10}{2}[10 + 9(5)] = 275$.

**A6.** $a,\ a + d,\ a + 2d,\ a + 3d,\ \ldots$

**A7.** $a = 2$, $d = 2$, $n = 20$. $S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}[4 + 19(2)] = 420$.

**A8.** $a = 21$, $d = -3$. $21 + (n-1)(-3) = -81 \Rightarrow (n-1) = 34 \Rightarrow n = 35$. It is the 35th term.

**A9.** $a_{25} = 7 - 3(25) = 7 - 75 = -68$.

**A10.** $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$, the difference between the sum of the first $n$ terms and the sum of the first $(n-1)$ terms.
