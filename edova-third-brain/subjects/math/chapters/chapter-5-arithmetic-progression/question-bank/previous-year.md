# Previous Year Questions

**Q1.** Find the 20th term of the AP: $3, 7, 11, 15, \ldots$ (CBSE 2023)

**Q2.** Which term of the AP $21, 18, 15, \ldots$ is $-81$? Also find the sum of terms between the 5th and the last term (the term $-81$). (CBSE 2022)

**Q3.** How many two-digit numbers are divisible by 3? (CBSE 2021)

**Q4.** The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $S_n = 3n^2 + 5n$. Find its first term and common difference. (CBSE 2020)

**Q5.** In an AP, the sum of the first 14 terms is 1050 and the first term is 10. Find the 20th term. (CBSE 2019)

---

## Answers

**A1.** $a = 3$, $d = 4$. $a_{20} = 3 + 19(4) = 79$.

**A2.** $a = 21$, $d = -3$. $21 + (n-1)(-3) = -81 \Rightarrow n = 35$. It is the 35th term.

**A3.** Two-digit multiples of 3: $12, 15, \ldots, 99$. $a = 12$, $d = 3$, $l = 99$. $n = 30$.

**A4.** $a_1 = S_1 = 3 + 5 = 8$. $S_2 = 12 + 10 = 22 \Rightarrow a_2 = 14$. First term $a = 8$, common difference $d = a_2 - a_1 = 6$.

**A5.** $S_{14} = \frac{14}{2}[2(10) + 13d] = 1050 \Rightarrow 7(20 + 13d) = 1050 \Rightarrow 20 + 13d = 150 \Rightarrow d = 10$. $a_{20} = 10 + 19(10) = 200$.
