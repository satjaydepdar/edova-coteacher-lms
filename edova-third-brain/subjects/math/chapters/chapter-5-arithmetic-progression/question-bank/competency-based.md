# Competency-Based Questions

**Q1.** A student saves ₹50 in the first month, and increases her monthly savings by a fixed amount each month. By the end of the 6th month, she has saved ₹525 in total. Find the fixed monthly increase.

**Q2.** Two APs have the same common difference. The first term of the first AP is 3 more than the first term of the second. What can you say about the difference between their $n$th terms, for any $n$?

**Q3.** Design a real-life situation (other than seating, savings, or stacking) that can be modelled as an AP. State its first term and common difference.

**Q4.** A runner increases her daily distance by a fixed amount each day, starting from 2 km on day 1. If she wants her total distance run over 10 days to be at least 65 km, what is the minimum daily increase needed?

---

## Answers

**A1.** $a = 50$, $S_6 = 525$. $S_6 = \frac{6}{2}[100 + 5d] = 525 \Rightarrow 3(100 + 5d) = 525 \Rightarrow 100 + 5d = 175 \Rightarrow d = 15$. The fixed increase is ₹15 per month.

**A2.** The difference between corresponding $n$th terms stays constant at 3 for every $n$, since $a_n^{(1)} - a_n^{(2)} = (a_1 + (n-1)d) - (a_2 + (n-1)d) = a_1 - a_2 = 3$.

**A3.** Example: A library adds 5 new books to a shelf every week, starting with 40 books. This forms an AP with $a = 40$, $d = 5$, where the $n$th term gives the number of books after $n$ weeks.

**A4.** $a = 2$, $n = 10$. $S_{10} = \frac{10}{2}[4 + 9d] \geq 65 \Rightarrow 5(4 + 9d) \geq 65 \Rightarrow 4 + 9d \geq 13 \Rightarrow d \geq 1$. The minimum daily increase is 1 km.
