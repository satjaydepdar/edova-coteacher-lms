# Long Answer Questions

**Q1.** Find the sum of the first 22 terms of the AP: $8, 3, -2, \ldots$

**Q2.** Which term of the AP $3, 8, 13, 18, \ldots$ is 78? Also find the sum of the first 16 terms.

**Q3.** The 3rd term of an AP is 16, and the 7th term exceeds the 5th term by 12. Find the AP.

**Q4.** Find the sum of the first 40 positive integers divisible by 6.

**Q5.** How many terms of the AP $9, 17, 25, \ldots$ must be taken to give a sum of 636?

**Q6.** If the sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $S_n = 3n^2 + 5n$, find its first term, common difference, and $n$th term.

**Q7.** The sum of the 4th and 8th terms of an AP is 24, and the sum of the 6th and 10th terms is 44. Find the AP.

**Q8.** Find the sum of all two-digit numbers divisible by 4.

---

## Answers

**A1.** $a = 8$, $d = -5$. $S_{22} = \frac{22}{2}[16 + 21(-5)] = 11(-89) = -979$.

**A2.** $3 + (n-1)5 = 78 \Rightarrow n = 16$. $S_{16} = \frac{16}{2}[6 + 15(5)] = 8(81) = 648$.

**A3.** $a + 2d = 16$. $(a + 6d) - (a + 4d) = 12 \Rightarrow 2d = 12 \Rightarrow d = 6$. Then $a + 12 = 16 \Rightarrow a = 4$. AP: $4, 10, 16, 22, 28, \ldots$

**A4.** Multiples of 6 up to the 40th: $a = 6$, $d = 6$, $n = 40$. $S_{40} = \frac{40}{2}[12 + 39(6)] = 20(246) = 4920$.

**A5.** $a = 9$, $d = 8$. $\frac{n}{2}[18 + (n-1)8] = 636 \Rightarrow 4n^2 + 5n - 636 = 0$. Solving with the quadratic formula gives $n = 12$ (the negative root is rejected).

**A6.** $a_1 = S_1 = 3 + 5 = 8$. $S_2 = 3(4) + 10 = 22 \Rightarrow a_2 = 14$. So $a = 8$, $d = a_2 - a_1 = 6$, and $a_n = a + (n-1)d = 6n + 2$.

**A7.** $a_4 + a_8 = 2a + 10d = 24$. $a_6 + a_{10} = 2a + 14d = 44$. Subtracting: $4d = 20 \Rightarrow d = 5$. Then $2a + 50 = 24 \Rightarrow a = -13$. AP: $-13, -8, -3, 2, 7, \ldots$

**A8.** Two-digit multiples of 4: $12, 16, \ldots, 96$. $a = 12$, $d = 4$, $l = 96$. $12 + (n-1)4 = 96 \Rightarrow n = 22$. $S_{22} = \frac{22}{2}(12 + 96) = 11(108) = 1188$.
