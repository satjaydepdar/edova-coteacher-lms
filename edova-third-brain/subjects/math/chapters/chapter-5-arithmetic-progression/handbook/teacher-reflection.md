# Teacher Reflection: Arithmetic Progressions

## After Each Lesson

- Could students identify an AP from a given list of numbers?
- Did students correctly find the common difference, including when it was negative or zero?
- Were students able to apply the $n$th term formula accurately?
- Did students understand the derivation of the sum formula?

## After the Chapter

- What percentage of students can find any term of an AP given sufficient information?
- What percentage can solve word problems involving APs?
- Which type of problem was most challenging — finding $n$, finding the sum, or word problems?
- Did students avoid common errors such as using $n$ instead of $(n - 1)$?

## Improvements for Next Year

- Where can I add more real-life examples (salary increments, penalties, savings)?
- Should I allocate more time to the derivation of the sum formula?
- What misconceptions arose, and how can I address them earlier?
