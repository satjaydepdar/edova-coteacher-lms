## Required Concepts from Previous Classes

### 1. Number Patterns and Sequences (Class IX)
- Recognising patterns in lists of numbers.
- Writing the next few terms of a pattern.

### 2. Basic Algebra
- Using variables to represent unknown quantities.
- Simplifying linear expressions such as $a + (n - 1)d$.
- Solving linear equations in one variable.

### 3. Pair of Linear Equations (Chapter 3)
- Forming and solving two linear equations to find two unknowns.
- Useful when the $m$th and $n$th terms of an AP are given and $a$ and $d$ must be found.

### 4. Substitution and Evaluation
- Substituting given values into a formula.
- Checking whether a calculated value satisfies the original condition.

### 5. Operations on Integers, Fractions, and Decimals
- Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing integers and fractions.
- Working with negative common differences.

## Quick Diagnostic Checks

1. Find the next two terms of the pattern: $5, 9, 13, 17, \ldots$
2. Solve for $x$: $2x + 5 = 17$.
3. If $a = 3$ and $d = 4$, find the value of $a + 5d$.
4. Solve the pair of equations: $a + 2d = 5$ and $a + 6d = 9$.
5. Find the sum $1 + 2 + 3 + \ldots + 10$.

## Common Gaps to Address

- Confusing the term number ($n$) with the value of the term ($a_n$).
- Forgetting that the common difference $d$ can be negative or zero.
- Misapplying the $n$th term formula by using $n$ instead of $(n - 1)$.
- Difficulty in translating word problems into the variables $a$, $d$, $n$, $a_n$, or $S_n$.
