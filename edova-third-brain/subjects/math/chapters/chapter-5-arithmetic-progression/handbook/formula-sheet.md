## General Form of an AP

$$a,\ a + d,\ a + 2d,\ a + 3d,\ \ldots$$

where $a$ is the first term and $d$ is the common difference.

## Common Difference

$$d = a_{k+1} - a_k$$

The value of $d$ must be the same for every pair of consecutive terms.

## $n$th Term (General Term)

$$a_n = a + (n - 1)d$$

where $a_n$ is the term in the $n$th position.

## Sum of First $n$ Terms

$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$$

or, when the last term $l$ is known:

$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$$

## Last Term of a Finite AP

$$l = a + (n - 1)d$$

## Relationship Between $n$th Term and Sum

$$a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$$

## Arithmetic Mean

If $a, b, c$ are in AP, then

$$b = \frac{a + c}{2}$$

## Sum of First $n$ Positive Integers

$$1 + 2 + 3 + \ldots + n = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}$$
