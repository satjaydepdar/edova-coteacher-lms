# Classroom Activities

## Activity 1: AP or Not?

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Provide cards with lists of numbers. Students classify each list as "AP" or "Not an AP" and, if it is an AP, identify the first term and common difference.

**Sample Cards:**
- $4, 10, 16, 22, \ldots$
- $1, -1, -3, -5, \ldots$
- $2, 4, 8, 16, \ldots$
- $0.6, 1.7, 2.8, 3.9, \ldots$
- $1, 1, 1, 1, \ldots$
- $-2, 2, -2, 2, \ldots$

---

## Activity 2: Build an AP with Strips

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4 | **Materials:** Strips of paper or number cards

Each group is given a first term and a common difference. They build the first ten terms of the AP using strips and display it on a number line or chart. Groups exchange values and verify each other's APs.

---

## Activity 3: Discover the Sum Formula

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Students write the AP $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 20$ forwards and backwards, add the columns, and observe that each pair sums to 21. They generalise the idea to derive $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(a + l)$.

---

## Activity 4: Real-Life AP Hunt

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams of 3

Students find examples of APs in daily life: taxi fares with fixed extra charges, stair steps, years of service with fixed increments, etc. Each team presents one example and writes its first four terms.
