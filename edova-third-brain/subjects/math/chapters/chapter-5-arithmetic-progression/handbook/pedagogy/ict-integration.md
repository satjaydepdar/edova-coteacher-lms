# ICT Integration

## Recommended Tools

1. **GeoGebra / Desmos**
   - Plot the terms of an AP as points on a graph.
   - Observe that the points lie on a straight line.
   - Change $a$ and $d$ dynamically and observe the effect.

2. **Spreadsheets (Excel / Google Sheets)**
   - Generate the first $n$ terms of an AP.
   - Calculate the sum of first $n$ terms using formulas.
   - Create charts showing term growth and cumulative sums.

3. **Kahoot / Quizizz / Google Forms**
   - Quick quizzes on identifying APs and finding terms.
   - True/false questions on common misconceptions.

## Suggested Activities

- **AP Generator:** Students enter $a$ and $d$ in a spreadsheet and generate the first 20 terms.
- **Sum Explorer:** Use a spreadsheet to verify the sum formula for different APs.
- **Graph Plotter:** Plot $a_n$ against $n$ and observe the linear relationship.
