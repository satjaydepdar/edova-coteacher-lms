# Experiential Learning

## Activity 1: Salary Increment Simulation

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Students role-play a job scenario with a starting salary and fixed annual increment. They create a salary table for 10 years and identify $a$, $d$, and $a_n$.

## Activity 2: Staircase AP

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Build a model staircase where each step rises by the same height. Measure the height of each step and write the AP formed. Relate it to $a_n = a + (n - 1)d$.

## Activity 3: Savings Jar

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Students decide on a starting saving amount and a fixed weekly increase. They fill a table showing savings each week and calculate total savings after a given number of weeks.

## Activity 4: Penalty Game

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams

Students simulate a delay penalty that increases by a fixed amount each day. They calculate the total penalty for 30 days using the sum formula.
