# Art Integration

## Activity 1: AP Pattern Poster

Students create a poster showing an AP using shapes or colours. Each shape represents a term, and the increase in size or number represents the common difference.

## Activity 2: Salary Growth Chart

Students draw a bar chart showing salary growth over 10 years with a fixed annual increment. They label $a$, $d$, and $a_{10}$.

## Activity 3: Sequence Collage

Create a collage showing examples of APs and non-APs from daily life. Label each example and explain why it is or is not an AP.

## Activity 4: Formula Bookmark

Students design bookmarks with the key AP formulas and decorate them with number patterns.
