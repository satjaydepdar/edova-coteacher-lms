# Inclusive Education

## For Students with Dyscalculia

- Use colour coding for $a$, $d$, $n$, $a_n$, and $S_n$ consistently.
- Provide formula cards with worked examples.
- Allow use of calculators for arithmetic operations after setting up the formula.
- Use manipulatives such as counters and number lines.

## For Students with Visual Impairments

- Provide tactile number lines and raised-line graphs.
- Use audio descriptions of sequences and patterns.
- Allow use of screen readers with accessible digital worksheets.

## For Students with Hearing Impairments

- Provide written instructions and visual cues.
- Use captioned videos if demonstrating spreadsheet or GeoGebra activities.
- Encourage peer note-sharing.

## For Slow Learners

- Break word problems into smaller steps: identify $a$, find $d$, choose the formula, substitute, solve.
- Focus first on positive common differences before introducing negative values.
- Provide additional practice with immediate feedback.

## For Gifted Students

- Offer enrichment tasks such as proving the sum formula.
- Explore the relationship between AP and linear functions.
- Assign project-based learning on real-life applications of APs.
