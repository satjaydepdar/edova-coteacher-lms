# Differentiation Strategies

## For Struggling Learners

- Use number lines and counters to visualise the common difference.
- Provide a formula card with $a_n$ and $S_n$ clearly stated.
- Start with simple APs having small positive common differences.
- Use step-by-step templates for word problems.

## For Average Learners

- Mixed practice identifying $a$, $d$, $n$, $a_n$, and $S_n$.
- Scaffolded problems with partially completed solutions.
- Pair work on NCERT Exercise 5.2 and 5.3 questions.

## For Advanced Learners

- Prove the sum formula independently.
- Explore problems where $n$ has two possible values.
- Investigate the relationship between arithmetic mean and AP.
- Solve Exercise 5.4 (optional) problems.

## Visual Learners

- Number lines and bar models for terms and sums.
- Colour-coded sequences showing the common difference.

## Kinesthetic Learners

- Build APs using strips or cards.
- Physical demonstration of pairing terms for the sum formula.

## Verbal Learners

- Explain the derivation of formulas to a partner.
- Write a "how-to" guide for solving AP word problems.
