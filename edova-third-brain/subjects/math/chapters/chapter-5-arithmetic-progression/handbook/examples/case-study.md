# Case Study Example

## Subba Rao's Salary Increment

Subba Rao started work in 1995 at an annual salary of ₹5000 and received an increment of ₹200 each year. In which year did his income reach ₹7000?

### Information

- Starting salary (1995) = ₹5000
- Annual increment = ₹200
- Target salary = ₹7000

### Questions

1. What is the first term and common difference of the AP formed by his salaries?
2. Which term of the AP is ₹7000?
3. In which year did his salary reach ₹7000?

### Solutions

**1. First term and common difference**

The salaries form the AP: $5000, 5200, 5400, \ldots$

$$a = 5000,\ d = 200$$

**2. Which term is ₹7000?**

Let the $n$th term be 7000.

$$a_n = a + (n - 1)d$$

$$7000 = 5000 + (n - 1)200$$

$$2000 = (n - 1)200$$

$$n - 1 = 10 \Rightarrow n = 11$$

So, ₹7000 is the 11th term.

**3. Year in which salary reached ₹7000**

The 1st term corresponds to 1995. Therefore, the 11th term corresponds to:

$$1995 + 11 - 1 = 2005$$

Subba Rao's salary reached ₹7000 in the year **2005**.

### Reflection

This case study shows how an AP models a real-life situation with a fixed annual increment. Similar problems can be solved for savings, penalties, and production.
