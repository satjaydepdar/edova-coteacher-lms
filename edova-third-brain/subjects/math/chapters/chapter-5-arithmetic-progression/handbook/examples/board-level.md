# Board Level Worked Examples

## Example 1: Find the Sum

Find the sum of the first 22 terms of the AP $8, 3, -2, \ldots$

**Solution:**

Here, $a = 8$, $d = 3 - 8 = -5$, $n = 22$.

$$S_{22} = \frac{22}{2}[2(8) + (22 - 1)(-5)]$$

$$S_{22} = 11[16 - 105]$$

$$S_{22} = 11(-89) = -979$$

The sum of the first 22 terms is $-979$.

## Example 2: Sum of First $n$ Positive Integers

Find the sum of the first $n$ positive integers.

**Solution:**

The first $n$ positive integers are $1, 2, 3, \ldots, n$.

Here, $a = 1$, $l = n$, and number of terms is $n$.

$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l) = \frac{n}{2}(1 + n) = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}$$

## Example 3: Cash Prizes

A sum of ₹700 is to be used to give seven cash prizes to students. If each prize is ₹20 less than its preceding prize, find the value of each prize.

**Solution:**

Let the first prize be ₹$a$. Then the prizes form an AP with $d = -20$ and $n = 7$.

$$S_7 = \frac{7}{2}[2a + (7 - 1)(-20)] = 700$$

$$\frac{7}{2}[2a - 120] = 700$$

$$7(a - 60) = 700$$

$$a - 60 = 100 \Rightarrow a = 160$$

The prizes are ₹160, ₹140, ₹120, ₹100, ₹80, ₹60, and ₹40.

## Example 4: Production Problem

A manufacturer of TV sets produced 600 sets in the third year and 700 sets in the seventh year. Assuming that the production increases uniformly by a fixed number every year, find:

(i) the production in the 1st year
(ii) the production in the 10th year
(iii) the total production in the first 7 years

**Solution:**

Let the production in the $n$th year be $a_n$. Then $a_3 = 600$ and $a_7 = 700$.

$$a + 2d = 600 \quad \text{...(1)}$$

$$a + 6d = 700 \quad \text{...(2)}$$

Subtracting (1) from (2): $4d = 100 \Rightarrow d = 25$.

From (1): $a = 600 - 50 = 550$.

(i) Production in the 1st year = **550 sets**.

(ii) Production in the 10th year:

$$a_{10} = 550 + 9 \times 25 = 550 + 225 = 775 \text{ sets}$$

(iii) Total production in the first 7 years:

$$S_7 = \frac{7}{2}[2(550) + 6(25)] = \frac{7}{2}[1100 + 150] = \frac{7}{2} \times 1250 = 4375 \text{ sets}$$
