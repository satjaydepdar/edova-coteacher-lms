# Moderate Worked Examples

## Example 4: Which Term is Given?

Which term of the AP $21, 18, 15, \ldots$ is $-81$? Is any term 0?

**Solution:**

Here, $a = 21$, $d = 18 - 21 = -3$, and $a_n = -81$.

$$a_n = a + (n - 1)d$$

$$-81 = 21 + (n - 1)(-3)$$

$$-81 = 21 - 3n + 3$$

$$-81 = 24 - 3n$$

$$3n = 105$$

$$n = 35$$

So, the 35th term is $-81$.

For $a_n = 0$:

$$0 = 21 + (n - 1)(-3)$$

$$0 = 24 - 3n$$

$$n = 8$$

So, the 8th term is $0$.

## Example 5: Determine the AP

Determine the AP whose 3rd term is 5 and the 7th term is 9.

**Solution:**

$$a_3 = a + 2d = 5 \quad \text{...(1)}$$

$$a_7 = a + 6d = 9 \quad \text{...(2)}$$

Subtracting (1) from (2):

$$4d = 4 \Rightarrow d = 1$$

Substituting in (1):

$$a + 2(1) = 5 \Rightarrow a = 3$$

The required AP is $3, 4, 5, 6, 7, \ldots$

## Example 6: Two-Digit Numbers Divisible by 3

How many two-digit numbers are divisible by 3?

**Solution:**

The list is $12, 15, 18, \ldots, 99$.

Here, $a = 12$, $d = 3$, and $a_n = 99$.

$$99 = 12 + (n - 1) \times 3$$

$$87 = (n - 1) \times 3$$

$$n - 1 = 29 \Rightarrow n = 30$$

There are **30** two-digit numbers divisible by 3.
