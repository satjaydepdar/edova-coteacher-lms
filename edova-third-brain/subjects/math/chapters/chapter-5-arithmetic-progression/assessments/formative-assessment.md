### Section B: Short Answer (2 × 5 = 10 marks)

**Q6.** Find the common difference of the AP: $15, 12, 9, 6, \ldots$

**Q7.** Find the 15th term of the AP: $7, 13, 19, 25, \ldots$

**Q8.** Is 301 a term of the AP: $5, 11, 17, 23, \ldots$? Justify.

**Q9.** Find the sum of the first 10 multiples of 5.

**Q10.** Find the sum: $2 + 4 + 6 + \ldots + 40$.

### Section C: Word Problem (5 × 1 = 5 marks)

**Q11.** A sum of ₹1000 is invested at a simple interest of ₹80 per year. Do the amounts at the end of each year form an AP? If so, find the amount at the end of the 8th year.

---

## Answer Key

**A1.** (b)
**A2.** (c)
**A3.** (b)
**A4.** (c)
**A5.** (c)

**A6.** $d = 12 - 15 = -3$.

**A7.** $a = 7$, $d = 6$. $a_{15} = 7 + 14(6) = 91$.

**A8.** $a = 5$, $d = 6$. $5 + (n-1)6 = 301 \Rightarrow (n-1) = 49.33\ldots$, not a whole number. So 301 is **not** a term of this AP.

**A9.** First 10 multiples of 5: $a = 5$, $d = 5$, $n = 10$. $S_{10} = \frac{10}{2}[10 + 9(5)] = 5(55) = 275$.

**A10.** $a = 2$, $d = 2$, $n = 20$. $S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}[4 + 19(2)] = 10(42) = 420$.

**A11.** Yes — the amounts at the end of each year increase by a fixed ₹80, so they form an AP with $a = 1080$ (end of year 1) and $d = 80$. Amount at the end of the 8th year $= 1080 + 7(80) = 1640$. Amount is ₹1640.
