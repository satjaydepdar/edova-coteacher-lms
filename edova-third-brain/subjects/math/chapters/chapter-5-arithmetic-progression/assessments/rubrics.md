# Assessment Rubrics

## Chapter 5: Arithmetic Progressions

---

## Rubric for Formula Derivations

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup** | Correctly writes the AP and states what is to be derived | Mostly correct setup | Partial setup | Missing |
| **Derivation Steps** | All steps clear and logically ordered (e.g., pairing method for $S_n$) | Most steps clear | Some jumps in reasoning | Reasoning unclear |
| **Formula Statement** | States final formula correctly with all variables defined | Minor omissions | Formula stated but variables unclear | Formula incorrect |
| **Application** | Applies the derived formula correctly to a worked example | Minor error | Some errors | Cannot apply formula |

---

## Rubric for Numerical Problems

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Identification** | Correctly identifies $a$, $d$, $n$, or $S_n$ as given/required | Minor error | Partial identification | Missing |
| **Formula Choice** | Selects the correct nth-term or sum formula | Appropriate but not most efficient | Some difficulty | Inappropriate |
| **Working Steps** | Clear and logical | Mostly clear | Some steps missing | Unclear |
| **Calculation** | Correct | Minor arithmetic error | One major error | Incorrect |
| **Final Answer** | Correct with correct units/context | Mostly correct | Incomplete | Missing |

---

## Rubric for Word and Case Study Problems

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Modelling** | Correctly identifies the real-life situation as an AP and states $a$, $d$ | Minor gaps | Partial modelling | Fails to model as AP |
| **Formula Application** | Applies nth term / sum formula correctly | Minor error | Some errors | Incorrect |
| **Interpretation** | Interprets the numerical answer correctly in context (e.g., rows, years, logs) | Mostly correct | Weak interpretation | Missing |
| **Validity Check** | Checks solution against real-world constraints (e.g., no negative terms) | Partial check | Not checked but answer correct | Answer invalid and unchecked |

---

## Overall Chapter Performance Levels

| Level | Marks Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced | 85–100% | Masters identifying APs, deriving and applying the nth term and sum formulas, and solving real-life applications |
| Proficient | 70–84% | Solves most problems correctly with minor errors |
| Developing | 50–69% | Understands basic concepts but struggles with derivations or multi-step problems |
| Beginning | Below 50% | Requires significant remediation on sequences and the general form of an AP |
