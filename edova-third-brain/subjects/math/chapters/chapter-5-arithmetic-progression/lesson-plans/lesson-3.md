# Lesson 3

## Chapter 5: Arithmetic Progressions
### Topic: Sum of First $n$ Terms of an AP (NCERT Section 5.4)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Derive the formula $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$.
- Use the alternative form $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$ when the last term is known.
- Find a term from consecutive partial sums using $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$.
- Solve real-life application problems involving the sum of an AP.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Sum formula (pairing method): $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$.
- Alternative form using the last term $l$: $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$.
- Relation between sum and term: $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Tell the story of young Gauss summing $1 + 2 + \ldots + 100$ instantly by pairing terms.
- **Recall:** Review the $n$th term formula from Lesson 2.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Derive:** Write $S_n$ forwards and backwards, add term by term to get $2S_n = n[2a + (n-1)d]$, and solve for $S_n$.
- **Practice — direct formula:** Find the sum of the first 22 terms of $8, 3, -2, \ldots$
- **Practice — last-term form:** Find the sum of the first 10 multiples of 5 using $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a+l)$.
- **Classic application:** Solve the log-stacking problem — 200 logs stacked from 20 down to 1 per row, decreasing by 1 each row; find the number of rows and logs in the top row.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise both forms of the sum formula and when to use each.
- Assign practice problems combining $n$th term and sum concepts.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the AP $2, 7, 12, \ldots$ Verify your answer using both forms of the sum formula.
