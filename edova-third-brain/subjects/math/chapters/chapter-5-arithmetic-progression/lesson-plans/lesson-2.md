# Lesson 2

## Chapter 5: Arithmetic Progressions
### Topic: $n$th Term of an AP (NCERT Section 5.3)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Derive and state the formula $a_n = a + (n-1)d$.
- Find any term of an AP given $a$, $d$, and $n$.
- Find $n$ given $a_n$ (i.e., determine which term a value corresponds to).
- Solve simple word problems using the $n$th term formula.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- $n$th term (general term) of an AP: $a_n = a + (n-1)d$.
- The formula can be used to find any one of $a$, $d$, $n$, or $a_n$ given the other three.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Ask, "What is the 50th term of the AP $3, 7, 11, \ldots$ — without listing all 50 terms?"
- **Recall:** Review the general form of an AP from Lesson 1.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Derive:** Build the formula by observing the pattern $a_1 = a$, $a_2 = a + d$, $a_3 = a + 2d$, ..., leading to $a_n = a + (n-1)d$.
- **Practice — Find a term:** Find $a_{10}$ for $2, 7, 12, 17, \ldots$
- **Practice — Find n:** Which term of $3, 8, 13, 18, \ldots$ is 78?
- **Word problem:** Use the taxi fare AP from Lesson 1 to find the fare for the 12th km.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the four uses of the $n$th term formula (find $a_n$, $n$, $a$, or $d$).
- Assign NCERT Exercise 5.2-style problems for homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Find the 15th term of the AP $5, 11, 17, \ldots$ Which term of the AP $3, 8, 13, \ldots$ is 78?
