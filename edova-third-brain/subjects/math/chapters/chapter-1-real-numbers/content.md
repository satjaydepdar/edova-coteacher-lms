# Real Numbers

Sample content used to test the OKF ingestion pipeline.

Euclid's Division Lemma: for positive integers a and b, there exist unique
integers q and r such that a = bq + r, where 0 <= r < b.

The Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic states every composite number can be
expressed as a product of primes, and this factorization is unique.
