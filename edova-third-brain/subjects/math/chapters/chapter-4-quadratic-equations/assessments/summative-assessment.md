## Section A: MCQs (1 × 5 = 5)

**Q1.** The equation $x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0$ has:

(a) distinct real roots
(b) equal real roots
(c) no real roots
(d) irrational roots

**Q2.** The standard form of $x(x + 3) = 10$ is:

(a) $x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$
(b) $x^2 + 3x + 10 = 0$
(c) $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$
(d) $x^2 - 10 = 0$

**Q3.** If the discriminant of a quadratic equation is negative, then:

(a) roots are real and distinct
(b) roots are real and equal
(c) roots are not real
(d) roots are rational

**Q4.** The value of $k$ for which $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$ has equal roots is:

(a) ±2
(b) ±2\sqrt{6}
(c) ±6
(d) ±12

**Q5.** The roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ are:

(a) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
(b) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (repeated)
(c) $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(d) no real roots

## Section C: Long Answer (3 × 5 = 15)

**Q11.** Find the roots of $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$ by factorisation.

**Q12.** A pole has to be erected on the boundary of a circular park of diameter 13 m such that the difference of its distances from two diametrically opposite gates A and B is 7 m. Is it possible to do so? If yes, find the distances from the two gates.

**Q13.** The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. Find the integers.

**Q14.** A train travels 480 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 8 km/h less, it would have taken 3 hours more. Find the speed of the train.

**Q15.** Find the value of $k$ for which $kx(x - 2) + 6 = 0$ has equal roots.

## Section D: Case Study (5 × 2 = 10)

### Case Study 1: Prayer Hall

A charity trust decides to build a prayer hall of carpet area 300 m². The length is one metre more than twice the breadth.

**Q16.** Form a quadratic equation for the dimensions of the hall.

**Q17.** Find the length and breadth of the hall.

### Case Study 2: Rectangular Mango Grove

Is it possible to design a rectangular mango grove whose length is twice its breadth and area is 800 m²?

**Q18.** Form a quadratic equation for the dimensions.

**Q19.** Find the length and breadth, or explain why it is not possible.
