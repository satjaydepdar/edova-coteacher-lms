**Q1.** Which of the following is a quadratic equation?

(a) $x + 5 = 0$
(b) $x^2 - 4 = 0$
(c) $x^3 + 2x = 0$
(d) $\sqrt{x} + 3 = 0$

**Q2.** The standard form of $x(x + 3) = 10$ is:

(a) $x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$
(b) $x^2 + 3x + 10 = 0$
(c) $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$
(d) $x^2 - 10 = 0$

**Q3.** The roots of $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$ are:

(a) 2 and 5
(b) -2 and -5
(c) 5 and -2
(d) -5 and 2

**Q4.** The discriminant of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ is:

(a) 8
(b) -8
(c) 16
(d) 28

**Q5.** If $x^2 - kx + 9 = 0$ has equal roots, then $k$ =

(a) 3
(b) ±3
(c) 6
(d) ±6

**Q6.** Solve by factorisation: $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$

(a) 1 and $\dfrac{3}{2}$
(b) -1 and $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
(c) 1 and $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
(d) -1 and $\dfrac{3}{2}$

**Q7.** The quadratic formula is:

(a) $x = \dfrac{b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
(b) $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
(c) $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 + 4ac}}{2a}$
(d) $x = \dfrac{b \pm \sqrt{b^2 + 4ac}}{2a}$

**Q8.** If $D < 0$ for a quadratic equation, the roots are:

(a) real and distinct
(b) real and equal
(c) not real
(d) rational

**Q9.** The equation $x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0$ has:

(a) distinct real roots
(b) equal real roots
(c) no real roots
(d) irrational roots

**Q10.** Which of the following is NOT a quadratic equation?

(a) $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
(b) $x^2 + 3x + 1 = (x - 2)^2$
(c) $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$
(d) $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$
