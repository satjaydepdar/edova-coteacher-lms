# Assessment Rubrics

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

---

## Rubric for Solving Quadratic Equations

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard Form** | Correctly writes equation in $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ with correct $a, b, c$ | Minor sign error in coefficients | Some errors in identifying coefficients | Unable to identify standard form |
| **Simplification** | Simplifies correctly before deciding if quadratic | Minor simplification error | Some simplification errors | Does not simplify |
| **Method Selection** | Chooses appropriate method (factorisation or formula) | Method chosen works but may not be most efficient | Method chosen with some difficulty | Method inappropriate |
| **Working Steps** | All steps clear and logical | Most steps clear | Some steps missing or unclear | Working unclear or missing |
| **Final Answer** | Correct roots, simplified | Correct with minor simplification error | One root correct or arithmetic error | Incorrect answer |
| **Interpretation** | Correctly interprets roots in context | Minor interpretation issue | Some interpretation | No interpretation |

---

## Rubric for Word Problems

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Variable Definition** | Clear and correct | Mostly clear | Unclear | Missing |
| **Equation Formation** | Correct quadratic equation | Minor error | Significant error | No equation formed |
| **Solution** | Correctly solved | Minor error | Partially correct | Incorrect |
| **Feasibility Check** | Rejects invalid roots with reason | Checks feasibility | Partial check | No check |
| **Final Statement** | Clear answer with units | Mostly clear | Incomplete | Missing |
