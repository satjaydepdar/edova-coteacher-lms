### Section A: MCQs (1 × 5 = 5 marks)

**Q1.** Which of the following is NOT a quadratic equation?

(a) $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
(b) $x^2 + 3x + 1 = (x - 2)^2$
(c) $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$
(d) $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$

**Q2.** The discriminant of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ is:

(a) 8
(b) -8
(c) 16
(d) 28

**Q3.** The roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ are:

(a) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
(b) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (repeated)
(c) $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(d) no real roots

**Q4.** If $x^2 - kx + 9 = 0$ has equal roots, then $k$ =

(a) 3
(b) ±3
(c) 6
(d) ±6

**Q5.** Which equation has no real roots?

(a) $x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0$
(b) $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$
(c) $3x^2 - 2x + \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$
(d) $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$

### Section B: Short Answer (2 × 5 = 10 marks)

**Q6.** Check whether $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$ is a quadratic equation.

**Q7.** Solve: $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$ by factorisation.

**Q8.** Find the discriminant and nature of roots of $3x^2 - 2x + \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$.

**Q9.** Find the roots of $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$ by factorisation.

**Q10.** Find $k$ if $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$ has equal roots.

### Section C: Word Problem (5 × 1 = 5 marks)

**Q11.** The area of a rectangular plot is 528 m². The length of the plot is one more than twice its breadth. Find the length and breadth of the plot.

---

## Answer Key

**A1.** (c)
**A2.** (b)
**A3.** (b)
**A4.** (d)
**A5.** (b)

**A6.** Expanding: $x^3 + 6x^2 + 12x + 8 = x^3 - 4$, so $6x^2 + 12x + 12 = 0$ or $x^2 + 2x + 2 = 0$. Yes, quadratic.

**A7.** $(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = 5$ or $x = -2$.

**A8.** $D = (-2)^2 - 4(3)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 4 - 4 = 0$. Equal roots.

**A9.** $(2x - 3)(x - 1) = 0$; $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ or $x = 1$.

**A10.** $k^2 - 24 = 0$; $k = \pm 2\sqrt{6}$.

**A11.** Let breadth = $x$, length = $2x + 1$.

$2x^2 + x - 528 = 0$
$(x - 16)(2x + 33) = 0$

Breadth = 16 m, Length = 33 m.
