# Lesson 1

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations
### Topic: Introduction and Quadratic Equations (NCERT Sections 4.1 and 4.2)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Define a quadratic equation in standard form.
- Identify whether a given equation is quadratic after simplification.
- Represent real-life situations as quadratic equations.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Standard form: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$.
- Coefficients: $a$, $b$, $c$.
- Simplifying equations before checking if they are quadratic.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Prayer hall problem from Section 4.1 — area 300 m², length one more than twice breadth.
- **Recall:** Review quadratic polynomials from Chapter 2.
- **Define:** Introduce quadratic equation using standard form.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Example 1:** Prayer hall — derive $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$.
- **Example 1 (ii):** Toys production problem — derive $x^2 - 55x + 750 = 0$.
- **Example 2:** Check whether given equations are quadratic (all four parts).
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 4.1 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the steps to identify a quadratic equation.
- Emphasise the need to simplify first.
- Assign remaining questions from NCERT Exercise 4.1 as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Check whether $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$ is a quadratic equation.
