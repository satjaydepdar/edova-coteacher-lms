# Lesson 2

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations
### Topic: Solution by Factorisation (NCERT Section 4.3)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Define a root of a quadratic equation.
- Solve quadratic equations by factorisation.
- Apply factorisation to solve word problems.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Root of a quadratic equation.
- Zeroes of a quadratic polynomial are the roots of the corresponding quadratic equation.
- Factorisation by splitting the middle term.
- Zero product property.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** What does it mean for a number to be a root of an equation?
- **Recall:** Review factorisation of quadratic polynomials from Chapter 2.
- **Connect:** Zeroes of polynomial = roots of equation.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Example 3:** Solve $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$ by factorisation.
- **Example 4:** Solve $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$.
- **Example 5:** Solve $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ (repeated root).
- **Example 6:** Prayer hall dimensions — solve $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$.
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 4.2 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the factorisation method.
- Address common errors (sign mistakes, not setting each factor to zero).
- Assign remaining questions from NCERT Exercise 4.2 as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Solve $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$ by factorisation.
