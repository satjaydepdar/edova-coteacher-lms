## Section A: Solve by Factorisation (NCERT Exercise 4.2)

**Q1.** Find the roots of:

(i) $\sqrt{2}x^2 + 7x + 5\sqrt{2} = 0$

(iv) $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$

## Section C: Nature of Roots

**Q3.** Find the values of $k$ for each of the following quadratic equations, so that they have two equal roots:

(i) $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$
(ii) $kx(x - 2) + 6 = 0$

**Q4.** Find the discriminant of $3x^2 - 2x + \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$ and hence find the nature of its roots.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.**
(i) $(\sqrt{2}x + 5)(x + \sqrt{2}) = 0$; $x = -\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{2}}$ or $x = -\sqrt{2}$
(ii) $(4x - 1)^2 = 0$; $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$ (repeated)
(iii) $(10x - 1)^2 = 0$; $x = \dfrac{1}{10}$ (repeated)
(iv) $(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = 5$ or $x = -2$

**Q2.**
(i) $x = \dfrac{7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 24}}{4} = \dfrac{7 \pm 5}{4}$; $x = 3$ or $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
(ii) $x = \dfrac{2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 32}}{2} = \dfrac{2 \pm 6}{2}$; $x = 4$ or $x = -2$
(iii) $x = \dfrac{5 \pm 1}{4}$; $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ or $x = 1$

**Q3.**
(i) $k^2 - 24 = 0$; $k = \pm 2\sqrt{6}$
(ii) $kx^2 - 2kx + 6 = 0$; $4k^2 - 24k = 0$; $k(k - 6) = 0$; $k = 0$ or $k = 6$

But $k = 0$ makes it linear, so $k = 6$.

**Q4.** $D = (-2)^2 - 4(3)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 4 - 4 = 0$; equal roots.
