## Based on Lesson 1: Introduction and Quadratic Equations (Sections 4.1 and 4.2)

**Q1.** Check whether the following are quadratic equations:

(i) $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
(ii) $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$
(iii) $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$
(iv) $x^3 - 4x^2 - x + 1 = (x - 2)^3$

**Q2.** Represent as a quadratic equation: The area of a rectangular plot is 528 m². The length is one more than twice the breadth.

(iii) $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$
(iv) $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$

**Q4.** Find the dimensions of the prayer hall whose area is 300 m² and length is one metre more than twice its breadth.

## Based on Lesson 3: Nature of Roots (Section 4.4)

**Q5.** Determine the nature of roots:

(i) $2x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0$
(ii) $3x^2 - 4\sqrt{3}x + 4 = 0$
(iii) $2x^2 - 6x + 3 = 0$

**Q6.** Find $k$ if $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$ has equal roots.

**Q7.** Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m²? If so, find its dimensions.

**Q1.**

**Q2.** Let breadth = $x$; length = $2x + 1$.

$2x^2 + x - 528 = 0$

**Q3.**
(i) $(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = 5$ or $x = -2$
(ii) $(2x - 3)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ or $x = -2$
(iii) $(3x - 2)(2x + 1) = 0$; $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$ or $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
(iv) $(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2})^2 = 0$; $x = \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (repeated)

**Q4.** $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$; $(x - 12)(2x + 25) = 0$.

Breadth = 12 m, Length = 25 m.

**Q5.**
(i) $D = -31$; no real roots
(ii) $D = 0$; equal roots
(iii) $D = 12$; two distinct real roots

**Q6.** $k^2 - 24 = 0$; $k = \pm 2\sqrt{6}$

**Q7.** Let length = $x$, breadth = $40 - x$.

$x(40 - x) = 400$
$(x - 20)^2 = 0$

Length = 20 m, Breadth = 20 m. Yes, possible.
