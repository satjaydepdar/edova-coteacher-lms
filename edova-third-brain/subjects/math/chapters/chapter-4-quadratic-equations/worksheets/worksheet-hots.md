## Section A: NCERT Exercise 4.2 Word Problems

**Q1.** Find two numbers whose sum is 27 and product is 182.

**Q2.** Find two consecutive positive integers, sum of whose squares is 365.

**Q3.** The altitude of a right triangle is 7 cm less than its base. If the hypotenuse is 13 cm, find the other two sides.

**Q4.** A cottage industry produces a certain number of pottery articles in a day. It was observed that the cost of production of each article (in rupees) was 3 more than twice the number of articles produced on that day. If the total cost of production on that day was ₹90, find the number of articles produced and the cost of each article.

## Section B: NCERT Exercise 4.3 Application Problems

**Q5.** Is it possible to design a rectangular mango grove whose length is twice its breadth, and the area is 800 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

**Q6.** Is the following situation possible? If so, determine their present ages. The sum of the ages of two friends is 20 years. Four years ago, the product of their ages in years was 48.

**Q7.** Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

---

<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** Let numbers be $x$ and $27 - x$.

$x(27 - x) = 182$
$x^2 - 27x + 182 = 0$
$(x - 13)(x - 14) = 0$

Numbers are 13 and 14.

**Q2.** Let integers be $x$ and $x + 1$.

$x^2 + (x + 1)^2 = 365$
$2x^2 + 2x - 364 = 0$
$x^2 + x - 182 = 0$
$(x + 14)(x - 13) = 0$

Integers are 13 and 14.

**Q3.** Let base = $x$ cm, altitude = $(x - 7)$ cm.

$x^2 + (x - 7)^2 = 169$
$2x^2 - 14x - 120 = 0$
$x^2 - 7x - 60 = 0$
$(x - 12)(x + 5) = 0$

Base = 12 cm, Altitude = 5 cm.

**Q4.** Let number of articles = $x$.

Cost per article = $(2x + 3)$ rupees.

$x(2x + 3) = 90$
$2x^2 + 3x - 90 = 0$
$(2x + 15)(x - 6) = 0$

$x = 6$.

Number of articles = 6, Cost per article = ₹15.

**Q5.** Let breadth = $x$, length = $2x$.

$2x^2 = 800$
$x^2 = 400$
$x = 20$

Breadth = 20 m, Length = 40 m. Yes, possible.

**Q6.** Let ages be $x$ and $20 - x$.

$(x - 4)(16 - x) = 48$
$-x^2 + 20x - 64 = 48$
$x^2 - 20x + 112 = 0$

$D = 400 - 448 = -48 < 0$

No real roots. Not possible.

**Q7.** Let length = $l$, breadth = $b$.

$l + b = 40$, $lb = 400$

Let $l = x$, $b = 40 - x$.

$x(40 - x) = 400$
$x^2 - 40x + 400 = 0$
$(x - 20)^2 = 0$

Length = 20 m, Breadth = 20 m. Yes, possible (a square).
