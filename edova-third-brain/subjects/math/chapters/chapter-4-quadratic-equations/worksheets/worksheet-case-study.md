## Case Study 1: John and Jivanti's Marbles (NCERT Example 1)

John and Jivanti together have 45 marbles. Both of them lost 5 marbles each, and the product of the number of marbles they now have is 124.

**Q1.** If John had $x$ marbles, express the number of marbles Jivanti had in terms of $x$.

**Q2.** Form a quadratic equation for this situation.

**Q3.** Find how many marbles each had originally.

---

## Case Study 2: Toys Production (NCERT Example 1)

A cottage industry produces a certain number of toys in a day. The cost of production of each toy (in rupees) was found to be 55 minus the number of toys produced in a day. On a particular day, the total cost of production was ₹750.

**Q4.** If the number of toys produced is $x$, express the cost of production of each toy.

**Q5.** Form a quadratic equation.

**Q6.** Find the number of toys produced on that day.

---

## Case Study 3: Pole in a Circular Park (NCERT Example 8)

A pole has to be erected at a point on the boundary of a circular park of diameter 13 m. The difference of its distances from two diametrically opposite fixed gates A and B is 7 m.

**Q7.** If the distance from gate B is $x$ m, express the distance from gate A.

**Q8.** Form a quadratic equation using Pythagoras theorem.

**Q9.** Find the distances from the two gates.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Case Study 1**

**A1.** Jivanti had $(45 - x)$ marbles.

**A2.** $(x - 5)(40 - x) = 124$; $x^2 - 45x + 324 = 0$

**A3.** $(x - 9)(x - 36) = 0$; John and Jivanti had 9 and 36 marbles (in either order).

**Case Study 2**

**A4.** Cost per toy = $(55 - x)$ rupees.

**A5.** $x(55 - x) = 750$; $x^2 - 55x + 750 = 0$

**A6.** $(x - 25)(x - 30) = 0$; 25 or 30 toys.

**Case Study 3**

**A7.** Distance from gate A = $(x + 7)$ m.

**A8.** $(x + 7)^2 + x^2 = 13^2$; $x^2 + 7x - 60 = 0$

**A9.** Discriminant = 289 > 0, so possible. $x = 5$ or $x = -12$. Since distance is positive, $x = 5$.

Pole is 5 m from gate B and 12 m from gate A.
