## Section A: Identify and Standard Form (Based on Exercise 4.1)

**Q1.** Check whether the following are quadratic equations:

(i) $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
(ii) $x^2 - 2x = (-2)(3 - x)$
(iii) $(x - 2)(x + 1) = (x - 1)(x + 3)$
(iv) $(x - 3)(2x + 1) = x(x + 5)$
(v) $(2x - 1)(x - 3) = (x + 5)(x - 1)$
(vi) $x^2 + 3x + 1 = (x - 2)^2$

**Q2.** Write the following situations as quadratic equations:

(i) The area of a rectangular plot is 528 m². The length of the plot is one more than twice its breadth.
(ii) The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306.

## Section C: Nature of Roots (Based on Exercise 4.3)

**Q4.** Find the nature of the roots of the following quadratic equations:

(i) $2x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0$
(ii) $3x^2 - 4\sqrt{3}x + 4 = 0$
(iii) $2x^2 - 6x + 3 = 0$

**Q1.**
(i) Quadratic: $x^2 + 7 = 0$
(ii) Quadratic: $x^2 - 4x + 6 = 0$
(iii) Not quadratic: simplifies to $-3x + 1 = 0$
(iv) Quadratic: $x^2 - 10x - 3 = 0$
(v) Quadratic: $x^2 - 11x + 8 = 0$
(vi) Not quadratic: simplifies to $7x - 3 = 0$ (degree 1)

**Q2.**
(i) Let breadth = $x$; length = $2x + 1$; equation: $2x^2 + x - 528 = 0$
(ii) Let integers be $x$ and $x + 1$; equation: $x^2 + x - 306 = 0$

**Q3.**
(i) $(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = 5$ or $x = -2$
(ii) $(2x - 3)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ or $x = -2$
(iii) $(10x - 1)^2 = 0$; $x = \dfrac{1}{10}$ (repeated)
(iv) Multiply by 8: $16x^2 - 8x + 1 = 0$; $(4x - 1)^2 = 0$; $x = \dfrac{1}{4}$ (repeated)

**Q4.**
(i) $D = 9 - 40 = -31 < 0$; no real roots
(ii) $D = 48 - 48 = 0$; equal real roots
(iii) $D = 36 - 24 = 12 > 0$; two distinct real roots
