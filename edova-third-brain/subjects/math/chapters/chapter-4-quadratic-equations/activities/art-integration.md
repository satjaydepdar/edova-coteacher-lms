# Art Integration

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

---

## Activity 1: Prayer Hall Poster

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual or pairs

Students create a poster of the prayer hall problem showing:
- The rectangle with labels
- The equation $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$
- The factorisation steps
- Final dimensions

---

## Activity 2: Formula Poster

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual

Design a colourful A3 poster of the quadratic formula with:
- The formula clearly displayed
- Labels for $a$, $b$, $c$
- A worked example from the textbook
- The three cases of the discriminant

---

## Activity 3: Discriminant Colour Wheel

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual

Create a colour wheel with sections for each discriminant case:
- Green: $D > 0$ → two distinct real roots
- Blue: $D = 0$ → two equal real roots
- Red: $D < 0$ → no real roots

Add one example equation from the textbook in each section.

---

## Activity 4: Comic Strip Problem Solving

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Create a 4–6 panel comic strip that tells the story of one NCERT word problem (marbles, toys, train, or park). Include:
- The problem statement
- Formulating the equation
- Solving it
- Interpreting the answer

---

## Activity 5: Geometric Representation

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Draw a diagram for the pole-in-circular-park problem. Label the diameter, the right angle, and the distances from the pole to each gate.
