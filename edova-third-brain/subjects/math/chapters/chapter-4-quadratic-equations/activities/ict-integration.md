# ICT Integration

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

---

## Tool 1: GeoGebra / Desmos

**Activity:** Plot parabolas corresponding to quadratic equations from the textbook and observe:
- Direction of opening
- Number of x-intercepts
- Vertex position

**Task:** Students match the graph to the nature of roots for equations from Exercise 4.3.

---

## Tool 2: Interactive Quizzes

**Platforms:** Kahoot, Quizizz, Google Forms

**Quiz Topics:**
- Identifying quadratic equations (Exercise 4.1)
- Finding discriminants (Exercise 4.3)
- Matching equations to their nature of roots
- Solving by factorisation (Exercise 4.2)

---

## Tool 3: Spreadsheets

**Activity:** Use Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets to:
- Create a table of values for $y = 2x^2 + x - 300$
- Plot the graph
- Estimate the root from the graph
- Verify algebraically that $x = 12$

---

## Tool 4: NCERT Digital Resources

**Suggested Resources:**
- NCERT exemplar problem solutions
- CBSE sample paper solutions

**Task:** Students watch a tutorial on a textbook example and summarise the method.

---

## Tool 5: Online Equation Solver

**Activity:** Use an online quadratic equation solver to verify answers after solving manually. Emphasise that the solver is for checking, not replacing the learning process.
