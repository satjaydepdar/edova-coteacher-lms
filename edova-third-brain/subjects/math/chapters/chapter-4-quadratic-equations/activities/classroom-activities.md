# Classroom Activities

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

---

## Activity 1: Quadratic Equation Sort

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Prepare cards with equations from NCERT Exercise 4.1. Students sort them into "Quadratic" and "Not Quadratic" and justify each decision.

**Sample Cards:**
- $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
- $x^2 - 2x = (-2)(3 - x)$
- $(x - 2)(x + 1) = (x - 1)(x + 3)$
- $(x - 3)(2x + 1) = x(x + 5)$
- $(2x - 1)(x - 3) = (x + 5)(x - 1)$
- $x^2 + 3x + 1 = (x - 2)^2$
- $(x + 2)^3 = 2x(x^2 - 1)$
- $x^3 - 4x^2 - x + 1 = (x - 2)^3$

## Activity 2: Factorisation Race

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams of 4

Each team receives equations from NCERT Exercise 4.2. The team that solves all correctly first wins.

**Equations:**
1. $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$
2. $2x^2 + x - 6 = 0$
3. $\sqrt{2}x^2 + 7x + 5\sqrt{2} = 0$
4. $2x^2 - x + \dfrac{1}{8} = 0$
5. $100x^2 - 20x + 1 = 0$

---

## Activity 3: Discriminant Quick-Fire

**Time:** 10 minutes | **Group Size:** Whole class

The teacher writes equations from NCERT Exercise 4.3 on the board. Students hold up cards:
- Green: two distinct real roots
- Blue: equal roots
- Red: no real roots

---

## Activity 4: Real-Life Problem Stations

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Stations with NCERT word problems. Groups rotate every 6 minutes.

**Station 1:** Rectangular plot area 528 m², length one more than twice breadth (Exercise 4.1).

**Station 2:** Product of two consecutive positive integers is 306 (Exercise 4.1).

**Station 3:** Rohan's mother is 26 years older; product of ages 3 years from now is 360 (Exercise 4.1).

**Station 4:** Train travels 480 km; 8 km/h less speed takes 3 hours more (Exercise 4.1).

**Station 5:** Right triangle altitude 7 cm less than base, hypotenuse 13 cm (Exercise 4.2).

**Station 6:** Rectangular mango grove length twice breadth, area 800 m² (Exercise 4.3).
