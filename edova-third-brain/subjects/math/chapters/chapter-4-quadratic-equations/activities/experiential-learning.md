# Experiential Learning

## Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

---

## Activity 1: Model the Prayer Hall

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs | **Materials:** Grid paper, ruler

Students draw a rectangle representing the prayer hall with area 300 square units. They label breadth as $x$ and length as $2x + 1$, then discover that $x = 12$ satisfies the equation.

---

## Activity 2: Number Product Hunt

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Find two positive numbers whose sum is 27 and product is 182. Explore different pairs and connect to factorisation of $x^2 - 27x + 182 = 0$.

---

## Activity 3: Marble Loss Simulation

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs | **Materials:** Counters or marbles

Two students start with a total of 45 counters. Each removes 5 counters. They check whether the product of their remaining counters can be 124, and find the possible starting numbers.

---

## Activity 4: Train Speed Discussion

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Whole class

Discuss the train problem from Exercise 4.1: How does reducing speed affect travel time? Students estimate the speed before solving algebraically.

---

## Activity 5: Design a Park

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Students design a rectangular park with given perimeter and area constraints (Exercise 4.3, Q5). They determine whether the design is possible and find dimensions.
