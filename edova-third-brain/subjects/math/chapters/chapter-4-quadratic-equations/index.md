# Chapter 4 — Quadratic Equations

## Overview

Quadratic Equations introduces equations of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$. The chapter covers identifying quadratic equations, solving them by factorisation, using the quadratic formula, and determining the nature of roots through the discriminant. Real-life situations such as finding the dimensions of a prayer hall, the number of toys produced, and the position of a pole in a park are used throughout.
