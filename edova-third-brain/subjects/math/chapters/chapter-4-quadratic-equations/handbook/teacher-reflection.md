# Teacher Reflection: Quadratic Equations

## After Each Lesson

- Did students correctly identify $a$, $b$, and $c$ including signs?
- Could students simplify equations before deciding if they are quadratic?
- Which factorisation cases caused the most difficulty?
- Were students able to connect the discriminant to the nature of roots?

## After the Chapter

- What percentage of students can solve a quadratic equation by factorisation?
- What percentage can use the quadratic formula correctly?
- Which word-problem type caused the most difficulty?
- Did students see the relevance of quadratic equations to real-life situations?

## Improvements for Next Year

- Where can I add more visual or hands-on activities?
- Should I allocate more time to factorisation or word problems?
- What misconceptions arose, and how can I address them earlier?
