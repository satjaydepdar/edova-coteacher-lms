## Knowledge (Remember)

- Recall the standard form of a quadratic equation: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$.
- State the quadratic formula.
- State the condition for the nature of roots using the discriminant.

## Understanding (Comprehend)

- Explain why the coefficient of $x^2$ must be non-zero.
- Describe the relationship between the zeroes of a quadratic polynomial and the roots of a quadratic equation.
- Interpret the discriminant $b^2 - 4ac$ to determine the nature of roots.

## Application (Apply)

- Identify and write equations in standard form.
- Solve quadratic equations by factorisation.
- Solve quadratic equations using the quadratic formula.
- Apply quadratic equations to solve real-life word problems.

## Analysis (Analyse)

- Determine whether a given equation is quadratic after simplification.
- Analyse word problems to formulate correct quadratic equations.
- Determine conditions on coefficients for equal, distinct real, or no real roots.

## Evaluation (Evaluate)

- Judge whether a given value is a root of a quadratic equation.
- Evaluate the correctness of a factorisation method.
- Verify whether a formulated equation accurately represents a given situation.

## Creation (Create)

- Design real-world problems that can be modelled using quadratic equations.
- Construct quadratic equations with specified roots or nature of roots.
