## Required Concepts from Previous Classes

### 1. Polynomials (Chapter 2)
- Definition of a polynomial in one variable.
- Degree of a polynomial.
- Quadratic polynomial of the form $ax^2 + bx + c$.
- Zeroes of a polynomial and their relation to factors.

### 2. Factorisation
- Factorising quadratic polynomials by splitting the middle term.
- Standard identities:
  - $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
  - $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$
  - $a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$

### 3. Linear Equations
- Solving linear equations in one variable.
- Formulating linear equations from word problems.

### 4. Real Numbers
- Operations on real numbers.
- Simplifying square roots.

### 5. Algebraic Manipulations
- Expanding brackets.
- Collecting like terms.
- Rearranging equations into standard form.

## Quick Diagnostic Checks

1. Factorise: $x^2 - 5x + 6$.
2. Solve: $2x - 7 = 11$.
3. Expand: $(x - 2)^2$.
4. Simplify: $\sqrt{72}$.
5. Find the zeroes of $x^2 - x - 6$.

## Common Gaps to Address

- Students often confuse the zero of a polynomial with the coefficient.
- Weakness in splitting the middle term can hinder the factorisation method.
- Difficulty in identifying $a$, $b$, $c$ with correct signs when using the quadratic formula.
