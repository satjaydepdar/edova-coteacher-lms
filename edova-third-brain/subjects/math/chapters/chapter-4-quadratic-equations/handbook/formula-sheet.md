## Standard Form

$$ax^2 + bx + c = 0, \quad a \neq 0$$

## Quadratic Formula

$$x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}, \quad \text{where } b^2 - 4ac \geq 0$$

## Discriminant

$$D = b^2 - 4ac$$

| Condition | Nature of Roots |
|---|---|
| $D > 0$ | Two distinct real roots |
| $D = 0$ | Two equal real roots |
| $D < 0$ | No real roots |

## Useful Identities

- $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
- $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$
- $a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$
