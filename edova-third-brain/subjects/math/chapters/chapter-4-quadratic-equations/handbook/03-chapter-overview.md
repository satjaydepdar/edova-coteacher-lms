# Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

## What Students Will Learn

This chapter builds on students' understanding of polynomials and linear equations to introduce equations of degree two. Students will:

1. Identify and formulate quadratic equations in standard form.
2. Check whether a given equation is quadratic after simplification.
3. Solve quadratic equations by factorisation.
4. Use the quadratic formula to find roots.
5. Use the discriminant to predict the nature of roots.
6. Apply quadratic equations to solve real-world problems.

## Competency Focus

- **Problem Solving:** Translate word problems into quadratic equations.
- **Reasoning:** Justify whether an equation is quadratic and interpret the discriminant.
- **Communication:** Present solutions clearly with logical steps.
