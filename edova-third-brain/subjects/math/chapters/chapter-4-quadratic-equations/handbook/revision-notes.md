# Revision Notes: Quadratic Equations

## 1. Identifying Quadratic Equations

Simplify the equation and check the highest power of $x$. It must be 2, and the coefficient of $x^2$ must not be zero.

## 2. Standard Form

Write as $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ with terms in descending order of degree.

## 3. Solution by Factorisation

- Split the middle term.
- Factor by grouping.
- Use the zero product property: if $pq = 0$, then $p = 0$ or $q = 0$.

## 4. Quadratic Formula

For $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$:

$$x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Use this when factorisation is difficult.

## 5. Nature of Roots

Calculate discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$:

| $D$ | Nature of Roots |
|---|---|
| $D > 0$ | Two distinct real roots |
| $D = 0$ | Two equal real roots |
| $D < 0$ | No real roots |

## 6. Word Problems

Common textbook problems:
- Rectangular plots and areas.
- Consecutive positive integers.
- Ages (Rohan's mother problem).
- Train speed problems.
- Pole in a circular park.
- Rectangular mango grove.

**Strategy:** Read carefully, define the variable, form the equation, solve, and verify the answer makes sense.

## 7. Important Tips for Board Exam

- Always simplify the equation first.
- Show all steps clearly.
- For nature-of-roots questions, calculate $D$ and conclude.
- For word problems, reject solutions that do not fit the context.
