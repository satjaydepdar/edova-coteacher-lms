# Board Exam Preparation: Quadratic Equations

## Expected Question Types

| Marks | Typical Question |
|---|---|
| 1 mark | Identify quadratic equation; find discriminant |
| 2 marks | Solve by factorisation; find nature of roots |
| 3 marks | Solve using quadratic formula; word problem |
| 4 marks | Complex word problem from Exercise 4.3 |
| 5 marks (case study) | Real-life application with multiple sub-questions |

## Frequent NCERT Topics

1. Checking whether equations are quadratic (Exercise 4.1).
2. Representing situations as quadratic equations (Exercise 4.1).
3. Finding roots by factorisation (Exercise 4.2).
4. Finding two numbers given sum and product.
5. Finding consecutive positive integers given sum of squares.
6. Right triangle altitude-base-hypotenuse problem.
7. Determining nature of roots using discriminant (Exercise 4.3).
8. Finding $k$ for equal roots.
9. Pole in circular park problem.
10. Rectangular mango grove / park problems.

## Time Management

- 1-mark questions: ~1 minute each.
- 2-mark questions: ~3 minutes each.
- 3-mark questions: ~5 minutes each.
- 4-mark questions: ~7 minutes each.

## Common Errors to Avoid

- Not simplifying before deciding if an equation is quadratic.
- Sign errors with $b$ and $c$ in the quadratic formula.
- Forgetting to reject negative or contextually invalid roots.
- Not writing the final conclusion for nature-of-roots questions.

## Sample Board-Style Questions

1. Find the discriminant of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ and hence find the nature of its roots.
2. Solve for $x$: $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$.
3. Find the roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ by factorisation.
4. The sum of the ages of two friends is 20 years. Four years ago, the product of their ages was 48. Is this possible? If so, find their present ages.
5. Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.
