# Classroom Activities

## Activity 1: Quadratic Equation Sort

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Prepare cards with equations from Exercise 4.1. Students sort them into "Quadratic" and "Not Quadratic" and justify each decision.

**Sample Cards:**
- $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
- $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$
- $x(2x + 3) = x^2 + 1$
- $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$

---

## Activity 2: Factorisation Race

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams of 4

Each team receives a set of equations from Exercise 4.2 to solve by factorisation. The team that solves all correctly first wins.

**Equations:**
1. $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$
2. $2x^2 + x - 6 = 0$
3. $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$
4. $100x^2 - 20x + 1 = 0$

---

## Activity 3: Discriminant Quick-Fire

**Time:** 10 minutes | **Group Size:** Whole class

The teacher writes equations from Exercise 4.3 on the board. Students hold up cards: green for two distinct real roots, blue for equal roots, red for no real roots.

---

## Activity 4: Real-Life Problem Stations

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Set up stations with NCERT word problems from Exercises 4.1, 4.2, and 4.3. Groups rotate every 6 minutes.

**Station 1:** Rectangular plot area 528 m², length one more than twice breadth.

**Station 2:** Product of two consecutive positive integers is 306.

**Station 3:** Rohan's mother is 26 years older; product of ages 3 years from now is 360.

**Station 4:** Train travels 480 km; 8 km/h less speed takes 3 hours more.

**Station 5:** Design a rectangular mango grove: length twice breadth, area 800 m².
