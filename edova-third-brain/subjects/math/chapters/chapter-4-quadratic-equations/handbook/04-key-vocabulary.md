## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Quadratic Equation** | A polynomial equation of degree two in one variable, written as $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$. |
| **Standard Form** | The arrangement $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ with terms in descending order of degree. |
| **Coefficient** | The numerical factor of a term; $a$ is the coefficient of $x^2$, $b$ of $x$, and $c$ is the constant term. |
| **Root / Zero** | A value of $x$ that satisfies the quadratic equation. The zeroes of the quadratic polynomial and the roots of the quadratic equation are the same. |
| **Factorisation** | Expressing a polynomial as a product of simpler polynomials (factors). |
| **Quadratic Formula** | The formula $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ giving the roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$. |
| **Discriminant** | The expression $D = b^2 - 4ac$ used to determine the nature of roots. |
| **Real Roots** | Roots that belong to the set of real numbers. |
| **Equal Roots** | Two identical roots, occurring when $D = 0$ (also called coincident roots). |
| **Distinct Real Roots** | Two different real roots, occurring when $D > 0$. |
| **No Real Roots** | No real solutions, occurring when $D < 0$. |
