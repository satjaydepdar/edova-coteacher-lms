# Easy Worked Examples

## Example 1: Check whether an equation is quadratic

Check whether $(x - 2)^2 + 1 = 2x - 3$ is a quadratic equation.

**Solution:**

LHS = $(x - 2)^2 + 1 = x^2 - 4x + 4 + 1 = x^2 - 4x + 5$

So, $x^2 - 4x + 5 = 2x - 3$

$$x^2 - 6x + 8 = 0$$

This is of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$. Therefore, it is a quadratic equation.

## Example 2: Solve by factorisation

Find the roots of $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$.

**Solution:**

Find two numbers with product $-10$ and sum $-3$: $-5$ and $2$.

$$x^2 - 5x + 2x - 10 = 0$$
$$x(x - 5) + 2(x - 5) = 0$$
$$(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$$

So $x = 5$ or $x = -2$.

## Example 3: Nature of roots

Find the discriminant of $x^2 - 4x + 4 = 0$ and state the nature of roots.

**Solution:**

$a = 1$, $b = -4$, $c = 4$

$$b^2 - 4ac = (-4)^2 - 4(1)(4) = 16 - 16 = 0$$

Since the discriminant is 0, the equation has two equal real roots.

## Example 4: Using the quadratic formula

Solve: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$

**Solution:**

$a = 1$, $b = -5$, $c = 6$

$$x = \dfrac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 24}}{2} = \dfrac{5 \pm 1}{2}$$

So $x = 3$ or $x = 2$.
