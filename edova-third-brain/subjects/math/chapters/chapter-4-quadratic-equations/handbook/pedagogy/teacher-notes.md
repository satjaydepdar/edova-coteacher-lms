# Teacher Notes: Quadratic Equations

## Lesson Flow Suggestion

### Day 1: Introduction and Identifying Quadratic Equations
- Define quadratic equation using standard form.
- Work through NCERT Example 2: checking whether equations are quadratic.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 4.1.

### Day 2: Representing Situations as Quadratic Equations
- Prayer hall problem from Section 4.1.
- John and Jivanti marbles problem.
- Cottage industry toys problem.
- Assign remaining questions from Exercise 4.1.

### Day 3: Solution by Factorisation
- Define root/zero of a quadratic equation.
- Review splitting the middle term.
- Work through NCERT Examples 3, 4, and 5.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 4.2.

### Day 4: Solving Word Problems by Factorisation
- Prayer hall dimensions (Example 6).
- Exercise 4.2 word problems.
- Emphasise rejecting invalid roots.

### Day 5: Nature of Roots
- Introduce discriminant.
- Use quadratic formula to find roots when they exist.
- Work through NCERT Examples 7, 8, and 9.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 4.3.

### Day 6: Mixed Practice and Revision
- Mixed problems from all exercises.
- Board-style questions.

## Teaching Tips

- Always insist on simplifying the equation first before deciding if it is quadratic.
- Use graphing tools to show the connection between roots and x-intercepts.
- Encourage students to verify roots by substitution.
- For word problems, ask students to check whether each root makes sense in the given context.
