# Inclusive Education

## For Students with Dyscalculia

- Use colour coding for $a$, $b$, $c$ consistently.
- Provide formula cards and step-by-step templates.
- Allow extra time for calculations.
- Use manipulatives like algebra tiles for factorisation.

## For Students with Visual Impairments

- Provide tactile graph paper.
- Use raised-line drawings for parabolas.
- Read equations aloud clearly and use audio descriptions.

## For Students with Hearing Impairments

- Provide written instructions and visual cues.
- Use captioned videos if showing tutorials.
- Encourage peer note-sharing.

## For Slow Learners

- Break factorisation into micro-steps.
- Provide additional practice with immediate feedback.
- Use frequent revision and reinforcement.
- Master equations with $a = 1$ before moving to $a \neq 1$.

## For Gifted Students

- Offer enrichment tasks such as deriving the quadratic formula.
- Introduce sum and product of roots relationships.
- Assign project-based learning on real-life applications from the textbook.
