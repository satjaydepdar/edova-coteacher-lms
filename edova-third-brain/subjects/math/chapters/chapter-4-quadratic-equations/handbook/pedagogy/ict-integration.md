# ICT Integration

## Recommended Tools

1. **GeoGebra / Desmos**
   - Plot parabolas and observe how changing $a$, $b$, $c$ affects the graph.
   - Visually confirm the number of x-intercepts based on the discriminant.

2. **Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets**
   - Create tables of values for quadratic functions.
   - Use graphs to estimate roots.

3. **Khan Academy / NCERT Exemplar Apps**
   - Practice problems with instant feedback.

4. **Interactive Quizzes**
   - Kahoot / Quizizz for quick revision of discriminant and roots.

## Suggested Activities

- **Parabola Explorer:** Students change sliders for $a$, $b$, $c$ and record how the graph changes.
- **Root Finder:** Use graphing software to find roots and verify algebraically.
- **Discriminant Challenge:** Online quiz where students classify equations by nature of roots.
