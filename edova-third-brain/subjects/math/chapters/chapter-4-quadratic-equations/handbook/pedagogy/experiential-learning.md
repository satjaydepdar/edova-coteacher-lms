# Experiential Learning

## Activity 1: Throwing a Ball

Throw a ball and observe its path. Discuss how the height of the ball over time can be modelled by a quadratic equation. This connects to projectile motion in physics.

## Activity 2: Area with Tiles

Use square tiles to form rectangles of given area. Find all possible dimensions and observe when a quadratic equation arises.

## Activity 3: Number Product Hunt

Find two numbers whose product is 48 and sum is 14. Explore how many such pairs exist and how this relates to factorisation.

## Activity 4: Classroom Garden Design

Design a small rectangular garden with a fixed perimeter. Determine possible dimensions and areas, leading to a quadratic equation for maximum area.
