# Differentiation Strategies

## For Struggling Learners

- Provide a checklist for identifying $a$, $b$, $c$.
- Use factorisation puzzles and matching activities.
- Allow use of formula sheet during practice.
- Pair with peer tutors for guided practice.
- Focus first on equations where $a = 1$.

## For Average Learners

- Mixed practice with factorisation and quadratic formula.
- Scaffolded word problems with partially filled steps.
- Regular short quizzes on discriminant and nature of roots.

## For Advanced Learners

- Explore the historical note about Sridharacharya's derivation of the quadratic formula.
- Solve equations with parameters (e.g., find $k$ for equal roots).
- Explore the relationship between sum/product of roots and coefficients.
- Investigate graphs of quadratic functions using graphing software.

## Visual Learners

- Use parabola graphs to connect roots and discriminant.
- Draw diagrams for word problems.

## Kinesthetic Learners

- Factorisation relay races.
- Card sorts matching equations to their roots.

## Verbal Learners

- Explain solution steps to a partner.
- Write "how-to" guides for factorisation and using the formula.
