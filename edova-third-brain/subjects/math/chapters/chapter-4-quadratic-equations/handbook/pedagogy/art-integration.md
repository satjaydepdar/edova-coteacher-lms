# Art Integration

## Activity 1: Parabola Art

Students create artistic designs using parabolas on graph paper or digital tools. They label vertices, axes of symmetry, and roots.

## Activity 2: Formula Poster

Students design a colourful poster of the quadratic formula with step-by-step instructions and an example.

## Activity 3: Discriminant Colour Coding

Provide a set of quadratic equations. Students colour-code them based on the nature of roots:
- Green: two distinct real roots
- Blue: two equal real roots
- Red: no real roots

## Activity 4: Story Comic Strip

Create a comic strip that tells the story of solving a word problem using a quadratic equation, showing each step visually.
