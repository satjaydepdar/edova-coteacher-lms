# Previous Year Questions

**Q1.** Find the discriminant of the quadratic equation $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ and hence find the nature of its roots.

**Q2.** Solve for $x$: $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$.

**Q3.** Find the roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$.

**Q4.** Find the value of $k$ for which the equation $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$ has equal roots.

**Q5.** The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. Find the integers.

**Q6.** The altitude of a right triangle is 7 cm less than its base. If the hypotenuse is 13 cm, find the other two sides.

**Q7.** A train travels a distance of 480 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 8 km/h less, then it would have taken 3 hours more to cover the same distance. Find the speed of the train.

**Q8.** Find the discriminant of $3x^2 - 2x + \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$ and hence find the nature of its roots.

---

## Answers

**A1.** $D = (-4)^2 - 4(2)(3) = 16 - 24 = -8 < 0$. No real roots.

**A2.** $(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = 5$ or $x = -2$.

**A3.** $D = 24 - 24 = 0$. Equal roots: $x = \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$.

**A4.** $k^2 - 24 = 0$; $k = \pm 2\sqrt{6}$.

**A5.** Let integers be $x$ and $x + 1$.

$x^2 + (x + 1)^2$ is not needed; use $x(x + 1) = 306$.
$x^2 + x - 306 = 0$
$(x + 18)(x - 17) = 0$

Integers: 17 and 18.

**A6.** Let base = $x$ cm, altitude = $(x - 7)$ cm.

$x^2 + (x - 7)^2 = 169$
$x^2 - 7x - 60 = 0$
$(x - 12)(x + 5) = 0$

Base = 12 cm, Altitude = 5 cm.

**A7.** Let speed = $x$ km/h.

$\dfrac{480}{x - 8} - \dfrac{480}{x} = 3$
$x^2 - 8x - 1280 = 0$
$(x - 40)(x + 32) = 0$

Speed = 40 km/h.

**A8.** $D = (-2)^2 - 4(3)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 4 - 4 = 0$. Equal roots.
