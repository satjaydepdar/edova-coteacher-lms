# Competency-Based Questions

**Q1.** A student claims that the equation $x^2 + px + q = 0$ has real roots if $p^2 \geq 4q$. Is the claim correct? Justify.

**Q2.** Formulate a real-life problem that can be modelled by the equation $x^2 - 55x + 750 = 0$ (similar to the toys production problem).

**Q3.** Analyse the error in the following solution:

Solve $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$.

Student's work:
$$(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$$
$$x - 5 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 5$$
$$x + 2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 2$$

Correct the error.

**Q4.** Design a quadratic equation whose roots are $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Verify by factorising.

**Q5.** The equation $x^2 - 20x + 112 = 0$ arose from a word problem about the ages of two friends. What does the negative discriminant tell you about the original situation?

**Q6.** Compare the methods of factorisation and using the quadratic formula. When would you prefer each?

---

## Answers

**A1.** Yes, the claim is correct. For real roots, $D = p^2 - 4q \geq 0$, i.e., $p^2 \geq 4q$.

**A2.** Sample: "A cottage industry produces toys. The cost per toy is ₹55 minus the number of toys. Total cost is ₹750. Find the number of toys." Answer: 25 or 30 toys.

**A3.** The factorisation is correct, but the student made a sign error. From $x + 2 = 0$, $x = -2$, not $x = 2$.

Correct roots: $x = 5$ or $x = -2$.

**A4.** Equation: $\left(x - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(x + \dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 0$

Multiply by 6: $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$.

Verification: $6x^2 - x - 2 = (3x - 2)(2x + 1)$, so roots are $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.

**A5.** The negative discriminant ($D = -48$) tells us that there are no real ages that satisfy the given conditions. The situation described in the problem is not possible.

**A6.** Factorisation is preferred when the quadratic can be easily factorised. The quadratic formula is preferred when factorisation is difficult or when we need to determine the nature of roots first.
