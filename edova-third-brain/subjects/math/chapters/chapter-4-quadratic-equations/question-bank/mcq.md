## Questions

**Q1.** Which of the following is a quadratic equation?

(a) $x + 5 = 0$
(b) $x^2 - 4 = 0$
(c) $x^3 + 2x = 0$
(d) $\sqrt{x} + 3 = 0$

**Q2.** The standard form of $x(x + 3) = 10$ is:

(a) $x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$
(b) $x^2 + 3x + 10 = 0$
(c) $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$
(d) $x^2 - 10 = 0$

**Q3.** The roots of $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$ are:

(a) 2 and 5
(b) -2 and -5
(c) 5 and -2
(d) -5 and 2

**Q4.** The discriminant of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ is:

(a) 8
(b) -8
(c) 16
(d) 28

**Q5.** If $x^2 - kx + 9 = 0$ has equal roots, then $k$ is:

(a) 3
(b) ±3
(c) 6
(d) ±6

**Q6.** The quadratic formula for $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ is:

(a) $x = \dfrac{b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
(b) $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
(c) $x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 + 4ac}}{2a}$
(d) $x = \dfrac{b \pm \sqrt{b^2 + 4ac}}{2a}$

**Q7.** If the discriminant of a quadratic equation is negative, then:

(a) roots are real and distinct
(b) roots are real and equal
(c) roots are not real
(d) roots are rational

**Q8.** The equation $x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0$ has:

(a) distinct real roots
(b) equal real roots
(c) no real roots
(d) irrational roots

**Q9.** Which of the following is NOT a quadratic equation?

(a) $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
(b) $x^2 + 3x + 1 = (x - 2)^2$
(c) $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$
(d) $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$

**Q10.** The roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ are:

(a) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
(b) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (repeated)
(c) $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(d) no real roots

## Answers

1. (b)  2. (a)  3. (c)  4. (b)  5. (d)  6. (b)  7. (c)  8. (b)  9. (c)  10. (b)
