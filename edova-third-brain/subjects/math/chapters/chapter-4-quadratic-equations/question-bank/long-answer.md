# Long Answer Questions

**Q1.** Find the roots of $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$ by factorisation.

**Q2.** Find the roots of $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$.

**Q3.** Find the roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$.

**Q4.** Find the discriminant of $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$ and hence find the nature of its roots.

**Q5.** A pole has to be erected at a point on the boundary of a circular park of diameter 13 metres in such a way that the differences of its distances from two diametrically opposite fixed gates A and B on the boundary is 7 metres. Is it possible to do so? If yes, at what distances from the two gates should the pole be erected?

**Q6.** Find the discriminant of $3x^2 - 2x + \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$ and hence find the nature of its roots. Find them, if they are real.

**Q7.** A train travels a distance of 480 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 8 km/h less, then it would have taken 3 hours more to cover the same distance. Find the speed of the train.

**Q8.** Is it possible to design a rectangular mango grove whose length is twice its breadth, and the area is 800 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

## Answers

**A1.** $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 2x^2 - 2x - 3x + 3 = 2x(x - 1) - 3(x - 1) = (2x - 3)(x - 1)$.

So $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ or $x = 1$.

**A2.** $6x^2 - x - 2 = 6x^2 + 3x - 4x - 2 = 3x(2x + 1) - 2(2x + 1) = (3x - 2)(2x + 1)$.

So $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$ or $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

**A3.** $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = (\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2})^2$.

So $x = \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (repeated).

**A4.** $D = (-4)^2 - 4(2)(3) = 16 - 24 = -8 < 0$. No real roots.

**A5.** Let distance from gate B = $x$ m. Then distance from gate A = $(x + 7)$ m.

$(x + 7)^2 + x^2 = 13^2$
$x^2 + 7x - 60 = 0$

$D = 289 > 0$, so possible.

$x = 5$ or $x = -12$. Since distance is positive, $x = 5$.

Pole is 5 m from gate B and 12 m from gate A.

**A6.** $D = (-2)^2 - 4(3)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 4 - 4 = 0$. Equal roots.

$x = \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

**A7.** Let speed = $x$ km/h.

$\dfrac{480}{x - 8} - \dfrac{480}{x} = 3$

Solving: $x^2 - 8x - 1280 = 0$
$(x - 40)(x + 32) = 0$

Speed = 40 km/h.

**A8.** Let breadth = $x$, length = $2x$.

$2x^2 = 800$
$x^2 = 400$
$x = 20$

Breadth = 20 m, Length = 40 m. Yes, possible.
