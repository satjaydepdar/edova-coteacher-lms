# Short Answer Questions

**Q1.** Check whether $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$ is a quadratic equation.

**Q2.** Solve: $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$.

**Q3.** Find the discriminant of $3x^2 - 2x + \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$.

**Q4.** If $x = 3$ is a root of $x^2 - kx + 6 = 0$, find $k$.

**Q5.** Find the roots of $2x^2 + x - 6 = 0$ by factorisation.

**Q6.** For what value of $k$ does $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$ have equal roots?

**Q7.** Find the dimensions of a rectangular plot whose area is 528 m² and whose length is one more than twice its breadth.

**Q8.** Find the nature of roots of $2x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0$.

**Q9.** A cottage industry produces a certain number of toys in a day. The cost of production of each toy is 55 minus the number of toys produced. If the total cost is ₹750, find the number of toys produced.

**Q10.** Find two consecutive positive integers whose squares sum to 365.

---

## Answers

**A1.** Expanding: $x^3 + 6x^2 + 12x + 8 = x^3 - 4$, so $6x^2 + 12x + 12 = 0$ or $x^2 + 2x + 2 = 0$. Yes, quadratic.

**A2.** $(x - 5)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = 5$ or $x = -2$.

**A3.** $D = (-2)^2 - 4(3)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 4 - 4 = 0$.

**A4.** Substituting $x = 3$: $9 - 3k + 6 = 0$, so $k = 5$.

**A5.** $(2x - 3)(x + 2) = 0$; $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$ or $x = -2$.

**A6.** For equal roots: $k^2 - 24 = 0$, so $k = \pm 2\sqrt{6}$.

**A7.** Let breadth = $x$, length = $2x + 1$.

$2x^2 + x - 528 = 0$
$(x - 16)(2x + 33) = 0$

Breadth = 16 m, Length = 33 m.

**A8.** $D = (-3)^2 - 4(2)(5) = 9 - 40 = -31 < 0$; no real roots.

**A9.** Let number of toys = $x$.

$x(55 - x) = 750$
$x^2 - 55x + 750 = 0$
$(x - 25)(x - 30) = 0$

25 or 30 toys.

**A10.** Let integers be $x$ and $x + 1$.

$x^2 + (x + 1)^2 = 365$
$2x^2 + 2x - 364 = 0$
$x^2 + x - 182 = 0$
$(x + 14)(x - 13) = 0$

Integers: 13 and 14.
