# Case Study Questions

## Case Study 1: Prayer Hall (NCERT Section 4.1)

A charity trust decides to build a prayer hall having a carpet area of 300 square metres with its length one metre more than twice its breadth.

**Q1.** If the breadth of the hall is $x$ metres, express the length in terms of $x$.

**Q2.** Form a quadratic equation representing the area of the hall.

**Q3.** Find the length and breadth of the hall.

---

## Case Study 2: Rectangular Plot (NCERT Exercise 4.1)

The area of a rectangular plot is 528 m². The length of the plot is one more than twice its breadth.

**Q4.** Form a quadratic equation for the dimensions of the plot.

**Q5.** Find the length and breadth of the plot.

---

## Case Study 3: Pole in Circular Park (NCERT Example 8)

A pole has to be erected on the boundary of a circular park of diameter 13 m such that the difference of its distances from two diametrically opposite gates A and B is 7 m.

**Q6.** Form a quadratic equation to find the position of the pole.

**Q7.** Is it possible to erect the pole? If yes, find the distances from the two gates.

---

## Answers

### Case Study 1

**A1.** Length = $(2x + 1)$ metres.

**A2.** $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$.

**A3.** $(x - 12)(2x + 25) = 0$. Since $x > 0$, $x = 12$.

Breadth = 12 m, Length = 25 m.

### Case Study 2

**A4.** $2x^2 + x - 528 = 0$.

**A5.** $(x - 16)(2x + 33) = 0$. Since $x > 0$, $x = 16$.

Breadth = 16 m, Length = 33 m.

### Case Study 3

**A6.** Let distance from gate B = $x$ m. Then distance from gate A = $(x + 7)$ m.

$(x + 7)^2 + x^2 = 13^2$
$x^2 + 7x - 60 = 0$.

**A7.** Discriminant = $49 + 240 = 289 > 0$, so possible.

$x = \dfrac{-7 \pm 17}{2}$, so $x = 5$ or $x = -12$.

Since distance is positive, $x = 5$.

Pole is 5 m from gate B and 12 m from gate A.
