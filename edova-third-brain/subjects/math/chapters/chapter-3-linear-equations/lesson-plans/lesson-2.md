# Lesson 2

## Chapter 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
### Topic: Substitution Method (NCERT Section 3.3.1)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Express one variable in terms of the other from a linear equation.
- Solve a pair of linear equations using the substitution method.
- Identify dependent and inconsistent pairs from the substitution result.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Substitution method steps:
  1. Express $x$ or $y$ from one equation.
  2. Substitute into the second equation.
  3. Solve for one variable.
  4. Substitute back to find the other variable.
- A true statement with no variable → infinitely many solutions.
- A false statement → no solution.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** "Can we solve the same pair without drawing a graph?"
- **Recall:** Solving linear equations in one variable.
- **Explain:** Why algebraic methods are needed when graphs give non-integral coordinates.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Example 4:** Solve $7x - 15y = 2$ and $x + 2y = 3$ by substitution.
- **Example 5:** Age problem — Aftab and his daughter.
- **Example 6:** Show that $2x + 3y = 9$ and $4x + 6y = 18$ have infinitely many solutions.
- **Example 7:** Show that $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and $2x + 4y - 12 = 0$ have no solution.
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 3.2 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the substitution method and special cases.
- Emphasise careful handling of signs.
- Assign remaining Exercise 3.2 questions as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Solve $x + y = 14$ and $x - y = 4$ by substitution.
