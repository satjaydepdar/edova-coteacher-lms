# Lesson 5

## Chapter 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables
### Topic: Word Problems and Mixed Practice

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Form a pair of linear equations from a word problem.
- Choose the most efficient method to solve real-life problems.
- Verify solutions and interpret them in context.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Steps for solving word problems:
  1. Identify the two unknowns.
  2. Form two equations using the given conditions.
  3. Solve using graphical or algebraic methods.
  4. Verify and state the answer in context.
- Common contexts: ages, digits, money, speed-distance, fixed and variable charges.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** "Where in daily life do we face two unknown quantities?"
- **Discuss:** Taxi fares, shopping bills, ages, two-digit numbers.
- **Recall:** All four methods learned so far.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Age Problem:** Aftab and his daughter.
- **Two-Digit Number Problem:** Sum and difference/reversal of digits.
- **Money Problem:** Lending library fixed and extra charges.
- **Fraction Problem:** Fraction changes when numbers are added to numerator and denominator.
- **Student Practice:** Solve mixed word problems from NCERT Exercises 3.2 and 3.3.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the chapter: general form, graphical interpretation, algebraic methods, reducible equations.
- Discuss common errors and how to avoid them.
- Assign a mixed practice worksheet as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Nine times the number is twice the number obtained by reversing the digits. Find the number.
