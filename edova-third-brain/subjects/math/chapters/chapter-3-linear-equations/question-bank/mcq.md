## Questions

**Q1.** A pair of linear equations which has no solution is called:

(a) consistent
(b) inconsistent
(c) dependent
(d) equivalent

**Q2.** If the lines representing a pair of linear equations intersect at a point, then the pair is:

(a) inconsistent
(b) consistent with a unique solution
(c) dependent
(d) none of these

**Q3.** For the pair $2x + 3y - 8 = 0$ and $4x + 6y - 16 = 0$, the lines are:

(a) intersecting
(b) parallel
(c) coincident
(d) perpendicular

**Q4.** The pair $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and $2x + 4y - 12 = 0$ is:

(a) consistent
(b) inconsistent
(c) dependent
(d) none of these

**Q5.** Which method is used to solve $7x - 15y = 2$ and $x + 2y = 3$ in NCERT Example 4?

(a) Graphical method
(b) Substitution method
(c) Elimination method
(d) Cross-multiplication method

**Q6.** If $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$ for a pair of linear equations, then the pair has:

(a) no solution
(b) infinitely many solutions
(c) a unique solution
(d) none of these

**Q7.** In the substitution method, the first step is to:

(a) make coefficients equal
(b) express one variable in terms of the other
(c) add the two equations
(d) subtract the two equations

**Q8.** The solution of $x + y = 14$ and $x - y = 4$ is:

(a) $x = 5, y = 9$
(b) $x = 9, y = 5$
(c) $x = 10, y = 4$
(d) $x = 4, y = 10$

**Q9.** The number of solutions of $2x + 3y = 9$ and $4x + 6y = 18$ is:

(a) zero
(b) one
(c) two
(d) infinitely many

**Q10.** A dependent pair of linear equations has:

(a) no solution
(b) a unique solution
(c) infinitely many solutions
(d) exactly two solutions

## Answers

1. (b)  2. (b)  3. (c)  4. (b)  5. (b)  6. (c)  7. (b)  8. (b)  9. (d)  10. (c)
