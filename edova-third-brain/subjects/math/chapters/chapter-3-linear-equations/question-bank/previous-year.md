# Previous Year Questions

**Q1.** Solve the following pair of equations by substitution: $x + y = 14$ and $x - y = 4$.

**Q2.** Solve by elimination: $3x + 4y = 10$ and $2x - 2y = 2$.

**Q3.** Check whether the pair $5x - 3y = 11$ and $-10x + 6y = -22$ is consistent or inconsistent.

**Q4.** The taxi charges in a city consist of a fixed charge together with the charge for the distance covered. For a distance of 10 km, the charge paid is ₹105 and for a journey of 15 km, the charge paid is ₹155. What are the fixed charges and the charge per km? How much does a person have to pay for travelling a distance of 25 km?

**Q5.** A fraction becomes $\dfrac{9}{11}$ if 2 is added to both numerator and denominator. If 3 is added to both, it becomes $\dfrac{5}{6}$. Find the fraction.

**Q6.** The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, nine times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. Find the number.

**Q7.** Half the perimeter of a rectangular garden, whose length is 4 m more than its width, is 36 m. Find the dimensions of the garden.

**Q8.** The ratio of incomes of two persons is $9 : 7$ and the ratio of their expenditures is $4 : 3$. If each saves ₹2000 per month, find their monthly incomes.

**Q9.** Solve graphically: $x + 3y = 6$ and $2x - 3y = 12$.

**Q10.** Meena went to a bank to withdraw ₹2000. She asked the cashier to give her ₹50 and ₹100 notes only. Meena got 25 notes in all. Find how many notes of ₹50 and ₹100 she received.

---

## Answers

**A1.** $x = 9$, $y = 5$.

**A2.** $x = 2$, $y = 1$.

**A3.** $\dfrac{5}{-10} = \dfrac{-3}{6} = \dfrac{-11}{-22} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. All ratios are equal, so the pair is dependent (consistent) with infinitely many solutions.

**A4.** Fixed charge = ₹5, charge per km = ₹10. Charge for 25 km = ₹255.

**A5.** The fraction is $\dfrac{7}{9}$.

**A6.** The number is 18.

**A7.** Let width = $x$ m and length = $(x + 4)$ m. Then $x + (x + 4) = 36 \Rightarrow x = 16$. Width = 16 m, length = 20 m.

**A8.** Incomes are ₹18,000 and ₹14,000.

**A9.** The lines intersect at $(6, 0)$. Solution: $x = 6$, $y = 0$.

**A10.** 10 notes of ₹50 and 15 notes of ₹100.
