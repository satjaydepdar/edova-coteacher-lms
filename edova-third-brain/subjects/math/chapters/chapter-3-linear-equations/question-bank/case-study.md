# Case Study Questions

## Case Study 1: Quiz Participants (NCERT Exercise 3.1 Q1(i))

10 students of Class X took part in a Mathematics quiz. The number of girls is 4 more than the number of boys.

**Q1.** If $x$ represents the number of boys and $y$ the number of girls, write the pair of linear equations.

**Q2.** Solve the equations graphically.

**Q3.** How many boys and how many girls took part in the quiz?

---

## Case Study 2: Taxi Charges (NCERT Exercise 3.2 Q3(iv))

The taxi charges in a city consist of a fixed charge together with a charge for the distance covered. For a distance of 10 km, the charge paid is ₹105. For a journey of 15 km, the charge paid is ₹155.

**Q4.** What are the fixed charge and the charge per km?

**Q5.** How much does a person have to pay for travelling a distance of 25 km?

---

## Case Study 3: Lending Library (NCERT Exercise 3.3 Q2(v))

A lending library has a fixed charge for the first three days and an additional charge for each day thereafter. Saritha paid ₹27 for a book kept for seven days, while Susy paid ₹21 for the book she kept for five days.

**Q6.** If the fixed charge is ₹$x$ and the extra charge per day is ₹$y$, write the two equations.

**Q7.** Find the fixed charge and the charge for each extra day.

---

## Case Study 4: Bank Notes (NCERT Exercise 3.3 Q2(iv))

Meena went to a bank to withdraw ₹2000. She asked the cashier to give her ₹50 and ₹100 notes only. Meena got 25 notes in all.

**Q8.** Write the pair of linear equations for the number of ₹50 and ₹100 notes.

**Q9.** Find how many notes of ₹50 and ₹100 she received.

---

## Answers

### Case Study 1

**A1.** $x + y = 10$ and $y = x + 4$, i.e., $x - y + 4 = 0$.

**A2.** The lines intersect at $(3, 7)$.

**A3.** 3 boys and 7 girls took part in the quiz.

### Case Study 2

**A4.** Let fixed charge = ₹$x$ and charge per km = ₹$y$. Equations: $x + 10y = 105$ and $x + 15y = 155$. Solving, $y = 10$ and $x = 5$. Fixed charge = ₹5, charge per km = ₹10.

**A5.** Charge for 25 km = $x + 25y = 5 + 250 = ₹255$.

### Case Study 3

**A6.** $x + 4y = 27$ and $x + 2y = 21$.

**A7.** Subtracting: $2y = 6 \Rightarrow y = 3$. Then $x = 15$. Fixed charge = ₹15, extra charge per day = ₹3.

### Case Study 4

**A8.** Let number of ₹50 notes = $x$ and ₹100 notes = $y$. Equations: $x + y = 25$ and $50x + 100y = 2000$.

**A9.** From the first equation, $x = 25 - y$. Substituting in the second: $50(25 - y) + 100y = 2000 \Rightarrow 1250 + 50y = 2000 \Rightarrow y = 15$. Then $x = 10$. Meena received 10 notes of ₹50 and 15 notes of ₹100.
