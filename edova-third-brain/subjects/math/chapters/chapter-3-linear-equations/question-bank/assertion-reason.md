# Assertion-Reason Questions

In each question, two statements are given:

- **Assertion (A)**
- **Reason (R)**

Choose the correct option:

(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true but R is false.
(d) A is false but R is true.

---

**Q1.** **Assertion (A):** The pair $2x + 3y = 9$ and $4x + 6y = 18$ has infinitely many solutions.

**Reason (R):** The two equations represent coincident lines.

**Q2.** **Assertion (A):** The pair $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and $2x + 4y - 12 = 0$ is inconsistent.

**Reason (R):** For inconsistency, $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$.

**Q3.** **Assertion (A):** The substitution method is useful when one variable can be easily expressed in terms of the other.

**Reason (R):** In the substitution method, we eliminate one variable by making coefficients equal.

**Q4.** **Assertion (A):** The graph of a pair of linear equations in two variables is represented by two lines.

**Reason (R):** A linear equation in two variables always represents a straight line.

**Q5.** **Assertion (A):** A dependent pair of linear equations has infinitely many solutions.

**Reason (R):** A dependent pair of equations is represented by coincident lines.

---

## Answers

1. (a) Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A.
2. (a) Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A.
3. (c) A is true but R is false (R describes the elimination method, not substitution).
4. (b) Both A and R are true but R does not explain A directly.
5. (a) Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A.
