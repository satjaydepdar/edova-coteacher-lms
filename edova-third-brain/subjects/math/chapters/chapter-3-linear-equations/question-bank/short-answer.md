# Short Answer Questions

**Q1.** Define a consistent pair of linear equations.

**Q2.** Define an inconsistent pair of linear equations.

**Q3.** What is a dependent pair of linear equations?

**Q4.** Without drawing, determine whether $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$ and $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$ represent intersecting, parallel, or coincident lines.

**Q5.** Check whether $3x + 2y = 5$ and $2x - 3y = 7$ are consistent or inconsistent.

**Q6.** Find the value of $k$ for which the pair $2x + ky = 3$ and $4x + 6y = 5$ has no solution.

**Q7.** Solve $x + y = 5$ and $2x - 3y = 4$ by substitution.

**Q8.** Solve $3x + 4y = 10$ and $2x - 2y = 2$ by elimination.

**Q9.** Solve $2x + 3y = 11$ and $2x - 4y = -24$, and hence find the value of $m$ for which $y = mx + 3$.

**Q10.** The larger of two supplementary angles exceeds the smaller by $18°$. Find the angles.

---

## Answers

**A1.** A consistent pair of linear equations is a pair that has at least one solution.

**A2.** An inconsistent pair of linear equations is a pair that has no solution.

**A3.** A dependent pair of linear equations is a pair that has infinitely many solutions.

**A4.** $\dfrac{5}{7} \neq \dfrac{-4}{6}$. Hence the lines intersect and the pair has a unique solution.

**A5.** $\dfrac{3}{2} \neq \dfrac{2}{-3}$, so the pair is consistent.

**A6.** For no solution, $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{k}{6} \neq \dfrac{3}{5}$. This gives $k = 3$.

**A7.** From the first equation, $x = 5 - y$. Substituting in the second: $2(5 - y) - 3y = 4 \Rightarrow 10 - 5y = 4 \Rightarrow y = \dfrac{6}{5}$. Then $x = \dfrac{19}{5}$.

**A8.** Multiply the second equation by 2: $4x - 4y = 4$. Add to the first: $7x = 14 \Rightarrow x = 2$. Then $y = 1$.

**A9.** Subtracting the equations: $7y = 35 \Rightarrow y = 5$. Then $x = -2$. Since $y = mx + 3$, we get $5 = -2m + 3 \Rightarrow m = -1$.

**A10.** Let the angles be $x°$ and $y°$. Then $x + y = 180$ and $x - y = 18$. Solving, $x = 99°$ and $y = 81°$.
