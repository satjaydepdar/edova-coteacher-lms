# Challenge Problems

**Q1.** Given the linear equation $2x + 3y - 8 = 0$, write another linear equation in two variables such that the geometrical representation of the pair so formed is:
(i) intersecting lines
(ii) parallel lines
(iii) coincident lines

**Q2.** Solve: $\dfrac{2}{x} + \dfrac{3}{y} = 13$ and $\dfrac{5}{x} - \dfrac{4}{y} = -2$.

**Q3.** A fraction becomes $\dfrac{9}{11}$ if 2 is added to both the numerator and the denominator. If 3 is added to both, it becomes $\dfrac{5}{6}$. Find the fraction.

**Q4.** Five years hence, the age of Jacob will be three times that of his son. Five years ago, Jacob's age was seven times that of his son. What are their present ages?

**Q5.** If we add 1 to the numerator and subtract 1 from the denominator, a fraction reduces to 1. It becomes $\dfrac{1}{2}$ if we only add 1 to the denominator. What is the fraction?

**Q6.** The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, nine times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. Find the number.

---

## Answers

**A1.** (i) Any equation with $\dfrac{2}{a} \neq \dfrac{3}{b}$, e.g., $3x + 2y - 5 = 0$.
(ii) Any equation with $\dfrac{2}{a} = \dfrac{3}{b} \neq \dfrac{-8}{c}$, e.g., $4x + 6y - 10 = 0$.
(iii) Any equation that is a multiple of the given equation, e.g., $4x + 6y - 16 = 0$.

**A2.** Let $u = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $v = \dfrac{1}{y}$. Then $2u + 3v = 13$ and $5u - 4v = -2$. Solving, $u = 2$ and $v = 3$. Hence $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $y = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

**A3.** Let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$. Equations: $11(x + 2) = 9(y + 2)$ and $6(x + 3) = 5(y + 3)$. Solving, $x = 7$ and $y = 9$. The fraction is $\dfrac{7}{9}$.

**A4.** Let Jacob's age be $x$ and son's age be $y$. Equations: $x + 5 = 3(y + 5)$ and $x - 5 = 7(y - 5)$. Solving, $x = 40$ and $y = 10$. Jacob is 40 years old and his son is 10 years old.

**A5.** Let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$. Equations: $\dfrac{x + 1}{y - 1} = 1$ and $\dfrac{x}{y + 1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Solving, $x = 3$ and $y = 5$. The fraction is $\dfrac{3}{5}$.

**A6.** Let the ten's digit be $x$ and unit's digit be $y$. Equations: $x + y = 9$ and $9(10x + y) = 2(10y + x)$. Simplifying the second: $88x - 11y = 0 \Rightarrow 8x = y$. Solving with $x + y = 9$, $x = 1$ and $y = 8$. The number is 18.
