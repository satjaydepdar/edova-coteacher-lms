# Competency-Based Questions

**Q1.** A student solves a pair of equations and gets $0 = 9$. What does this result mean? Explain with reference to the graphical representation.

**Q2.** Design a real-life problem that can be represented by the pair of equations $5x + 7y = 50$ and $7x + 5y = 46$.

**Q3.** Compare the substitution and elimination methods. When would you prefer one over the other?

**Q4.** A student claims that the pair $x + y = 5$ and $2x + 2y = 10$ has a unique solution. Is the claim correct? Justify.

**Q5.** Analyse the nature of solutions of $3x + 2y = 5$ and $2x - 3y = 7$ without fully solving them.

---

## Answers

**A1.** The result $0 = 9$ is a false statement. It means the pair of equations has no solution. Graphically, the two lines are parallel.

**A2.** Sample problem: "5 pencils and 7 pens together cost ₹50, whereas 7 pencils and 5 pens together cost ₹46. Find the cost of one pencil and one pen."

**A3.** Substitution is preferred when one variable can be easily isolated. Elimination is preferred when coefficients can be made equal by simple multiplication. Both methods give the same solution.

**A4.** The claim is incorrect. The second equation is exactly twice the first, so the lines coincide and the pair has infinitely many solutions.

**A5.** $\dfrac{3}{2} \neq \dfrac{2}{-3}$. Since the ratios of $x$ and $y$ coefficients are not equal, the pair is consistent with a unique solution.
