## General Form

A pair of linear equations in two variables $x$ and $y$:

$$a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$$
$$a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$$

where $a_1, b_1, c_1, a_2, b_2, c_2$ are real numbers.

## Consistency Conditions

| Condition | Nature | Graphical Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$ | Consistent | Intersecting lines; unique solution |
| $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$ | Inconsistent | Parallel lines; no solution |
| $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} = \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$ | Dependent (consistent) | Coincident lines; infinitely many solutions |

## Substitution Method

1. From one equation, express $x$ (or $y$) in terms of the other variable.
2. Substitute this expression in the second equation.
3. Solve the resulting equation in one variable.
4. Substitute back to find the other variable.

## Elimination Method

1. Multiply the equations by suitable numbers to make the coefficients of one variable equal.
2. Add or subtract the equations to eliminate that variable.
3. Solve for the remaining variable.
4. Substitute back to find the other variable.

## Cross-Multiplication Method

$$\frac{x}{b_1c_2 - b_2c_1} = \frac{y}{c_1a_2 - c_2a_1} = \frac{1}{a_1b_2 - a_2b_1}$$

provided $a_1b_2 - a_2b_1 \neq 0$.

## Reducible Equations

For equations of the form $\dfrac{a_1}{x} + \dfrac{b_1}{y} = c_1$ and $\dfrac{a_2}{x} + \dfrac{b_2}{y} = c_2$, substitute $u = \dfrac{1}{x}$ and $v = \dfrac{1}{y}$ to obtain a linear pair in $u$ and $v$.
