# Teacher Reflection: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

## After Each Lesson

- Could students form a pair of linear equations from a word problem?
- Did students correctly plot lines and interpret the graphical solution?
- Were students able to choose the appropriate algebraic method?
- Which step in substitution or elimination caused the most difficulty?

## After the Chapter

- What percentage of students can solve a pair of equations by substitution?
- What percentage can solve by elimination and cross-multiplication?
- Which type of word problem was most challenging — ages, digits, speeds, or money?
- Did students consistently verify their solutions?

## Improvements for Next Year

- Where can I add more real-life examples (taxi fares, shopping, sports scores)?
- Should I allocate more time to graphical interpretation before moving to algebra?
- What misconceptions arose, and how can I address them earlier?
