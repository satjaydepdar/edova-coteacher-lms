# Visual Learning: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

## 1. Types of Solutions on a Graph

Use three large graphs on the board:
- **Intersecting lines:** $x + y = 5$ and $x - y = 1$ meeting at $(3, 2)$.
- **Parallel lines:** $2x + 3y = 6$ and $2x + 3y = 12$ (same slope, different intercepts).
- **Coincident lines:** $x + y = 4$ and $2x + 2y = 8$ (same line drawn twice).

## 2. Slope-Intercept Form

Rewrite equations in the form $y = mx + c$ to show:
- $m$ determines the slope.
- $c$ determines the $y$-intercept.
- Same $m$ and different $c$ $\rightarrow$ parallel lines.
- Same $m$ and same $c$ $\rightarrow$ coincident lines.

## 3. Coefficient Ratio Visual

Display the consistency test as a three-step flowchart:

$$\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} \rightarrow \text{Unique solution}$$
$$\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \dfrac{c_1}{c_2} \rightarrow \text{No solution}$$
$$\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} = \dfrac{c_1}{c_2} \rightarrow \text{Infinitely many solutions}$$

## 4. Method Choice Diagram

Create a decision tree:
- Is one variable easy to isolate? $\rightarrow$ Substitution.
- Can coefficients be matched easily? $\rightarrow$ Elimination.
- Are coefficients fractions or large numbers? $\rightarrow$ Cross-multiplication.

## 5. Real-Life Scenario Cards

Use visual cards showing shopping bills, age timelines, and distance-time graphs to help students form equations.

## Suggested Classroom Visual Aids

- Graph paper and rulers for plotting lines.
- GeoGebra or Desmos for dynamic graphing.
- Coloured markers to highlight corresponding coefficients.
- Flashcards for method selection and consistency tests.
