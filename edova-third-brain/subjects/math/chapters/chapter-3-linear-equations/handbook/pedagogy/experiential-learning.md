# Experiential Learning

## Activity 1: Market Survey

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Students visit a school canteen or shop (or use hypothetical prices) to collect data on two items. They form a pair of equations and solve to find unit prices.

## Activity 2: Human Graph

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Whole class

Students stand at coordinate points on a large grid drawn on the floor. Two groups form two lines, and the class identifies the point of intersection or parallelism.

## Activity 3: Taxi Fare Investigation

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Students collect taxi fare data for two different distances from family members or local transport apps. They form equations and find the fixed charge and per-km charge.

## Activity 4: Number Trick

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

One student thinks of a two-digit number. The partner asks for the sum of digits and the difference of digits, then forms and solves equations to find the number.
