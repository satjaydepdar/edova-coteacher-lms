# Classroom Dialogue

## Opening Discussion

**Teacher:** "If 5 pencils and 7 pens cost ₹50, and 7 pencils and 5 pens cost ₹46, can we find the cost of each item using one equation?"

**Student:** "No, because there are two unknowns."

**Teacher:** "Right. We need two equations. Today we will learn how to solve such pairs."

## On Graphical Solutions

**Teacher:** "What does it mean if two lines on a graph meet at one point?"

**Student:** "There is exactly one solution."

**Teacher:** "And if the lines never meet?"

**Student:** "There is no solution."

## On Algebraic Methods

**Teacher:** "When is substitution easier than elimination?"

**Student:** "When one variable is easy to isolate."

**Teacher:** "When is cross-multiplication useful?"

**Student:** "When the coefficients are large or fractions."

## Closing Reflection

**Teacher:** "Where in real life do we use a pair of linear equations?"

**Student:** "Taxi fares, shopping bills, age problems, and speed-distance problems."
