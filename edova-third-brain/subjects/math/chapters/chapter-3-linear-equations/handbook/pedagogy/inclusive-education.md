# Inclusive Education

## For Students with Dyscalculia

- Use colour coding for coefficients, variables, and constants consistently.
- Provide step-by-step solution templates.
- Allow extra time for arithmetic operations and verification.
- Use manipulatives such as algebra tiles for forming equations.

## For Students with Visual Impairments

- Provide large-print or tactile graph sheets.
- Use raised-line drawings for coordinate axes.
- Read equations and graphs aloud using clear spatial language.

## For Students with Hearing Impairments

- Provide written instructions and visual cues.
- Use captioned videos for graphing demonstrations.
- Encourage peer note-sharing.

## For Slow Learners

- Break word problems into smaller steps.
- Focus on one method at a time before introducing others.
- Provide additional practice with immediate feedback.

## For Gifted Students

- Offer enrichment tasks such as deriving the cross-multiplication formula.
- Ask students to create case studies based on real-life data.
- Assign project-based learning on linear programming applications.
