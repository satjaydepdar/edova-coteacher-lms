# Teacher Notes: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

## Lesson Flow Suggestion

### Day 1: Introduction and Formation of Equations (Section 3.1)
- Revise linear equations in two variables from Class IX.
- Form pairs of equations from real-life situations.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 3.1 word problems.

### Day 2: Graphical Method (Section 3.2)
- Plot lines and identify intersecting, parallel, and coincident cases.
- Introduce the coefficient ratio test.
- Work through NCERT Examples 1, 2, and 3.

### Day 3: Substitution Method (Section 3.3.1)
- Explain the steps with emphasis on sign handling.
- Work through NCERT Examples 4, 5, 6, and 7.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 3.3.

### Day 4: Elimination Method (Section 3.3.2)
- Demonstrate making coefficients equal.
- Work through NCERT Examples 8, 9, and 10.
- Assign remaining Exercise 3.3 problems.

### Day 5: Cross-Multiplication Method (Section 3.4)
- Derive or present the formula.
- Practice examples with fractional coefficients.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 3.4.

### Day 6: Equations Reducible to Linear Form (Section 3.5)
- Introduce substitution techniques.
- Work through reducible equation examples.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 3.5.

### Day 7: Summary and Mixed Practice
- Recap all methods and consistency tests.
- Mixed board-style questions and case studies.

## Teaching Tips

- Encourage students to verify solutions in both equations.
- Use graphing software to visualise parallel, intersecting, and coincident lines.
- Emphasise careful handling of signs when transposing terms.
- Provide a decision-making flowchart for choosing the appropriate method.
