# Art Integration

## Activity 1: Graph Art Gallery

Students create large, colourful graphs showing the three types of solution cases and display them in the classroom.

## Activity 2: Equation Storybook

Students write and illustrate short stories that lead to a pair of linear equations. Each story includes the equations and their solution.

## Activity 3: Method Poster

Design a poster for one algebraic method (substitution, elimination, or cross-multiplication) with a worked example and a step-by-step flowchart.

## Activity 4: Real-Life Diorama

Create a small diorama of a market, taxi stand, or library. Label the variables and write a pair of equations based on the scene.
