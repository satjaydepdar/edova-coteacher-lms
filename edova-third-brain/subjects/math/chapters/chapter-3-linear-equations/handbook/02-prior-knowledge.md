## Required Concepts from Previous Classes

### 1. Linear Equations in One Variable (Class VIII)
- Standard form: $ax + b = 0$, where $a \neq 0$.
- Solving by transposing terms and isolating the variable.

### 2. Linear Equations in Two Variables (Class IX)
- Standard form: $ax + by + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$.
- A single linear equation in two variables has infinitely many solutions.
- Plotting solutions on a coordinate plane gives a straight line.

### 3. Coordinate Geometry Basics (Class IX)
- Cartesian plane, $x$-axis, $y$-axis, origin, and quadrants.
- Plotting points $(x, y)$ and drawing a line through two points.

### 4. Ratio and Proportion
- Simplifying ratios and comparing two ratios.
- Cross-multiplication: if $\dfrac{a}{b} = \dfrac{c}{d}$, then $ad = bc$.

### 5. Algebraic Manipulations
- Expanding brackets, collecting like terms, and transposition.
- Solving simple simultaneous equations by inspection.

## Quick Diagnostic Checks

1. Solve $2x + 5 = 17$.
2. Write any two solutions of $x + y = 7$.
3. Plot the points $(0, 3)$ and $(2, 0)$ and draw the line passing through them.
4. If $\dfrac{3}{x} = \dfrac{9}{4}$, find $x$.
5. Simplify $2(3x - 4y) + 5(x + 2y)$.

## Common Gaps to Address

- Confusing a linear equation in one variable with a pair of linear equations in two variables.
- Incorrect plotting of points due to sign errors.
- Difficulty in transposing terms correctly across the equals sign.
- Weakness in forming equations from word problems.
