## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Linear Equation in Two Variables** | An equation of the form $ax + by + c = 0$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are real numbers and $a \neq 0$, $b \neq 0$. |
| **Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables** | Two linear equations in the same two variables $x$ and $y$, usually written as $a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$ and $a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$. |
| **Consistent Pair** | A pair of linear equations that has at least one solution. |
| **Inconsistent Pair** | A pair of linear equations that has no solution; graphically represented by parallel lines. |
| **Dependent Pair** | A pair of linear equations that has infinitely many solutions; graphically represented by coincident lines. |
| **Unique Solution** | Exactly one solution; graphically represented by two intersecting lines. |
| **Graphical Method** | Solving a pair of equations by drawing their graphs and finding the point of intersection. |
| **Substitution Method** | An algebraic method in which one variable is expressed in terms of the other and substituted in the second equation. |
| **Elimination Method** | An algebraic method in which one variable is eliminated by making its coefficients equal and adding/subtracting the equations. |
| **Cross-Multiplication Method** | An algebraic method using the formula $\dfrac{x}{b_1c_2 - b_2c_1} = \dfrac{y}{c_1a_2 - c_2a_1} = \dfrac{1}{a_1b_2 - a_2b_1}$. |
| **Coefficient Ratio Test** | Comparing $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}$, $\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$, and $\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$ to determine consistency. |
