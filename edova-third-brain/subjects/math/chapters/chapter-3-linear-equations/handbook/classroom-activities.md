# Classroom Activities

## Activity 1: Real-Life Equation Pairs

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Give students situations such as buying pens and pencils, ages of two people, or taxi fares. Each pair writes two linear equations and explains what $x$ and $y$ represent.

**Sample Situations:**
- 5 pencils and 7 pens cost ₹50; 7 pencils and 5 pens cost ₹46.
- The sum of two numbers is 14 and their difference is 4.
- A taxi charges a fixed amount plus a charge per km.

---

## Activity 2: Graphical Solution Match-Up

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4 | **Materials:** Graph paper, ruler

Each group graphs a different pair of equations, identifies the nature of the lines, and finds the solution if it exists. Groups then swap graphs and verify each other's work.

---

## Activity 3: Method Choice Relay

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams of 4

Prepare cards with pairs of equations. Teams decide whether substitution, elimination, or cross-multiplication is most efficient and solve one card per method.

---

## Activity 4: Consistency Detective

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Students are given several pairs of equations. Without fully solving, they use the coefficient ratio test to classify each pair as consistent (unique), inconsistent, or dependent.
