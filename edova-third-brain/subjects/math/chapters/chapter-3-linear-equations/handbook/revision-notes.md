# Revision Notes: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

## 1. General Form

A pair of linear equations in two variables:

$$a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$$
$$a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$$

## 2. Graphical Interpretation

- **Intersecting lines** $\rightarrow$ unique solution $\rightarrow$ consistent.
- **Parallel lines** $\rightarrow$ no solution $\rightarrow$ inconsistent.
- **Coincident lines** $\rightarrow$ infinitely many solutions $\rightarrow$ dependent/consistent.

## 3. Algebraic Consistency Test

Compare the ratios $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}$, $\dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$, and $\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$:

- Intersecting: $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$
- Parallel: $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$
- Coincident: $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} = \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$

## 4. Methods of Solution

| Method | Best Used When |
|---|---|
| Substitution | One variable can be easily isolated. |
| Elimination | Coefficients can be made equal by simple multiplication. |
| Cross-Multiplication | Coefficients are messy or fractions are involved. |

## 5. Common Diagram Patterns

- Lines with different slopes $\rightarrow$ intersect at one point.
- Lines with same slope but different intercepts $\rightarrow$ parallel.
- Lines with same slope and same intercept $\rightarrow$ coincident.

## 6. Word Problem Strategy

1. Identify the two unknowns and denote them by $x$ and $y$.
2. Form two equations using the given conditions.
3. Solve using any algebraic method.
4. Verify the solution in both equations.

## 7. Important Tips for Board Exam

- Always verify the final answer by substituting in both original equations.
- Write the general form before applying the consistency test.
- For case studies, clearly define variables and state the equations formed.
- Be careful with sign changes during transposition.
