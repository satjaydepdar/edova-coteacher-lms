# Introduction

## What is a Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables?

You already know that a linear equation in two variables $x$ and $y$ can be written as:

$$ax + by + c = 0$$

where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are real numbers, and $a \neq 0$, $b \neq 0$.

When two such equations are considered together, they form a **pair of linear equations in two variables**. The general form is:

$$a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$$
$$a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$$

## Real-Life Example

Akhila went to a fair. She took rides on a Giant Wheel and played Hoopla. The number of times she played Hoopla was half the number of rides she had. If each ride cost ₹3 and each Hoopla game cost ₹4, and she spent ₹20 in total, how many rides and how many Hoopla games did she have?

Let $x$ = number of rides and $y$ = number of Hoopla games. Then:

$$y = \frac{1}{2}x \quad \text{or} \quad x - 2y = 0$$
$$3x + 4y = 20$$

This is a pair of linear equations in two variables.

## Solution of a Pair

A solution is a pair of values $(x, y)$ that satisfies **both** equations simultaneously. In this chapter, you will learn to find such solutions using graphical and algebraic methods.
