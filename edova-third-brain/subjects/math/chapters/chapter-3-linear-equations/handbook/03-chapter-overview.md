# Chapter 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

## What Students Will Learn

This chapter extends students' knowledge of linear equations to a pair of linear equations in two variables. Students will:

1. Represent real-life situations as a pair of linear equations.
2. Solve a pair of linear equations graphically and interpret the nature of solutions.
3. Solve pairs of linear equations algebraically using substitution, elimination, and cross-multiplication methods.
4. Determine consistency by comparing the ratios of coefficients.
5. Reduce non-linear equations to a pair of linear equations and solve them.
6. Apply these methods to solve word problems from daily life.

## Key Examples from the Textbook

- Example 1: Check graphically whether $x + 3y = 6$ and $2x - 3y = 12$ are consistent.
- Example 2: Determine graphically whether $5x - 8y + 1 = 0$ and $3x - \dfrac{24}{5}y + \dfrac{3}{5} = 0$ have infinitely many solutions.
- Example 3: Word problem on pants and skirts solved graphically.
- Example 4: Solve $7x - 15y = 2$ and $x + 2y = 3$ by substitution.
- Example 5: Age problem solved by substitution.
- Example 6: Show that $2x + 3y = 9$ and $4x + 6y = 18$ have infinitely many solutions.
- Example 7: Show that $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and $2x + 4y - 12 = 0$ are inconsistent.
- Example 8: Income-expenditure problem solved by elimination.
- Example 9: Show inconsistency using elimination.
- Example 10: Two-digit number problem solved by elimination.

## Competency Focus

- **Problem Solving:** Translate word problems into algebraic equations.
- **Reasoning:** Predict the nature of solutions from coefficient ratios.
- **Communication:** Present solutions step-by-step and verify results.
