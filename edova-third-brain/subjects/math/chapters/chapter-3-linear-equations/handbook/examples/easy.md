# Easy Worked Examples

## Example 1: Check Consistency Using Ratios

Check whether the following pair is consistent:

$$5x - 4y + 8 = 0$$
$$7x + 6y - 9 = 0$$

**Solution:**

Here $a_1 = 5$, $b_1 = -4$, $c_1 = 8$, $a_2 = 7$, $b_2 = 6$, $c_2 = -9$.

$$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{5}{7}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{-4}{6} = -\frac{2}{3}$$

Since $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \dfrac{b_1}{b_2}$, the pair is consistent with a unique solution.

## Example 2: Solve by Substitution

Solve:

$$x + y = 14 \quad \text{...(1)}$$
$$x - y = 4 \quad \text{...(2)}$$

**Solution:**

From equation (1): $x = 14 - y$.

Substitute in equation (2):

$$(14 - y) - y = 4$$
$$14 - 2y = 4$$
$$2y = 10$$
$$y = 5$$

Substitute back:

$$x = 14 - 5 = 9$$

The solution is $x = 9$, $y = 5$.

## Example 3: Solve by Elimination

Solve:

$$x + y = 5 \quad \text{...(1)}$$
$$2x - 3y = 4 \quad \text{...(2)}$$

**Solution:**

Multiply equation (1) by 3:

$$3x + 3y = 15 \quad \text{...(3)}$$

Add equations (2) and (3):

$$5x = 19$$
$$x = \frac{19}{5}$$

Substitute in equation (1):

$$\frac{19}{5} + y = 5$$
$$y = 5 - \frac{19}{5} = \frac{6}{5}$$

The solution is $x = \dfrac{19}{5}$, $y = \dfrac{6}{5}$.
