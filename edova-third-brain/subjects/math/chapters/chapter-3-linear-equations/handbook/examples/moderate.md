# Moderate Worked Examples

## Example 4: Age Problem

Aftab tells his daughter, "Seven years ago, I was seven times as old as you were then. Also, three years from now, I shall be three times as old as you will be." Find their present ages.

**Solution:**

Let Aftab's present age be $s$ years and his daughter's present age be $t$ years.

Seven years ago:

$$s - 7 = 7(t - 7)$$
$$s - 7t + 42 = 0 \quad \text{...(1)}$$

Three years hence:

$$s + 3 = 3(t + 3)$$
$$s - 3t = 6 \quad \text{...(2)}$$

From equation (2): $s = 3t + 6$.

Substitute in equation (1):

$$(3t + 6) - 7t + 42 = 0$$
$$-4t + 48 = 0$$
$$t = 12$$

Therefore $s = 3(12) + 6 = 42$.

Aftab is 42 years old and his daughter is 12 years old.

## Example 5: Infinitely Many Solutions

Show that $2x + 3y = 9$ and $4x + 6y = 18$ have infinitely many solutions.

**Solution:**

Divide the second equation by 2:

$$2x + 3y = 9$$

which is identical to the first equation.

Alternatively:

$$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{-9}{-18} = \frac{1}{2}$$

Since all three ratios are equal, the equations are dependent and have infinitely many solutions.

## Example 6: Inconsistent Pair

Show that the pair $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and $2x + 4y - 12 = 0$ has no solution.

**Solution:**

$$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{c_1}{c_2} = \frac{-4}{-12} = \frac{1}{3}$$

Since $\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$, the pair is inconsistent and has no solution.
