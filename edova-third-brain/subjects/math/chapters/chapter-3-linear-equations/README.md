# Chapter 3 – Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

## Overview
This directory contains curated resources for teaching and learning **Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables** in CBSE Class 10 Mathematics.

## Contents
- `ncert/` – NCERT textbook content and exercises
- `handbook/` – Teacher handbook with notes, strategies, and reflections
- `lesson-plans/` – Ready-to-use lesson plans
- `worksheets/` – Practice worksheets (basic, standard, HOTS, case study)
- `assessments/` – Formative, summative, competency-based assessments and rubrics
- `question-bank/` – Categorized question bank
- `activities/` – Classroom, experiential, art, and ICT integration activities
- `assets/` – Diagrams, illustrations, graphs, and classroom images
- `exports/` – Generated PDF, DOCX, and PPTX outputs
