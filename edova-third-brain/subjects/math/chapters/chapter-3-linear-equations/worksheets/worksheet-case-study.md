## Case Study 1: Akhila at the Fair (NCERT Section 3.1)

Akhila went to a fair. She wanted to enjoy rides on the Giant Wheel and play Hoopla. The number of times she played Hoopla is half the number of rides she had on the Giant Wheel. Each ride costs ₹3 and a game of Hoopla costs ₹4. She spent ₹20 in total.

**Q1.** If $x$ is the number of rides and $y$ is the number of Hoopla games, write the two linear equations that represent the situation.

**Q2.** Solve the pair of equations graphically or algebraically to find the number of rides and the number of Hoopla games.

**Q3.** Verify that your answer satisfies both conditions of the problem.

---

## Case Study 2: The Lending Library (NCERT Exercise 3.3)

A lending library has a fixed charge for the first three days and an additional charge for each day thereafter. Saritha paid ₹27 for a book kept for seven days, while Susy paid ₹21 for the book she kept for five days.

**Q4.** Define the variables and form the pair of linear equations for the fixed charge and the extra day charge.

**Q5.** Find the fixed charge and the charge for each extra day.

**Q6.** How much would a person have to pay for keeping the book for 10 days?

**Case Study 1**

**A1.**

$$y = \dfrac{1}{2}x \Rightarrow x - 2y = 0$$
$$3x + 4y = 20$$

**A2.** Substitute $x = 2y$ into the second equation:

$$3(2y) + 4y = 20 \Rightarrow 10y = 20 \Rightarrow y = 2$$

Then $x = 4$. Akhila had **4 rides** and played **2 Hoopla games**.

**A3.** Check: $2 = \dfrac{1}{2}(4)$ ✓ and $3(4) + 4(2) = 12 + 8 = 20$ ✓.

---

**Case Study 2**

**A4.** Let the fixed charge for the first three days be ₹$x$ and the extra charge per day be ₹$y$.

Saritha kept the book for 7 days, so 4 extra days:

$$x + 4y = 27$$

Susy kept the book for 5 days, so 2 extra days:

$$x + 2y = 21$$

**A5.** Subtracting the second equation from the first:

$$2y = 6 \Rightarrow y = 3$$

Substituting in $x + 2y = 21$: $x + 6 = 21 \Rightarrow x = 15$. The fixed charge is **₹15** and the extra day charge is **₹3**.

**A6.** For 10 days, there are 7 extra days. Total charge = $15 + 7 \times 3 = ₹36$.
