## Grading Rubric for Worksheets

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 90–100% | Excellent; all equations formed and solved correctly; clear working and verification |
| 75–89% | Good; minor algebraic or sign errors; understands methods |
| 60–74% | Satisfactory; understands concepts but needs practice in forming equations and choosing methods |
| Below 60% | Needs remediation; review graphical interpretation, substitution, and elimination methods |
