**Q2.** Let length be $x$ m and width be $y$ m.

$$x = y + 4 \Rightarrow x - y = 4$$
$$x + y = 36$$

Adding: $2x = 40 \Rightarrow x = 20$. Then $y = 16$. The dimensions are **20 m × 16 m**.

**Q3.** From the first equation, $s = t + 3$. Substituting in the second equation:

$$\dfrac{t + 3}{3} + \dfrac{t}{2} = 6$$

Multiply by 6: $2(t + 3) + 3t = 36 \Rightarrow 5t + 6 = 36 \Rightarrow t = 6$. Then $s = 9$.

So $s = 9,\ t = 6$.

**Q4.** Let the angles be $x$ and $y$ with $x > y$.

$$x + y = 180°$$
$$x - y = 18°$$

Adding: $2x = 198° \Rightarrow x = 99°$. Then $y = 81°$. The angles are **99° and 81°**.

**Q5.** Multiply the second equation by 2:

$$4x - 4y = 4$$

Adding to the first equation: $7x = 14 \Rightarrow x = 2$. Substituting in $3x + 4y = 10$: $6 + 4y = 10 \Rightarrow y = 1$.

So $x = 2,\ y = 1$.

**Q6.** Let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$.

$$\dfrac{x + 1}{y - 1} = \dfrac{1}{2} \Rightarrow 2x + 2 = y - 1 \Rightarrow 2x - y = -3$$
$$\dfrac{x}{y + 1} = \dfrac{1}{3} \Rightarrow 3x = y + 1 \Rightarrow 3x - y = 1$$

Subtracting the first equation from the second: $x = 4$. Then $y = 11$. The fraction is $\dfrac{4}{11}$.

**Q7.** From the second equation, $x = 3 - 2y$. Substituting in the first equation:

$$7(3 - 2y) - 15y = 2 \Rightarrow 21 - 14y - 15y = 2 \Rightarrow -29y = -19 \Rightarrow y = \dfrac{19}{29}$$

Then $x = 3 - 2\left(\dfrac{19}{29}\right) = \dfrac{87 - 38}{29} = \dfrac{49}{29}$.

So $x = \dfrac{49}{29},\ y = \dfrac{19}{29}$.

**Q8.** Let the fixed charge be ₹$x$ and the charge per km be ₹$y$.

$$x + 10y = 105$$
$$x + 15y = 155$$

Subtracting: $5y = 50 \Rightarrow y = 10$. Then $x = 5$. The fixed charge is **₹5** and the charge per km is **₹10**.

For 25 km: $5 + 25 \times 10 = ₹255$.
