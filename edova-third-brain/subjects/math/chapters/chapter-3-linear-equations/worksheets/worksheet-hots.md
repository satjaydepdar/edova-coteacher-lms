## Section A: Ratio and Consistency Reasoning

**Q1.** For what value of $k$ will the following pair of linear equations have infinitely many solutions?

$$2x + 3y = 7$$
$$(k - 1)x + (k + 2)y = 3k$$

**Q2.** Find the value of $p$ for which the pair of equations

$$3x + y = 1$$
$$(2p - 1)x + (p - 1)y = 2p + 1$$

has no solution.

---

## Section B: Multi-Step Word Problems

**Q3.** A fraction becomes $\dfrac{9}{11}$ if 2 is added to both the numerator and the denominator. If 3 is added to both the numerator and the denominator, it becomes $\dfrac{5}{6}$. Find the fraction.

**Q4.** Five years hence, the age of Jacob will be three times that of his son. Five years ago, Jacob's age was seven times that of his son. What are their present ages?

---

## Section C: Mixed Algebraic Methods

**Q5.** The ratio of incomes of two persons is $9 : 7$ and the ratio of their expenditures is $4 : 3$. If each of them manages to save ₹2000 per month, find their monthly incomes.

**Q6.** Solve the following pair of equations by any suitable algebraic method:

$$\dfrac{3x}{2} - \dfrac{5y}{3} = -2$$
$$\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{y}{2} = \dfrac{13}{6}$$

**Q1.** For infinitely many solutions:

$$\dfrac{2}{k - 1} = \dfrac{3}{k + 2} = \dfrac{7}{3k}$$

From $\dfrac{2}{k - 1} = \dfrac{3}{k + 2}$:

$$2(k + 2) = 3(k - 1) \Rightarrow 2k + 4 = 3k - 3 \Rightarrow k = 7$$

Verify with $\dfrac{3}{k + 2} = \dfrac{7}{3k}$: $\dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{7}{21} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. So $k = 7$.

**Q2.** For no solution:

$$\dfrac{3}{2p - 1} = \dfrac{1}{p - 1} \neq \dfrac{1}{2p + 1}$$

From $\dfrac{3}{2p - 1} = \dfrac{1}{p - 1}$:

$$3(p - 1) = 2p - 1 \Rightarrow 3p - 3 = 2p - 1 \Rightarrow p = 2$$

Verify: $\dfrac{3}{3} = 1$, $\dfrac{1}{1} = 1$, but $\dfrac{1}{5} \neq 1$. So $p = 2$.

**Q3.** Let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$.

$$\dfrac{x + 2}{y + 2} = \dfrac{9}{11} \Rightarrow 11x - 9y = -4$$
$$\dfrac{x + 3}{y + 3} = \dfrac{5}{6} \Rightarrow 6x - 5y = -3$$

Solving: From the first equation, $x = \dfrac{9y - 4}{11}$. Substituting:

$$6\left(\dfrac{9y - 4}{11}\right) - 5y = -3 \Rightarrow 54y - 24 - 55y = -33 \Rightarrow y = 9$$

Then $x = \dfrac{81 - 4}{11} = 7$. The fraction is $\dfrac{7}{9}$.

**Q4.** Let Jacob's present age be $x$ years and his son's present age be $y$ years.

$$x + 5 = 3(y + 5) \Rightarrow x - 3y = 10$$
$$x - 5 = 7(y - 5) \Rightarrow x - 7y = -30$$

Subtracting: $4y = 40 \Rightarrow y = 10$. Then $x = 40$. Their present ages are **40 years and 10 years**.

**Q5.** Let the incomes be ₹$9x$ and ₹$7x$, and the expenditures be ₹$4y$ and ₹$3y$.

$$9x - 4y = 2000$$
$$7x - 3y = 2000$$

Multiply the first equation by 3 and the second by 4:

$$27x - 12y = 6000$$
$$28x - 12y = 8000$$

Subtracting: $x = 2000$. Then $y = 4000$. The monthly incomes are **₹18,000 and ₹14,000**.

**Q6.** Multiply the first equation by 6 and the second by 6 to clear denominators:

$$9x - 10y = -12$$
$$2x + 3y = 13$$

Multiply the first by 3 and the second by 10:

$$27x - 30y = -36$$
$$20x + 30y = 130$$

Adding: $47x = 94 \Rightarrow x = 2$. Substituting in $2x + 3y = 13$: $4 + 3y = 13 \Rightarrow y = 3$.

So $x = 2,\ y = 3$.
