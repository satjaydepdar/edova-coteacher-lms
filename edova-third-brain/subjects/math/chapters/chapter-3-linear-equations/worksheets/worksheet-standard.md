**Chapter 3 – Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables**
**Class:** X  |  **Subject:** Mathematics  |  **Time:** 25 minutes

## Section A: Graphical Method (NCERT Exercise 3.1)

**Q1.** 10 students of Class X took part in a Mathematics quiz. If the number of girls is 4 more than the number of boys, find the number of boys and girls who took part in the quiz. (Form the pair of linear equations and solve graphically.)

**Q2.** Given the linear equation $2x + 3y - 8 = 0$, write another linear equation in two variables such that the geometrical representation of the pair so formed is:

(i) intersecting lines

(ii) parallel lines

(iii) coincident lines

## Section B: Substitution Method (NCERT Exercise 3.2)

**Q3.** Solve the following pair of equations by the substitution method:

$$3x - y = 3$$
$$9x - 3y = 9$$

**Q4.** The difference between two numbers is 26 and one number is three times the other. Find them.

---

## Section C: Elimination Method (NCERT Exercise 3.3)

**Q5.** Solve the following pair of equations by the elimination method:

$$x + y = 5$$
$$2x - 3y = 4$$

**Q6.** The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. Also, nine times this number is twice the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. Find the number.

---

## Section D: Application

**Q7.** 5 pencils and 7 pens together cost ₹50, whereas 7 pencils and 5 pens together cost ₹46. Find the cost of one pencil and that of one pen.

<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** Let the number of boys be $x$ and the number of girls be $y$.

$$x + y = 10$$
$$y = x + 4 \Rightarrow x - y = -4$$

Solving gives $x = 3$ and $y = 7$. So there are **3 boys** and **7 girls**.

**Q2.**
(i) Any equation with $\dfrac{2}{a} \neq \dfrac{3}{b}$, e.g., $3x + 2y - 6 = 0$.

(ii) Any equation with $\dfrac{2}{a} = \dfrac{3}{b} \neq \dfrac{-8}{c}$, e.g., $4x + 6y - 10 = 0$.

(iii) Any equation with $\dfrac{2}{a} = \dfrac{3}{b} = \dfrac{-8}{c}$, e.g., $4x + 6y - 16 = 0$.

**Q3.** From the first equation, $y = 3x - 3$. Substituting in the second equation:

$$9x - 3(3x - 3) = 9 \Rightarrow 9x - 9x + 9 = 9 \Rightarrow 9 = 9$$

This is true for all values of $x$. Hence the pair has **infinitely many solutions**.

**Q4.** Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$ with $x > y$.

$$x - y = 26$$
$$x = 3y$$

Substituting: $3y - y = 26 \Rightarrow y = 13$. Then $x = 39$. The numbers are **13 and 39**.

**Q5.** Multiply the first equation by 3:

$$3x + 3y = 15$$

Adding to the second equation: $5x = 19 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{19}{5}$.

Substituting in $x + y = 5$: $y = 5 - \dfrac{19}{5} = \dfrac{6}{5}$.

So $x = \dfrac{19}{5},\ y = \dfrac{6}{5}$.

**Q6.** Let the ten's and unit's digits be $x$ and $y$ respectively.

$$x + y = 9$$
$$9(10x + y) = 2(10y + x) \Rightarrow 88x = 11y \Rightarrow 8x = y$$

Substituting in the first equation: $x + 8x = 9 \Rightarrow x = 1$, so $y = 8$. The number is **18**.

**Q7.** Let the cost of one pencil be ₹$x$ and one pen be ₹$y$.

$$5x + 7y = 50$$
$$7x + 5y = 46$$

Adding: $12x + 12y = 96 \Rightarrow x + y = 8$.

Subtracting: $-2x + 2y = 4 \Rightarrow y - x = 2$.

Solving gives $x = 3$ and $y = 5$. So one pencil costs **₹3** and one pen costs **₹5**.
