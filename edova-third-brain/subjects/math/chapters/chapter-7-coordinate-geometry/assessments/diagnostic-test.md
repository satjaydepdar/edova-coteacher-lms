# Diagnostic Test

## Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry
### Topics Covered: Cartesian Plane, Plotting Points, Reflection

**Learning Objectives:**
- Recall the names of the coordinate axes and the origin.
- Identify the quadrant of a given point.
- Plot points on the Cartesian plane.
- Find the reflection of a point in the x-axis, y-axis, and origin.

**Duration / Class Flow:**
- **Intro (5 mins):** Explain that this is a pre-test on basic coordinate geometry ideas.
- **Teaching/Test (15 mins):** Students solve the 5 problems below independently.
- **Practice/Review (10 mins):** Discuss common sign errors in reflection.
- **Wrap-up (5 mins):** Clarify quadrant rules.

---

### Assessment Problems

**Q1.** Write the coordinates of the origin.

**Q2.** In which quadrant does the point (−3, 4) lie?

**Q3.** Plot the points A(2, 3), B(−1, 4), C(−2, −3), and D(3, −2) on a graph.

**Q4.** Write the reflection of the point (5, −7) in:
  (a) the x-axis
  (b) the y-axis
  (c) the origin

**Q5.** State whether the following are true or false:
  (a) The abscissa of a point is its y-coordinate.
  (b) All points on the x-axis have y-coordinate 0.
  (c) The point (−4, 0) lies in the second quadrant.
