# Summative Assessment

## Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry
### Topics Covered: Full Chapter Synthesis

**Learning Objectives:**
- Synthesize all concepts of coordinate geometry: plotting, reflection, distance, midpoint, and section formula.
- Evaluate comprehensive understanding through varied question formats.

**Duration / Class Flow:**
- **Intro (5 mins):** Read instructions. Remind students to write formulas clearly.
- **Test (60 mins):** Students independently solve all 15 problems.
- **Wrap-up (N/A):** Collect for formal grading.

---

### Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 mark each)

**Q1.** The distance between (0, 0) and (3, 4) is:
(a) 5  (b) 7  (c) 25  (d) 1

**Q2.** The midpoint of the segment joining (2, 3) and (6, 9) is:
(a) (4, 6)  (b) (8, 12)  (c) (3, 3)  (d) (2, 3)

**Q3.** The reflection of (4, −5) in the x-axis is:
(a) (−4, 5)  (b) (4, 5)  (c) (−4, −5)  (d) (5, 4)

**Q4.** A point on the x-axis has coordinates:
(a) (x, y)  (b) (0, y)  (c) (x, 0)  (d) (−x, 0)

---

### Section B: Very Short Answer Questions (2 marks each)

**Q5.** In which quadrant does (−2, −3) lie?

**Q6.** Find the distance between (a, 0) and (0, b).

**Q7.** Find the coordinates of the point that divides the segment joining (2, 4) and (6, 8) in the ratio 1:1.

**Q8.** Write the reflection of (−3, 7) in the origin.

---

### Section C: Short Answer Questions (3 marks each)

**Q9.** Find the point on the x-axis equidistant from (2, −5) and (−2, 9).

**Q10.** Show that the points A(1, 2), B(4, 5), and C(7, 8) are collinear.

**Q11.** Find the ratio in which the point (2, 1) divides the segment joining (−1, 7) and (5, −2).

**Q12.** The vertices of a triangle are A(0, 0), B(5, 0), and C(0, 12). Find the length of the median from A to BC.

---

### Section D: Long Answer Questions (4 marks each)

**Q13.** Find the coordinates of the points of trisection of the line segment joining (4, −1) and (−2, −3).

**Q14.** Prove that the points (3, 0), (6, 4), and (−1, 3) are the vertices of a right-angled triangle.

**Q15.** A(2, 3), B(4, k), and C(6, −3) are collinear. Find the value of k.
