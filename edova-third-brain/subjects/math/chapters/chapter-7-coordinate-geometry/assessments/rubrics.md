# Assessment Rubrics

## Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry
### Topics Covered: Distance Formula, Midpoint Formula, Section Formula

**Learning Objectives:**
- Provide teachers with a standardized grading tool for coordinate geometry problems.
- Differentiate between levels of mathematical reasoning and accuracy.

**Duration / Class Flow:**
- **Intro (5 mins):** Distribute the rubric so students understand grading criteria.
- **Teaching/Practice (30 mins):** Assign exemplar problems and have students attempt them.
- **Wrap-up (10 mins):** Peer-grade using the rubrics to build self-assessment skills.

---

### Rubric 1: Formula Application (Distance / Midpoint / Section)

| Criteria | Excellent (4 marks) | Proficient (3 marks) | Basic (2 marks) | Below Basic (1 mark) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formula Selection** | Correctly identifies and writes the relevant formula. | Writes the correct formula but with minor notation errors. | Selects a formula but it is not appropriate for the problem. | Does not write any formula. |
| **Substitution** | Substitutes all values correctly into the formula. | Substitutes correctly but misses one value. | Substitutes some values incorrectly. | Does not substitute values. |
| **Execution** | Solves accurately with correct units and final answer. | Makes a minor arithmetic error but method is correct. | Makes multiple arithmetic errors. | Cannot complete the calculation. |

---

### Rubric 2: Geometric Reasoning (Collinearity, Figure Properties)

| Criteria | Excellent (4 marks) | Proficient (3 marks) | Basic (2 marks) | Below Basic (1 mark) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Approach** | Chooses the most efficient method (e.g., distance check for collinearity). | Chooses a valid method but not the most efficient. | Chooses a partially correct method. | No clear approach. |
| **Logical Flow** | Steps are clear, sequential, and lead to a valid conclusion. | Mostly clear with one minor gap. | Steps are out of order or incomplete. | No logical sequence. |
| **Conclusion** | States a clear, justified conclusion. | States a conclusion with weak justification. | States an incorrect or unsupported conclusion. | No conclusion. |

---

### Exemplar Assessment Problems

**Exemplar 1 (Use Rubric 1):**
Find the distance between the points (−3, 4) and (5, −2).

**Exemplar 2 (Use Rubric 2):**
Show that the points A(1, 2), B(3, 4), and C(5, 6) are collinear.
