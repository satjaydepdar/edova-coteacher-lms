# Formative Assessment

## Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry
### Topics Covered: Distance Formula, Midpoint Formula, Section Formula

**Learning Objectives:**
- Apply the distance formula to find the distance between two points.
- Use the midpoint formula to find the midpoint of a line segment.
- Apply the section formula for internal division in a given ratio.

**Duration / Class Flow:**
- **Intro (5 mins):** Recap the formulas for distance, midpoint, and section.
- **Teaching (15 mins):** Work through one example of each formula on the board.
- **Practice (20 mins):** Students solve the problems below individually or in pairs.
- **Wrap-up (5 mins):** Take up Q3 and Q6 to address common errors.

---

### Assessment Problems

**Q1.** Find the distance between the points (1, 2) and (4, 6).

**Q2.** Find the midpoint of the segment joining (−2, 5) and (4, −1).

**Q3.** Find the point that divides the segment joining (1, 3) and (5, 7) internally in the ratio 2:1.

**Q4.** Find the distance of the point (−8, 15) from the origin.

**Q5.** Determine whether the points A(1, 1), B(4, 5), and C(7, 9) are collinear.

**Q6.** Find the coordinates of a point on the y-axis equidistant from (3, 2) and (−1, 4).
