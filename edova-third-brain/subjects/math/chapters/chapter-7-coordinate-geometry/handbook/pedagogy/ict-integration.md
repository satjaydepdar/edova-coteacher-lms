## GeoGebra activities

### Activity 1: Discovering the distance formula
- Plot two movable points A and B
- Draw the right triangle with perpendiculars to axes
- Display the side lengths and the calculated distance
- Students drag points and observe that Pythagoras always holds
- Lead to the algebraic formula

### Activity 2: Section formula explorer
- Plot A and B with a slider for the ratio m:n
- Point P moves along AB as the slider changes
- Students set specific ratios and verify the coordinates match the formula

### Activity 3: Collinearity checker
- Plot three points
- Show distances AB, BC, AC
- Students adjust points until AB + BC = AC (collinear)
- Then move one point off the line and see the inequality

### Activity 4: Quadrilateral builder
- Plot 4 points and calculate all sides + diagonals in real-time
- Students try to create a square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram
- The tool highlights which properties are satisfied

## Desmos activities

- Desmos has built-in coordinate geometry activities
- Use the "Geometry" tool for interactive exploration
- Create custom Card Sorts for matching problems to formulas

## Spreadsheet activity

- Create a spreadsheet where students enter x₁, y₁, x₂, y₂
- Formulas automatically calculate distance, midpoint, and section point for a given ratio
- Students can verify their manual calculations instantly

## Caution

Technology should support understanding, not replace it. Students should be able to solve problems by hand before relying on tools. Use tech for discovery and verification, not for doing the work.
