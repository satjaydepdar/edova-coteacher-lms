## Activity 1: Coordinate Art

Students create a picture by plotting points and connecting them in sequence — exactly like the textbook's aeroplane activity (Section 7.1 opening).

**Task:** Design your own coordinate art figure using at least 20 points. Write the coordinate list and the connection order. Calculate at least 3 distances between key points in your design.

**Math focus:** Plotting accuracy, distance calculation.

## Activity 2: Geometric Pattern Design

Students use the distance formula to create patterns with specific properties.

**Task:** Design a pattern where:
- All points are equidistant from a centre point (circle of points)
- Consecutive points are at a fixed distance from each other (regular polygon)
- Verify both conditions using the distance formula

**Math focus:** Equidistant conditions, geometric verification.

## Activity 3: Perspective and Coordinate Geometry

Connect to art perspective: parallel lines in a painting converge at a vanishing point.

**Task:** Given a "vanishing point" V(a, b) and two parallel "lines" represented by pairs of points, verify using coordinate geometry that lines through corresponding points pass through (or near) V.

**Math focus:** Collinearity, distance as a check.

## Activity 4: Rangoli with Coordinates

Design a rangoli pattern on a coordinate grid where the design has specific geometric properties (rotational symmetry, reflection symmetry) that can be verified using coordinates and distances.

**Math focus:** Midpoint formula (for symmetry axes), distance formula (for equal lengths).

## Assessment

Grade the art for creativity and effort. Grade the math for correct application of formulas and accurate calculations. Keep the two scores separate but both required.
