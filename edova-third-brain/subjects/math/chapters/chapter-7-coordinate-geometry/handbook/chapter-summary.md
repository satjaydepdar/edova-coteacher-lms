## What we learnt

1. **Distance Formula:** The distance between P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂) is √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]. This is derived by constructing a right triangle and applying Pythagoras' theorem. The distance from the origin is √(x² + y²).

2. **Applications of Distance Formula:**
   - Verifying collinearity: AB + BC = AC
   - Identifying triangles (equilateral, isosceles, right, scalene)
   - Identifying quadrilaterals (square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram)
   - Finding points equidistant from two given points
   - Finding points on axes satisfying distance conditions

3. **Section Formula:** If P divides AB internally in ratio m₁:m₂, then P = ((m₁x₂+m₂x₁)/(m₁+m₂), (m₁y₂+m₂y₁)/(m₁+m₂)). Derived using AA similarity of triangles. Remember: m₁ pairs with the second point's coordinates.

4. **Applications of Section Formula:**
   - Finding the point dividing a segment in a given ratio
   - Finding the ratio when the dividing point is given (use k:1 form)
   - Trisection of segments (ratios 1:2 and 2:1)
   - Division by axes (set x=0 or y=0 and solve)
   - Parallelogram properties (diagonals bisect each other)

5. **Midpoint Formula:** M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2). A special case of the section formula with ratio 1:1. Used for parallelogram diagonals, circle centres, and dividing segments into equal parts.
