## After teaching this chapter, reflect on:

### Content delivery
1. Which derivation went smoother — distance or section formula? Why?
2. Did the build-up-from-cases approach work for the distance formula, or should I try a different entry point next time?
3. How well did students connect the midpoint formula to the section formula? Did any still treat them as separate things to memorise?

### Student understanding
4. What percentage of students consistently swapped m₁/m₂ with x₁/x₂? Was my correction strategy effective?
5. Could most students identify the correct figure type from coordinates, or did they struggle with the decision tree?
6. How did students handle the "find the ratio" type problems vs. "find the point" type problems?

### Assessment insights
7. Which question on the assessment had the most errors? What does that tell me about my teaching?
8. Were students able to set up equidistant-point problems correctly, even if they made arithmetic errors?
9. Did students remember to verify their answers, or did they submit first attempts?

### Pedagogy
10. Which classroom activity was most effective? Which fell flat?
11. Did I use enough visual aids? Was the grid-on-the-floor activity worth the setup time?
12. Would the ICT integration activities have helped, or would they have slowed things down?

### For next time
13. What would I change in my time allocation?
14. Are there practice problems I should add or remove?
15. What should I revisit in the next chapter (Triangles/Polynomials review) to reinforce coordinate geometry skills?
