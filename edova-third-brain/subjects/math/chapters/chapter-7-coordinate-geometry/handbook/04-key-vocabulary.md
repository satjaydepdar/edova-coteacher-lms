## Core terms

| Term | Definition | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Abscissa | The x-coordinate of a point; its distance from the y-axis | In P(5, 3), the abscissa is 5 |
| Ordinate | The y-coordinate of a point; its distance from the x-axis | In P(5, 3), the ordinate is 3 |
| Distance formula | Formula to find the distance between two points using their coordinates | d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²] |
| Section formula | Formula to find coordinates of a point dividing a line segment in a given ratio | P = ((m₁x₂+m₂x₁)/(m₁+m₂), ...) |
| Midpoint | The point that divides a line segment into two equal parts; ratio 1:1 | M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2) |
| Collinear points | Points that lie on the same straight line | Verified when AB + BC = AC |
| Internal division | When the dividing point lies between the two endpoints of the segment | All problems in this chapter use internal division |
| Trisection | Dividing a line segment into three equal parts | Points divide in ratio 1:2 and 2:1 |
| Perpendicular bisector | The line perpendicular to a segment at its midpoint; locus of equidistant points | x ∓ y = 2 is the ⟂ bisector in Example 4 |

## Geometric figure properties (needed for applications)

| Figure | Defining properties (coordinate verification) |
|--------|----------------------------------------------|
| Square | All 4 sides equal AND both diagonals equal |
| Rectangle | Opposite sides equal AND both diagonals equal |
| Rhombus | All 4 sides equal (diagonals need NOT be equal) |
| Parallelogram | Diagonals bisect each other (midpoints coincide) |
| Isosceles triangle | At least 2 sides equal |
| Right triangle | Sum of squares of two sides equals square of third side |
| Equilateral triangle | All 3 sides equal |
