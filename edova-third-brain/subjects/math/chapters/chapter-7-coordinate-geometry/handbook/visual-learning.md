## Essential diagrams to draw on the board

### Diagram 1: Distance formula derivation
- Draw x and y axes
- Plot P(x₁, y₁) in the first quadrant
- Plot Q(x₂, y₂) also in the first quadrant
- Drop perpendiculars PR and QS to x-axis
- Draw PT perpendicular to QS
- Label: PT = x₂ − x₁, QT = y₂ − y₁
- Mark the right angle at T
- Write PQ² = PT² + QT² next to the triangle

### Diagram 2: Section formula derivation
- Draw A(x₁, y₁) and B(x₂, y₂)
- Mark P between A and B
- Drop perpendiculars AR, PS, BT to x-axis
- Draw AQ parallel to x-axis and PC perpendicular to BT
- Label the similar triangles ΔPAQ and ΔBPC
- Show the ratio m₁:m₂ on the segments

### Diagram 3: Equidistant point = perpendicular bisector
- Plot A(7, 1) and B(3, 5)
- Draw the segment AB
- Find and plot the midpoint M
- Draw the perpendicular bisector through M
- Plot the line x ∓ y = 2
- Show that any point on this line is equidistant from A and B

### Diagram 4: Quadrilateral verification
- Plot all four points
- Connect them in order
- Label all side lengths and diagonal lengths
- Use colour coding: sides in one colour, diagonals in another

## Visual strategies

- **Colour code consistently:** x-values in blue, y-values in red throughout the chapter
- **Use a physical coordinate grid:** A reusable grid on the board or a magnetic grid
- **Animate with sticky notes:** Place sticky notes at coordinates and move them to show division points
- **Highlight the right triangle:** Always highlight the right triangle used in the distance formula with a different colour
