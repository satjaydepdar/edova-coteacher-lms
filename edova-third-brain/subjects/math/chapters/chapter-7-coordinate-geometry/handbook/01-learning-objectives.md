## Knowledge (Remember)

- State the distance formula between two points P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂)
- State the section formula for internal division in the ratio m₁ : m₂
- State the midpoint formula
- Recall that points on the x-axis have coordinates (x, 0) and on the y-axis (0, y)

## Understanding (Comprehend)

- Explain how the distance formula is derived using Pythagoras' theorem
- Explain how the section formula is derived using similarity of triangles
- Describe the relationship between the perpendicular bisector and the locus of equidistant points
- Interpret geometric conditions (collinearity, triangle types, quadrilateral types) in terms of distances

## Application (Apply)

- Calculate the distance between any two given points
- Determine whether three points are collinear using the distance formula
- Verify properties of geometric figures (square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram) using distance formula
- Find the coordinates of a point dividing a line segment in a given ratio
- Find the midpoint of a line segment
- Solve word problems involving distance and division of line segments

## Analysis (Analyse)

- Compare different methods to verify collinearity (distance formula vs. area of triangle)
- Analyse a geometric figure's properties from given coordinates and justify the figure type
- Determine unknown coordinates given conditions about distances or midpoints
- Analyse when to use distance formula vs. section formula vs. midpoint formula

## Evaluation (Evaluate)

- Judge whether a given set of points forms a specific quadrilateral, with justification
- Evaluate the efficiency of different solution approaches for the same problem
- Verify whether a derived relation between x and y correctly represents an equidistant condition

## Creation (Create)

- Formulate relations between x and y for points satisfying given distance conditions
- Design coordinate geometry problems based on real-world contexts
- Create proofs of geometric properties using coordinate methods
