## Example 1 — Distance between two points

Find the distance between (2, 3) and (4, 1).

**Solution:**
PQ = √[(4 − 2)² + (1 − 3)²]
   = √[4 + 4]
   = √8
   = 2√2

## Example 2 — Distance from origin

Find the distance of the point (−3, 4) from the origin.

**Solution:**
OP = √[(−3)² + 4²]
   = √[9 + 16]
   = √25
   = 5

## Example 3 — Midpoint

Find the midpoint of the segment joining (1, −2) and (3, 6).

**Solution:**
M = ((1 + 3)/2, (−2 + 6)/2)
  = (4/2, 4/2)
  = (2, 2)

## Example 4 — Section formula (straightforward)

Find the point that divides the segment joining (2, 1) and (6, 5) in the ratio 1:1.

**Solution:**
Since the ratio is 1:1, this is the midpoint:
P = ((2 + 6)/2, (1 + 5)/2) = (4, 3)

## Example 5 — Section formula (simple ratio)

Find the point that divides the segment joining (1, 2) and (4, 5) in the ratio 2:1.

**Solution:**
x = (2×4 + 1×1)/(2 + 1) = (8 + 1)/3 = 3
y = (2×5 + 1×2)/(2 + 1) = (10 + 2)/3 = 4
P = (3, 4)

## Example 6 — Point on y-axis equidistant from two points

Find a point on the y-axis equidistant from (5, −2) and (−3, 2).

**Solution:**
Let the point be P(0, y).
AP = BP → AP² = BP²
(5 − 0)² + (−2 − y)² = (−3 − 0)² + (2 − y)²
25 + 4 + 4y + y² = 9 + 4 − 4y + y²
29 + 4y = 13 − 4y
8y = −16
y = −2
P = (0, −2)
