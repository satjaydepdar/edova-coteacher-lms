## Case Study 1 — City Planning

A city planner is designing a rectangular park. The park's corners are at A(1, 2), B(7, 2), C(7, 5) and D(1, 5) on a grid where each unit represents 100 metres.

**Q1:** What are the dimensions of the park in metres?
**Q2:** A fountain is to be placed at the centre of the park. Find its coordinates.
**Q3:** A pathway connects the midpoint of AB to the midpoint of CD. Find the length of this pathway.
**Q4:** Is the park actually a square? Justify.

**Solutions:**
A1: Length AB = 6 units = 600 m. Breadth BC = 3 units = 300 m.
A2: Centre = midpoint of AC = (4, 3.5).
A3: Midpoint of AB = (4, 2). Midpoint of CD = (4, 5). Pathway length = 3 units = 300 m.
A4: No. Sides are 600 m and 300 m — not all equal. It is a rectangle.

## Case Study 2 — Sports Day

During Sports Day, Niharika runs 1/4th of distance AD on the 2nd line and posts a green flag at point G. Preet runs 1/5th of distance AD on the 8th line and posts a red flag at point R. AD = 100 m, lines are 1 m apart.

**Q1:** If A = (2, 0) and D = (2, 100), find the coordinates of G and R.
**Q2:** Find the distance between the two flags.
**Q3:** Rashmi posts a blue flag halfway between G and R. Find its coordinates.

**Solutions:**
A1: G is on 2nd line (x=2), at 1/4 of AD: y = 25. G = (2, 25).
R is on 8th line (x=8), at 1/5 of AD: y = 20. R = (8, 20).
A2: GR = √[(8−2)² + (20−25)²] = √[36 + 25] = √61 m.
A3: Midpoint of GR = ((2+8)/2, (25+20)/2) = (5, 22.5).

## Case Study 3 — Classroom Seating

Four friends are seated at A(3, 1), B(0, 1), C(0, ∓1) and D(3, ∓1) in a classroom (each unit = 1 metre).

**Q1:** Champa says ABCD is a square. Chameli disagrees. Who is correct?
**Q2:** A fifth friend wants to sit at the exact centre. Where should they sit?
**Q3:** If the teacher is at T(6, 0), which friend is closest to the teacher?

**Solutions:**
A1: AB = 3, BC = 2, CD = 3, DA = 2. Not all sides equal. It's a rectangle, not a square. Chameli is correct.
A2: Centre = midpoint of AC = (1.5, 0).
A3: TA = √[9+1] = √10, TB = √[36+1] = √37, TC = √[36+1] = √37, TD = √[9+1] = √10. A and D are closest.
