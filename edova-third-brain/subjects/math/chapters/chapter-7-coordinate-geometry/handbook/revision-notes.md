## Three Formulas to Remember

### 1. Distance Formula
PQ = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]
From origin: OP = √(x² + y²)

### 2. Section Formula (Internal Division)
P divides AB in ratio m₁ : m₂
x = (m₁x₂ + m₂x₁) / (m₁ + m₂)
y = (m₁y₂ + m₂y₁) / (m₁ + m₂)
**Remember: m₁ goes with x₂ (second point)**

### 3. Midpoint Formula
M = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)
(This is section formula with m₁ : m₂ = 1 : 1)

## Key Results

- **Collinearity:** AB + BC = AC (largest = sum of other two)
- **Square:** All sides equal + both diagonals equal
- **Rhombus:** All sides equal (diagonals may differ)
- **Rectangle:** Opposite sides equal + both diagonals equal
- **Parallelogram:** Diagonals bisect each other (midpoints coincide)
- **Equidistant point:** AP = BP → lies on perpendicular bisector of AB
- **Point on x-axis:** Has form (a, 0)
- **Point on y-axis:** Has form (0, b)
- **Trisection:** Ratios are 1:2 and 2:1

## Common Traps

- Don't forget to SQUARE the differences in distance formula
- m₁ pairs with x₂, NOT x₁
- AP = (3/7)AB means ratio is 3:4, NOT 3:7
- 4 equal sides ≠ square (could be rhombus)
- HCF × LCM = product only for TWO numbers (irrelevant here, but don't confuse concepts)
- For finding ratio: use k:1 form, set one coordinate equal to known value, solve for k
