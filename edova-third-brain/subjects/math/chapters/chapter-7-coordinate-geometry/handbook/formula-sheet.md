## Distance Formula

Distance between P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂):

**d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]**

Distance from origin: **d = √(x² + y²)**

---

## Section Formula (Internal Division)

P divides A(x₁, y₁) and B(x₂, y₂) in ratio m₁ : m₂:

**x = (m₁ · x₂ + m₂ · x₁) / (m₁ + m₂)**

**y = (m₁ · y₂ + m₂ · y₁) / (m₁ + m₂)**

Using k : 1 notation:

**x = (k · x₂ + x₁) / (k + 1)**

**y = (k · y₂ + y₁) / (k + 1)**

---

## Midpoint Formula

Midpoint of P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂):

**M = ((x₁ + x₂) / 2 , (y₁ + y₂) / 2)**

## Geometric Verification Conditions

| Figure | Conditions to Check |
|--------|-------------------|
| Collinear | AB + BC = AC |
| Equilateral △ | AB = BC = CA |
| Isosceles △ | Any two sides equal |
| Right △ | a² + b² = c² |
| Square | All sides equal + diagonals equal |
| Rhombus | All sides equal |
| Rectangle | Opposite sides equal + diagonals equal |
| Parallelogram | Midpoint of AC = Midpoint of BD |

---

## Special Points

- Point on x-axis: (a, 0)
- Point on y-axis: (0, b)
- Trisection ratios: 1 : 2 and 2 : 1
- Fraction to ratio: AP = (p/q) AB → AP : PB = p : (q − p)
