# Glossary – Coordinate Geometry

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Coordinate Axes** | The two perpendicular number lines (x-axis and y-axis) that form the Cartesian plane. |
| **Origin** | The point of intersection of the coordinate axes, denoted as O(0, 0). |
| **Quadrant** | Any of the four regions into which the coordinate axes divide the plane. |
| **Abscissa** | The x-coordinate of a point. |
| **Ordinate** | The y-coordinate of a point. |
| **Cartesian Plane** | A two-dimensional plane defined by the x-axis and y-axis. |
| **Reflection** | A mirror image of a point or shape across a line. |
| **Distance Formula** | Formula to find the distance between two points in a plane. |
| **Section Formula** | Formula to find coordinates of a point dividing a line segment in a given ratio. |
| **Area of Triangle** | Formula to calculate area given coordinates of three vertices. |
