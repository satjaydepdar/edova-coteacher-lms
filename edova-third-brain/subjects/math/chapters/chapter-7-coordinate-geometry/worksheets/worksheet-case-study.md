## Case Study 1 — Relay Tower Placement

A town B is located 36 km east and 15 km north of town A. Taking town A as the origin $(0, 0)$ and 1 km as one unit on both axes, the coordinates of B are $(36, 15)$. A telecom company wants to position a relay tower at point P between A and B such that the distance of the tower from B is twice its distance from A.

**Q1.** The distance between town A and town B is:
(a) $51$ km &emsp; (b) $21$ km &emsp; (c) $39$ km &emsp; (d) $30$ km

**Q2.** The ratio in which P divides the line segment AB is:
(a) $1:1$ &emsp; (b) $2:1$ &emsp; (c) $1:2$ &emsp; (d) $3:1$ 
**Q3.** The coordinates of the relay tower P are:
(a) $(24, 10)$ &emsp; (b) $(12, 5)$ &emsp; (c) $(18, 7.5)$ &emsp; (d) $(9, 3.75)$ 
**Q4.** Find the distance of the relay tower P from town A.

**Q5.** If another tower Q is placed at the midpoint of AB, find the distance between towers P and Q.

## Case Study 2 — Classroom Seating Arrangement

In a classroom, three students Ashima, Bharti and Camella are seated at positions $A(3, 1)$, $B(6, 4)$ and $C(8, 6)$ respectively on a coordinate grid drawn on the classroom floor.

**Q1.** The distance AB is:
(a) $2\sqrt{2}$ units &emsp; (b) $3\sqrt{2}$ units &emsp; (c) $\sqrt{18}$ units &emsp; (d) Both (b) and (c)

**Q2.** The distance AC is:
(a) $\sqrt{50}$ units &emsp; (b) $5\sqrt{2}$ units &emsp; (c) Both (a) and (b) &emsp; (d) $\sqrt{40}$ units

**Q3.** Find the distance BC.

**Q4.** Are the three students seated in a straight line? Justify your answer using the distance formula.

**Q5.** If a fourth student Deepa sits at the midpoint of BC, her coordinates are:
(a) $(7, 5)$ &emsp; (b) $(14, 10)$ &emsp; (c) $(1, -1)$ &emsp; (d) $(5, 2.5)$

## Case Study 3 — Sports Day Flag Placement

To conduct Sports Day activities in a rectangular school ground ABCD, lines are drawn with chalk powder at a distance of 1 m each. 100 flower pots are placed at 1 m intervals along AD. Niharika runs $\frac{1}{4}$th the distance AD on the 2nd line and posts a green flag at point G. Preet runs $\frac{1}{5}$th the distance AD on the 8th line and posts a red flag at point R. Rashmi must post a blue flag exactly halfway between G and R.

**Q1.** The coordinates of the green flag G are:
(a) $(2, 25)$ &emsp; (b) $(2, 75)$ &emsp; (c) $(25, 2)$ &emsp; (d) $(75, 2)$ 
**Q2.** The coordinates of the red flag R are:
(a) $(8, 20)$ &emsp; (b) $(8, 80)$ &emsp; (c) $(20, 8)$ &emsp; (d) $(80, 8)$ 
**Q3.** The distance between the green flag and the red flag is:
(a) $\sqrt{61}$ m &emsp; (b) $\sqrt{51}$ m &emsp; (c) $\sqrt{41}$ m &emsp; (d) $11$ m

**Q4.** Find the coordinates where Rashmi should post the blue flag.

**Q5.** On which numbered line should Rashmi post the blue flag?
(a) 3rd line &emsp; (b) 4th line &emsp; (c) 5th line &emsp; (d) 6th line

### Case Study 1 — Relay Tower Placement

**Q1.** (c) $AB = \sqrt{36^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{1296+225} = \sqrt{1521} = 39$ km

**Q2.** (c) Distance from B is twice the distance from A, so $PB = 2PA$, i.e., $PA : PB = 1 : 2$ 
**Q3.** (b) P divides AB in $1:2$: $P = \left(\dfrac{1(36)+2(0)}{3},\; \dfrac{1(15)+2(0)}{3}\right) = (12,\; 5)$ 
**Q4.** $AP = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{144+25} = \sqrt{169} = 13$ km

**Q5.** Midpoint Q of AB $= \left(\dfrac{0+36}{2},\; \dfrac{0+15}{2}\right) = (18,\; 7.5)$ 
 $PQ = \sqrt{(18-12)^2 + (7.5-5)^2} = \sqrt{36+6.25} = \sqrt{42.25} = 6.5$ km

### Case Study 2 — Classroom Seating Arrangement

**Q1.** (d) $AB = \sqrt{(6-3)^2+(4-1)^2} = \sqrt{9+9} = \sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$ units

**Q2.** (c) $AC = \sqrt{(8-3)^2+(6-1)^2} = \sqrt{25+25} = \sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$ units

**Q3.** $BC = \sqrt{(8-6)^2+(6-4)^2} = \sqrt{4+4} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$ units

**Q4.** $AB + BC = 3\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{2} = 5\sqrt{2} = AC$. Since $AB + BC = AC$, the points A, B, C are **collinear** — yes, they are seated in a straight line.

**Q5.** (a) Midpoint of $B(6,4)$ and $C(8,6) = \left(\dfrac{6+8}{2},\; \dfrac{4+6}{2}\right) = (7,\; 5)$ 
### Case Study 3 — Sports Day Flag Placement

**Q1.** (a) 2nd line → $x=2$; $\frac{1}{4}$th of AD $= \frac{100}{4}=25$ → $y=25$. G $= (2, 25)$ 
**Q2.** (a) 8th line → $x=8$; $\frac{1}{5}$th of AD $= \frac{100}{5}=20$ → $y=20$. R $= (8, 20)$ 
**Q3.** (a) $GR = \sqrt{(8-2)^2+(20-25)^2} = \sqrt{36+25} = \sqrt{61}$ m

**Q4.** Blue flag (midpoint of GR) $= \left(\dfrac{2+8}{2},\; \dfrac{25+20}{2}\right) = (5,\; 22.5)$
