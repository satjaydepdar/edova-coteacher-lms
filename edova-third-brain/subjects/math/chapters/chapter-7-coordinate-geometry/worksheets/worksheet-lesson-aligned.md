## Lesson 1: Cartesian Plane, Plotting, and Reflection

### Section A - Concept Check (1 mark each)

1. The horizontal axis is called the ______ axis.
2. The point of intersection of the coordinate axes is called the ______.
3. In which quadrant does the point (-4, 7) lie?

### Section B - Application (2 marks each)

4. Plot the points A(3, 2), B(-2, 4), C(-3, -1), and D(2, -3) on a graph.
5. Write the reflection of the point (-5, 6) in:
   (a) the x-axis  
   (b) the y-axis  
   (c) the origin

## Lesson 2: Distance Formula and Midpoint Formula

### Section A - Concept Check (1 mark each)

1. Write the distance formula between two points P(x1, y1) and Q(x2, y2).
2. Write the midpoint formula for the segment joining A(x1, y1) and B(x2, y2).
3. Find the distance of the point (5, 12) from the origin.

### Section B - Short Answer (2 marks each)

4. Find the distance between (1, -3) and (4, 1).
5. Find the midpoint of the segment joining (-5, 7) and (3, -1).

### Section C - Numerical (3 marks each)

6. Determine whether the points A(1, 1), B(4, 5), and C(7, 9) are collinear.
7. Find the point on the y-axis equidistant from (2, 3) and (-4, 1).

---

## Lesson 3: Section Formula

### Section A - Concept Check (1 mark each)

1. Write the section formula for internal division in the ratio m1 : m2.
2. The midpoint formula is a special case of the section formula when the ratio is ______.
3. Find the coordinates of the point that divides the segment joining (0, 0) and (6, 8) in the ratio 1:1.

### Section B - Application (2 marks each)

4. Find the point that divides the segment joining (1, 2) and (7, 8) in the ratio 2:1.
5. Find the coordinates of the points of trisection of the segment joining (2, 1) and (5, 7).
