## Questions (2 marks each)

1. Find the distance between the points (−5, 7) and (−1, 3).

2. Find the midpoint of the segment joining A(2, −3) and B(−6, 7).

3. Find the point on the x-axis which is equidistant from (2, −5) and (−2, 9).

4. If the point (3, k) divides the segment joining (1, −1) and (5, 7) in the ratio 1:1, find k.

5. Find the coordinates of the point which divides the join of (−1, 7) and (4, −3) in the ratio 2:3.

## Questions (3 marks each)

6. Find the ratio in which the y-axis divides the line segment joining the points (5, −6) and (−1, −4). Also find the point of intersection.

7. If the points A(6, 1), B(8, 2), C(9, 4) and D(p, 3) are the vertices of a parallelogram, find p.

8. Find a relation between x and y such that the point (x, y) is equidistant from the points (3, 6) and (−3, 4).

9. Check whether (5, −2), (6, 4) and (7, −2) are the vertices of an isosceles triangle.

10. If Q(0, 1) is equidistant from P(5, ∓3) and R(x, 6), find the values of x.

## Answers

1. √[(4)² + (−4)²] = √32 = 4√2
2. ((2−6)/2, (−3+7)/2) = (−2, 2)
3. (−7, 0)
4. k = (−1+7)/2 = 3
5. ((2×4+3×(−1))/5, (2×(−3)+3×7)/5) = (1, 3)
6. Ratio 5:1, Point (0, −13/3)
7. p = 7
8. 3x − y = 5
9. AB = √37, BC = √37, AC = 2. Yes, isosceles.
10. x = ±4
