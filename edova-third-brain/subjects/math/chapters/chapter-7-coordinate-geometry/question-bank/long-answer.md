## Questions (4 marks each)

1. Show that the points (1, 7), (4, 2), (−1, −1) and (−4, 4) are the vertices of a square.

2. Find the coordinates of the points of trisection of the line segment joining (4, −1) and (−2, −3).

3. Name the type of quadrilateral formed by the points (−1, −2), (1, 0), (−1, 2) and (−3, 0). Give reasons.

4. Find the area of a rhombus if its vertices are (3, 0), (4, 5), (−1, 4) and (−2, −1) taken in order.

5. In a classroom, 4 friends are seated at A(3, 1), B(0, 1), C(0, ∓1) and D(3, ∓1). Using the distance formula, determine if ABCD is a square.

## Questions (5 marks each)

6. If A(2, −1), B(3, 4), C(−2, 3) and D(−3, −2) are four points, determine whether ABCD is a square, a rectangle, or neither. Justify your answer completely.

7. To conduct Sports Day activities, in a rectangular school ground ABCD, lines are drawn at 1m intervals. 100 flower pots are placed along AD at 1m intervals. Niharika runs 1/4th of AD on the 2nd line and posts a green flag. Preet runs 1/5th of AD on the 8th line and posts a red flag. Find the distance between the flags. If Rashmi posts a blue flag halfway between the two flags, where should she post it?

8. Find the values of y for which the distance between P(2, ∓3) and Q(10, y) is 10 units. Also find the coordinates of the point on the x-axis which is equidistant from (2, −5) and (−2, 9).
