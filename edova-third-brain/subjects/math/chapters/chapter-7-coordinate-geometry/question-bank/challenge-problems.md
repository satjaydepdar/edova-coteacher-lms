## Distance Formula

**Q1.** If the points $A(2, 3)$, $B(4, k)$, and $C(6, -3)$ are collinear, find the value of $k$.

**Q2.** If $P(x, y)$ is equidistant from $A(a + b,\; b - a)$ and $B(a - b,\; a + b)$, prove that $bx = ay$.

**Q3.** Find the points on the x-axis that are at a distance of $5$ units from the point $(2, 3)$.

**Q4.** If the distance between $P(x, 3)$ and $Q(4, -2)$ is $13$ units, find all possible values of $x$.

**Q5.** The centre of a circle is at $(a,\; a - 1)$. Find the values of $a$ if the circle passes through the point $(3, 1)$ and has a radius of $5$ units.

**Q6.** Using the distance formula, show that the points $(3, -2)$, $(-5, 4)$, and $(7, -6)$ are collinear.

**Q7.** If the distance between the points $(a, -1)$ and $(3, 5)$ is $10$ units, find the values of $a$. Hence determine how many such points exist on the x-axis.

**Q8.** Point $A$ lies on the line $y = 2x$ and is at a distance of $\sqrt{5}$ units from the origin. Find the coordinates of all possible positions of $A$.

## Section Formula & Midpoint

**Q9.** Find the coordinates of the point on the line segment joining $A(-2, 5)$ and $B(3, -1)$ that is twice as far from $A$ as it is from $B$.

**Q10.** Find the coordinates of the midpoint of the line segment joining the two points of trisection of the segment joining $(4, -1)$ and $(-2, -3)$.

**Q11.** The line segment joining $A(2, 3)$ and $B(6, -5)$ is intercepted by the x-axis at point $K$. Find the ratio $AK : KB$ and the coordinates of $K$.

**Q12.** If $A(-1, 3)$, $B(1, -1)$, and $C(5, 1)$ are the vertices of $\triangle ABC$, find the length of the median from $A$ to side $BC$.

**Q13.** Determine the ratio in which the point $(2, y)$ divides the line segment joining $A(-2, 6)$ and $B(4, 0)$. Also find the value of $y$.

**Q14.** The midpoint of the segment joining $(3, p)$ and $(-7, 3)$ is $(-2, q)$. Find the values of $p$ and $q$.

**Q15.** Point $P$ divides the segment joining $A(2, 1)$ and $B(6, -5)$ internally in the ratio $3 : 1$. A point $Q$ lies on the extension of $AB$ beyond $B$ such that $PQ = AB$. Find the coordinates of $Q$.

## Collinearity & Properties of Figures

**Q16.** Show that the points $(1, -1)$, $(5, 2)$, and $(9, 5)$ are collinear. Find the ratio in which $(5, 2)$ divides the segment joining $(1, -1)$ and $(9, 5)$.

**Q17.** If $(x, y)$, $(1, 2)$, and $(3, 4)$ are collinear, prove that $x + y = 3$.

**Q18.** Find the value of $k$ for which the points $(7, -2)$, $(5, 1)$, and $(3, k)$ are collinear.

**Q19.** Prove that the quadrilateral with vertices $A(-2, -1)$, $B(1, 3)$, $C(7, 2)$, and $D(4, -2)$ is a rhombus. Determine whether it is also a square, and justify your answer.

**Q20.** Three consecutive vertices of a parallelogram are $(1, -2)$, $(3, 6)$, and $(5, 10)$. Find the fourth vertex. Determine whether this parallelogram is a rectangle.
