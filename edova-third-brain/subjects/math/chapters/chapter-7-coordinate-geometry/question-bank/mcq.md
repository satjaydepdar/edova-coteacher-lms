## Questions

**Q1.** The distance between the points (0, 3) and (0, 7) is:
(a) 4  (b) 10  (c) √58  (d) √40

**Q2.** The distance of the point (−6, 8) from the origin is:
(a) 10  (b) 14  (c) √100  (d) √28

**Q3.** The midpoint of the segment joining (2, 3) and (4, 7) is:
(a) (3, 5)  (b) (6, 10)  (c) (1, 2)  (d) (3, 4)

**Q4.** The point which divides the segment joining (−1, 7) and (4, −3) in the ratio 2:3 is:
(a) (1, 3)  (b) (1, 5)  (c) (5, 1)  (d) (3, 1)

**Q5.** If the points (a, 0), (0, b) and (1, 1) are collinear, then:
(a) a + b = 1  (b) a ∓ b = 1  (c) 1/a + 1/b = 1  (d) ab = 1

**Q6.** The perpendicular bisector of the segment joining (2, 5) and (6, 3) is:
(a) x + y = 8  (b) x ∓ y = 3  (c) 2x ∓ y = 7  (d) x ∓ 2y = −4

**Q7.** A point on the x-axis equidistant from (3, 2) and (−1, 4) is:
(a) (0, 0)  (b) (1, 0)  (c) (−1, 0)  (d) (2, 0)

**Q8.** The points A(1, 2), B(4, 4) and C(7, 6) are:
(a) Vertices of an equilateral triangle  (b) Collinear
(c) Vertices of an isosceles triangle  (d) Vertices of a right triangle

**Q9.** If P divides AB internally in the ratio 3:2 and A = (1, −2), B = (6, 8), then P is:
(a) (4, 4)  (b) (3, 4)  (c) (4, 3)  (d) (5, 5)

**Q10.** The distance between (−a, b) and (a, −b) is:
(a) √(2a² + 2b²)  (b) 2√(a² + b²)  (c) √(a² + b²)  (d) 2(a + b)

## Answers

1. (a)  2. (a)  3. (a)  4. (a)  5. (c)  6. (b)  7. (c)  8. (b)  9. (a)  10. (b)
