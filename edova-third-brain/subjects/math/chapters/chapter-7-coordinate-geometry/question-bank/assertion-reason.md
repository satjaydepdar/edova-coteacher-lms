## Instructions

For each question, choose:
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true but R is false
(d) A is false but R is true
(e) Both A and R are false

## Questions

**Q1.**
**A:** The distance between (1, 0) and (5, 0) is 4 units.
**R:** Distance between two points on the x-axis is the difference of their x-coordinates.

**Q2.**
**A:** The point (0, 5) divides the segment joining (−3, 5) and (3, 5) in the ratio 1:1.
**R:** Any point on the perpendicular bisector of a segment divides it in the ratio 1:1.

**Q3.**
**A:** If AB + BC = AC, then points A, B, C are collinear.
**R:** Three points are collinear if and only if they lie on a straight line.

**Q4.**
**A:** The midpoint of the segment joining (a, b) and (−a, −b) is the origin.
**R:** The midpoint formula is M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2).

**Q5.**
**A:** A quadrilateral with all sides equal is always a square.
**R:** A square has all sides equal and both diagonals equal.

## Answers

1. (a)  2. (a)  3. (a)  4. (a)  5. (c) — A is false (it could be a rhombus); R is true.
