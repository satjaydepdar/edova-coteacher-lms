# Board Exam Preparation: Triangles

## Expected Question Types

| Marks | Typical Question |
|---|---|
| 1 mark | Identify similar figures; recall a theorem statement; state a criterion. |
| 2 marks | Apply BPT/converse to find an unknown length; identify similarity criterion. |
| 3 marks | Prove two triangles similar using AA/SSS/SAS; solve a shadow/height problem. |
| 4 marks | Prove BPT or its converse; prove a relationship involving medians/proportionality. |
| 5 marks (case study) | Real-life indirect measurement (shadow, lamp-post, tower, etc.). |

## Frequent NCERT Topics

1. Definition of similar figures and similarity of polygons (Exercise 6.1).
2. Using the Basic Proportionality Theorem to find unknown lengths (Exercise 6.2).
3. Converse of BPT to show a line is parallel to the third side (Exercise 6.2).
4. Proving triangles similar using AA, SSS, or SAS criteria (Exercise 6.3).
5. Shadow/height problems using AA similarity (Example 7, Exercise 6.3 Q15).
6. Problems involving medians of similar triangles (Example 8, Exercise 6.3 Q16).
7. Proving relationships in trapeziums using similarity (Exercise 6.3 Q3).
8. RHS similarity criterion from the "Note to the Reader".

## Time Management

- 1-mark questions: ~1 minute each.
- 2-mark questions: ~3 minutes each.
- 3-mark questions: ~5 minutes each.
- 4-mark questions: ~7 minutes each.
- Case study questions: ~8–10 minutes each.

## Common Errors to Avoid

- Writing similarity with incorrect vertex correspondence.
- Using AAA when AA is sufficient.
- Confusing the ratio $\dfrac{AD}{DB}$ with $\dfrac{AD}{AB}$ in BPT problems.
- Forgetting to state the reason (theorem/criterion) for each step in a proof.
- Including topics such as areas of similar triangles or Pythagoras theorem, which are **not** in this NCERT chapter.

## Sample Board-Style Questions

1. State and prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Theorem 6.1).
2. In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 3.9$ cm, $EQ = 3$ cm, $PF = 3.6$ cm and $FR = 2.4$ cm. Is $EF \parallel QR$? Justify.
3. Prove that the line joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side.
4. Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a trapezium $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel DC$ intersect at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.
5. A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long. Find the height of the tower.
6. If $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$ and $CM$, $RN$ are medians, prove that $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.
