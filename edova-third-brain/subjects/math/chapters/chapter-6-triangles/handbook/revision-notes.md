# Revision Notes: Triangles

## 1. Similar Figures

- Same shape, not necessarily same size.
- All congruent figures are similar; the converse is not true.
- For polygons: equal corresponding angles + proportional corresponding sides.

## 2. Similarity of Triangles

- $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$ means:
  - $\angle A = \angle D$, $\angle B = \angle E$, $\angle C = \angle F$
  - $\dfrac{AB}{DE} = \dfrac{BC}{EF} = \dfrac{CA}{FD}$
- Always check the vertex correspondence before writing the similarity statement.

## 3. Basic Proportionality Theorem

- If $DE \parallel BC$ in $\Delta ABC$, then $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$.
- Converse: If $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$, then $DE \parallel BC$.

## 4. Similarity Criteria

| Criterion | What to check |
|---|---|
| AA | Two pairs of equal angles. |
| AAA | All three pairs of equal angles (same as AA in triangles). |
| SSS | All three pairs of sides proportional. |
| SAS | One pair of equal angles with including sides proportional. |
| RHS | Right triangles with hypotenuse and one side proportional. |

## 5. Common Diagram Patterns

- Parallel lines cutting two sides of a triangle $\rightarrow$ BPT.
- Vertically opposite angles + alternate angles $\rightarrow$ AA similarity.
- Shared angle + one equal angle (often from parallel lines or right angles) $\rightarrow$ AA similarity.
- Medians in similar triangles divide them into similar smaller triangles.

## 6. Indirect Measurement

Shadow problems: use AA similarity of right triangles formed by objects and their shadows.

$$\frac{h_1}{h_2} = \frac{s_1}{s_2}$$

## 7. Important Tips for Board Exam

- Write the theorem/criterion used at each step.
- Keep vertex correspondence correct.
- Do not include topics like areas of similar triangles or Pythagoras theorem — they are not part of this NCERT chapter.
