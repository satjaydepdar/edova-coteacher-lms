## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Similar Figures** | Figures that have the same shape but not necessarily the same size. |
| **Congruent Figures** | Figures that have the same shape and the same size. |
| **Scale Factor** | The ratio of corresponding sides of two similar polygons. |
| **Similar Polygons** | Two polygons of the same number of sides are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio. |
| **Similar Triangles** | Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio. |
| **Equiangular Triangles** | Triangles in which all corresponding angles are equal. |
| **Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)** | A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio. |
| **Converse of BPT** | If a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then it is parallel to the third side. |
| **AAA Criterion** | Angle–Angle–Angle similarity criterion (Theorem 6.3). |
| **AA Criterion** | If two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another triangle, the triangles are similar. |
| **SSS Criterion** | Side–Side–Side similarity criterion (Theorem 6.4). |
| **SAS Criterion** | Side–Angle–Side similarity criterion (Theorem 6.5). |
| **RHS Criterion** | In two right triangles, if hypotenuse and one side are proportional, the triangles are similar. |
