## Similarity of Polygons

Two polygons of the same number of sides are similar if:
- their corresponding angles are equal, and
- their corresponding sides are in the same ratio (proportion).

## Similarity of Triangles

Two triangles are similar if:
- their corresponding angles are equal, and
- their corresponding sides are in the same ratio.

Symbolically, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$.

## Basic Proportionality Theorem (Theorem 6.1)

In $\Delta ABC$, if $DE \parallel BC$ with $D$ on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$, then

$$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$$

## Converse of BPT (Theorem 6.2)

In $\Delta ABC$, if $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$, then $DE \parallel BC$.

## Similarity Criteria

| Criterion | Condition |
|---|---|
| **AAA / AA** (Theorem 6.3) | Corresponding angles are equal. |
| **SSS** (Theorem 6.4) | Corresponding sides are proportional. |
| **SAS** (Theorem 6.5) | One pair of corresponding angles equal and the including sides proportional. |
| **RHS** (Note to Reader) | In right triangles, hypotenuse and one side are proportional. |

## Useful Result from BPT

If $DE \parallel BC$, then

$$\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC}$$

## Shadow / Height Problems

If two vertical objects cast shadows at the same time, the triangles formed are similar by AA criterion:

$$\frac{\text{height of object 1}}{\text{height of object 2}} = \frac{\text{length of shadow 1}}{\text{length of shadow 2}}$$
