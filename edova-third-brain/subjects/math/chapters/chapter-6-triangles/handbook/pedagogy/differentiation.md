# Differentiation Strategies

## For Struggling Learners

- Provide pre-labelled diagrams with corresponding sides and angles colour-coded.
- Start with concrete examples (circles, squares, equilateral triangles) before general polygons.
- Use a step-by-step template for BPT problems.
- Allow use of a formula/criterion card during practice.

## For Average Learners

- Mixed practice identifying the appropriate similarity criterion.
- Scaffolded proofs with missing reasons to fill in.
- Pair work on NCERT Exercise 6.2 and 6.3 questions.

## For Advanced Learners

- Prove the medians result independently (Example 8).
- Explore the relationship between the ratio of corresponding medians and the ratio of corresponding sides.
- Investigate the RHS similarity criterion and why it works for right triangles.

## Visual Learners

- Use colour-coded diagrams and dynamic geometry software.
- Draw the same triangle enlarged on grid paper.

## Kinesthetic Learners

- Paper-cutting activities to verify similarity.
- Outdoor shadow measurement.

## Verbal Learners

- Explain proofs to a partner.
- Write a "how-to" guide for each similarity criterion.
