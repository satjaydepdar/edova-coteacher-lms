# Art Integration

## Activity 1: Scale Drawing of the Taj Mahal

Students create scale drawings of the Taj Mahal in different sizes and explain why the drawings are similar.

## Activity 2: Similarity Collage

Create a collage using cut-outs of similar and non-similar figures. Label each pair with the reason.

## Activity 3: Criterion Poster

Design a poster for one similarity criterion (AA, SSS, SAS, RHS) with a diagram, statement, and a worked example from the textbook.

## Activity 4: Shadow Art

Students draw a scene showing a person and a lamp-post with their shadows. They label the similar triangles and write the ratio equation.
