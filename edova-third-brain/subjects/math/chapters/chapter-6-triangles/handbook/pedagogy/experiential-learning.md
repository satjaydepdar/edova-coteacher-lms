# Experiential Learning

## Activity 1: Shadow Measurement

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Measure the height of a tree or building using the length of its shadow and a metre stick. Use AA similarity to calculate the unknown height.

## Activity 2: Enlargement by Scale Factor

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Draw a simple polygon on grid paper and enlarge it by a scale factor of $2$ or $3$. Verify that corresponding angles are equal and sides are in the same ratio.

## Activity 3: Paper Triangle Similarity

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Cut out two triangles with the same angles but different side lengths. Measure and confirm that corresponding sides are proportional.

## Activity 4: Camera Obscura / Bulb Shadow

Replicate the textbook activity: place a polygon cut-out between a light source and a screen. Observe that the shadow is an enlarged similar figure.
