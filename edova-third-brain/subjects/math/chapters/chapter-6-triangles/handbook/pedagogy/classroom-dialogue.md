# Classroom Dialogue

## Opening Discussion

**Teacher:** "Two photographs of the Taj Mahal of different sizes are printed from the same negative. Are they the same shape?"

**Student:** "Yes."

**Teacher:** "Are they the same size?"

**Student:** "No."

**Teacher:** "So they are an example of similar figures. Today we will learn what makes two figures similar."

## On the Basic Proportionality Theorem

**Teacher:** "If a line parallel to one side of a triangle cuts the other two sides, what can you say about the segments?"

**Student:** "They are divided in the same ratio."

**Teacher:** "How can we prove that?"

**Student:** "By comparing areas of triangles on the same base and between the same parallels."

## On Similarity Criteria

**Teacher:** "Do we need all three angles and all three sides to check similarity?"

**Student:** "No. AA, SSS, or SAS is enough."

**Teacher:** "Why is AA enough?"

**Student:** "Because the third angle is fixed by the angle sum property."

## Closing Reflection

**Teacher:** "Where in real life do we use similar triangles?"

**Student:** "To find heights of towers or trees using shadows."
