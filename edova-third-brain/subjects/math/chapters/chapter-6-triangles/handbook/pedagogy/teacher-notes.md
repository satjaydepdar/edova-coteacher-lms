# Teacher Notes: Triangles

## Lesson Flow Suggestion

### Day 1: Introduction and Similar Figures (Sections 6.1 and 6.2)
- Distinguish congruent and similar figures using cut-outs.
- State the definition of similar polygons and the meaning of scale factor.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 6.1.

### Day 2: Similarity of Triangles and BPT (Section 6.3)
- Define similar triangles and equiangular triangles.
- State and prove Theorem 6.1 (Basic Proportionality Theorem).
- Work through NCERT Example 1.

### Day 3: Converse of BPT and Applications
- State and prove Theorem 6.2 (Converse of BPT).
- Work through NCERT Examples 2 and 3.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 6.2.

### Day 4: AAA and SSS Similarity Criteria (Section 6.4)
- State Theorems 6.3 and 6.4.
- Emphasise that AA is sufficient in triangles.
- Work through NCERT Examples 4 and 5.

### Day 5: SAS Similarity and Further Examples
- State Theorem 6.5.
- Work through NCERT Examples 6, 7, and 8.
- Assign NCERT Exercise 6.3.

### Day 6: Summary and Mixed Practice
- Recap all theorems and criteria.
- Mention the RHS similarity criterion from the "Note to the Reader".
- Mixed board-style questions.

## Teaching Tips

- Insist on correct vertex correspondence when writing $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$.
- Use GeoGebra to verify BPT numerically.
- Encourage students to state the theorem or criterion used in each step of a proof.
- Keep the chapter scope clear: areas of similar triangles and Pythagoras theorem are **not** in this NCERT chapter.
