# ICT Integration

## Recommended Tools

1. **GeoGebra / Desmos**
   - Construct a triangle and a line parallel to one side to verify the Basic Proportionality Theorem.
   - Drag vertices and observe that the ratios remain equal.

2. **Interactive Similarity Applets**
   - Visualise enlargement and scale factor.
   - Compare corresponding angles and side ratios dynamically.

3. **Kahoot / Quizizz / Google Forms**
   - Quick quizzes on identifying similarity criteria.
   - True/false questions on congruent vs similar figures.

## Suggested Activities

- **BPT Explorer:** Students draw triangles in GeoGebra, construct parallel lines, and measure segment ratios.
- **Criterion Matcher:** Drag triangle pairs to the correct criterion box (AA, SSS, SAS, RHS).
- **Shadow Simulator:** Use a virtual light source and object to see how shadow length changes with distance.
