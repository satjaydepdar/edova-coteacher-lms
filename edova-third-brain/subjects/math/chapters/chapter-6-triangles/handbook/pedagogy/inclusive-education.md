# Inclusive Education

## For Students with Dyscalculia

- Use colour coding for corresponding sides and angles consistently.
- Provide step-by-step proof templates.
- Allow extra time for ratio calculations.
- Use manipulatives (card cut-outs) to verify similarity.

## For Students with Visual Impairments

- Provide tactile diagrams with raised lines.
- Use geo-boards or string models to form triangles.
- Read diagrams aloud using clear spatial language.

## For Students with Hearing Impairments

- Provide written instructions and visual cues.
- Use captioned videos if showing GeoGebra demonstrations.
- Encourage peer note-sharing.

## For Slow Learners

- Break proofs into micro-steps.
- Focus first on AA similarity before moving to SSS and SAS.
- Provide additional practice with immediate feedback.

## For Gifted Students

- Offer enrichment tasks such as proving the converse of BPT rigorously.
- Ask students to derive the RHS similarity criterion from the other criteria.
- Assign project-based learning on real-life applications of similarity.
