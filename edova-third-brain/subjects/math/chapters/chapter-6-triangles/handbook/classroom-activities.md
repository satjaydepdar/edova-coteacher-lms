# Classroom Activities

## Activity 1: Similar or Not?

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Provide cards with pairs of figures (circles, squares, rectangles, rhombuses, equilateral triangles, isosceles triangles). Students classify each pair as "similar", "congruent", or "neither" and justify using the definition of similarity.

**Sample Cards:**
- Two circles of different radii.
- A square and a rectangle.
- A square and a rhombus.
- Two equilateral triangles of different sizes.
- Two photographs of the same monument in different sizes.

---

## Activity 2: Discover the Basic Proportionality Theorem

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4 | **Materials:** Ruled paper, ruler, protractor

Students draw any triangle $ABC$, mark a point $D$ on $AB$, draw $DE \parallel BC$ meeting $AC$ at $E$, and measure $AD$, $DB$, $AE$, $EC$. They verify that $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$.

---

## Activity 3: Similarity Criteria Card Sort

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams of 4

Prepare cards describing pairs of triangles (e.g., two angles equal; sides in ratio $2:3$; one angle equal with including sides proportional). Teams match each case to the appropriate criterion: AA, SSS, SAS, or RHS.

---

## Activity 4: Shadow Measurement Challenge

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Students measure the height of a lamp-post or tree using the length of their own shadow and the object's shadow. They use AA similarity to calculate the unknown height (based on Example 7 and Exercise 6.3 Q15).
