# Introduction

## What are Similar Figures?

You are already familiar with triangles and many of their properties. In Class IX, you studied congruence of triangles. Recall:

- **Congruent figures** have the same shape and the same size.
- **Similar figures** have the same shape but not necessarily the same size.

In this chapter, we study figures that have the same shape but may differ in size, with special focus on the similarity of triangles.

## Indirect Measurement

Can you guess how the heights of mountains (for example, Mount Everest) or the distances of very distant objects (for example, the moon) have been found out? They have **not** been measured directly with a measuring tape. Instead, they are found using the idea of **indirect measurement**, which is based on the principle of similarity of figures.

This idea will be illustrated in Example 7, Question 15 of Exercise 6.3, and in later chapters on heights and distances.
