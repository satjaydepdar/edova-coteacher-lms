# RHS Similarity Criterion

## Note to the Reader

At the end of the chapter, the textbook includes the following note:

> If in two right triangles, hypotenuse and one side of one triangle are proportional to the hypotenuse and one side of the other triangle, then the two triangles are similar.

This may be referred to as the **RHS Similarity Criterion**.

## When to Use It

The RHS similarity criterion applies **only to right triangles**. It is useful because you need only check:

1. Both triangles are right-angled.
2. The hypotenuse of one triangle is proportional to the hypotenuse of the other.
3. One pair of corresponding sides is proportional.

The textbook notes that if this criterion is used in Example 2 of Chapter 8, the proof becomes simpler.
