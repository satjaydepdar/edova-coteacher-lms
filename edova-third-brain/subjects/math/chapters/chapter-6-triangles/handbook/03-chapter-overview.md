# Chapter 6: Triangles

## What Students Will Learn

This chapter extends students' knowledge of congruent triangles to the study of similar triangles. Students will:

1. Distinguish between congruent and similar figures.
2. Use the definition of similarity for polygons and triangles.
3. Apply the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Theorem 6.1) and its converse (Theorem 6.2).
4. Prove similarity of triangles using AAA/AA, SSS, and SAS criteria (Theorems 6.3, 6.4, 6.5).
5. Solve problems involving indirect measurement using similar triangles.
6. Recognise the RHS similarity criterion stated in the "Note to the Reader".

## Key Examples from the Textbook

- Example 1: A line parallel to $BC$ intersects $AB$ and $AC$; prove $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}$.
- Example 2: Trapezium $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel DC$ and $EF \parallel AB$; prove $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{BF}{FC}$.
- Example 3: Given $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR}$ and $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$, prove $\Delta PQR$ is isosceles.
- Example 4: If $PQ \parallel RS$, prove $\Delta POQ \sim \Delta SOR$.
- Example 5: Find $\angle P$ when sides of two triangles are proportional.
- Example 6: Given $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$, show $\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle B = \angle D$.
- Example 7: A girl walking away from a lamp-post; find the length of her shadow.
- Example 8: Medians of similar triangles; prove related triangle similarities.

## Competency Focus

- **Problem Solving:** Translate geometric diagrams into ratio equations.
- **Reasoning:** Justify similarity using appropriate criteria and theorems.
- **Communication:** Write proofs with correct symbolic correspondence and logical steps.
