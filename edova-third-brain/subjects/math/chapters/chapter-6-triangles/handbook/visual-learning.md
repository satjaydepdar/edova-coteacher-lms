# Visual Learning: Triangles

## 1. Congruent vs Similar

Use two sets of cut-outs:
- Two identical triangles (congruent and similar).
- Two equilateral triangles of different sizes (similar but not congruent).
- A square and a rectangle (not similar despite equal angles).
- A square and a rhombus (not similar despite proportional sides).

## 2. Scale Factor with Photographs

Show Taj Mahal photographs of different sizes. Explain that every length is enlarged in the same ratio and every angle remains unchanged.

## 3. Basic Proportionality Theorem Diagram

Draw $\Delta ABC$ with $DE \parallel BC$. Label segments $AD$, $DB$, $AE$, $EC$ and highlight the ratio equation:

$$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$$

## 4. Similarity Criteria at a Glance

| Criterion | Diagram Hint |
|---|---|
| AA / AAA | Two (or three) pairs of equal angles marked with the same arcs. |
| SSS | Corresponding sides marked with lengths in the same ratio. |
| SAS | One equal angle and the two including sides proportional. |
| RHS | Right triangles with hypotenuse and one leg in proportion. |

## 5. Shadow Problem Diagram

Draw the lamp-post $AB$, the girl $CD$, and their common shadow $BE = BD + DE$. Mark right angles and the common angle at $E$ to show AA similarity of $\Delta ABE$ and $\Delta CDE$.

## 6. Medians in Similar Triangles

Draw $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$ with medians $CM$ and $RN$. Shade $\Delta AMC$ and $\Delta PNR$ to show they are similar by SAS.

## Suggested Classroom Visual Aids

- Coloured card cut-outs for sorting similar figures.
- GeoGebra for dynamic exploration of BPT and similarity.
- Grid paper for scale drawings.
- Measuring tape and metre stick for shadow activities.
