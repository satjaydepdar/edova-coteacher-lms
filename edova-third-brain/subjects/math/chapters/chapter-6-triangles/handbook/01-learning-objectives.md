## Knowledge (Remember)

- Define similar figures and distinguish them from congruent figures.
- State the definition of similarity of polygons and triangles.
- State Theorem 6.1 (Basic Proportionality Theorem) and Theorem 6.2 (Converse of BPT).
- State Theorems 6.3, 6.4, and 6.5 for similarity of triangles.

## Understanding (Comprehend)

- Explain why all congruent figures are similar but the converse is not true.
- Interpret the meaning of scale factor for similar polygons.
- Describe how the Basic Proportionality Theorem divides the sides of a triangle.
- Compare the AAA, AA, SSS, SAS, and RHS similarity criteria.

## Application (Apply)

- Decide whether two given polygons are similar using the definition.
- Apply the Basic Proportionality Theorem and its converse to solve problems.
- Use AA, SSS, and SAS criteria to prove that two triangles are similar.
- Solve real-life problems involving similar triangles (shadows, heights, distances).

## Analysis (Analyse)

- Determine the correct correspondence of vertices when writing similarity symbolically.
- Analyse a diagram to identify pairs of similar triangles and the appropriate criterion.
- Relate the ratios of corresponding sides to the equality of corresponding angles.

## Evaluation (Evaluate)

- Judge whether a given pair of triangles is similar and justify the criterion used.
- Evaluate the correctness of a proof involving similarity.
- Decide whether a real-world situation can be modelled using similar triangles.

## Creation (Create)

- Design examples of similar and non-similar polygons.
- Construct a problem involving indirect measurement using similar triangles.
- Develop a visual proof or poster explaining one of the similarity criteria.
