# Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) Examples

## Example 6: Product of Segments and Equal Angles

In the figure, $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$. Show that $\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle B = \angle D$.

**Solution:**

Given:

$$OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$$

Therefore:

$$\frac{OA}{OC} = \frac{OD}{OB} \quad \text{...(1)}$$

Also, $\angle AOD = \angle COB$ (vertically opposite angles). \quad ...(2)

From (1) and (2), by the SAS similarity criterion:

$$\Delta AOD \sim \Delta COB$$

Hence, corresponding angles are equal:

$$\angle A = \angle C \quad \text{and} \quad \angle D = \angle B$$

## Example 7: Shadow of a Girl Walking from a Lamp-post

A girl of height $90$ cm is walking away from the base of a lamp-post at a speed of $1.2$ m/s. If the lamp is $3.6$ m above the ground, find the length of her shadow after $4$ seconds.

**Solution:**

Let $AB$ be the lamp-post and $CD$ the girl after walking for $4$ seconds. Let $DE = x$ metres be the length of her shadow.

Distance walked in $4$ seconds:

$$BD = 1.2 \times 4 = 4.8 \text{ m}$$

In $\Delta ABE$ and $\Delta CDE$:

- $\angle B = \angle D = 90°$
- $\angle E = \angle E$ (common)

By the AA similarity criterion:

$$\Delta ABE \sim \Delta CDE$$

Therefore:

$$\frac{BE}{DE} = \frac{AB}{CD}$$

$$\frac{4.8 + x}{x} = \frac{3.6}{0.9}$$

$$\frac{4.8 + x}{x} = 4$$

$$4.8 + x = 4x$$

$$3x = 4.8$$

$$x = 1.6$$

So, the length of her shadow after $4$ seconds is $1.6$ m.

## Example 8: Medians of Similar Triangles

$CM$ and $RN$ are respectively the medians of $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta PQR$. If $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, prove that:

1. $\Delta AMC \sim \Delta PNR$
2. $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$
3. $\Delta CMB \sim \Delta RNQ$

**Solution:**

Given: $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$.

Therefore:

$$\frac{AB}{PQ} = \frac{BC}{QR} = \frac{CA}{RP} \quad \text{...(1)}$$

and

$$\angle A = \angle P,\quad \angle B = \angle Q,\quad \angle C = \angle R \quad \text{...(2)}$$

Since $CM$ and $RN$ are medians:

$$AB = 2AM \quad \text{and} \quad PQ = 2PN$$

From (1):

$$\frac{2AM}{2PN} = \frac{CA}{RP}$$

$$\frac{AM}{PN} = \frac{CA}{RP} \quad \text{...(3)}$$

Also, $\angle MAC = \angle NPR$ [from (2)]. \quad ...(4)

From (3) and (4), by SAS similarity:

$$\Delta AMC \sim \Delta PNR \quad \text{...(5)}$$

From (5):

$$\frac{CM}{RN} = \frac{CA}{RP} \quad \text{...(6)}$$

But from (1), $\dfrac{CA}{RP} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$. Therefore:

$$\frac{CM}{RN} = \frac{AB}{PQ}$$

For the third part, since $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$ and $AB = 2BM$, $PQ = 2QN$:

$$\frac{CM}{RN} = \frac{2BM}{2QN} = \frac{BM}{QN}$$

Also $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{BC}{QR}$ [from (1) and the result above].

Thus:

$$\frac{CM}{RN} = \frac{BC}{QR} = \frac{BM}{QN}$$

By SSS similarity:

$$\Delta CMB \sim \Delta RNQ$$
