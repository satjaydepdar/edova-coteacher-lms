# Teacher Reflection: Triangles

## After Each Lesson

- Could students distinguish between congruent and similar figures?
- Did students write similarity statements with correct vertex correspondence?
- Were students able to identify the appropriate similarity criterion from a diagram?
- Which step in BPT proofs caused the most difficulty?

## After the Chapter

- What percentage of students can apply the Basic Proportionality Theorem correctly?
- What percentage can prove triangle similarity using AA, SSS, and SAS?
- Which type of problem was most challenging — proofs, numerical BPT, or shadow applications?
- Did students avoid confusing similarity with congruence?

## Improvements for Next Year

- Where can I add more hands-on activities (shadows, paper cutting, GeoGebra)?
- Should I allocate more time to vertex correspondence and symbolic notation?
- What misconceptions arose, and how can I address them earlier?
