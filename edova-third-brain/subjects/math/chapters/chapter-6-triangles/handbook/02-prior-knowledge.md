## Required Concepts from Previous Classes

### 1. Congruence of Triangles (Class IX)
- Congruent figures have the same shape and the same size.
- Criteria for congruency: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, RHS.
- Symbolic representation: $\Delta ABC \cong \Delta DEF$ with correct vertex correspondence.

### 2. Angles and Parallel Lines
- Corresponding angles and alternate interior angles formed by a transversal.
- If two lines are parallel, corresponding (and alternate) angles are equal.

### 3. Ratio and Proportion
- Meaning of ratio and equivalent ratios.
- If $\dfrac{a}{b} = \dfrac{c}{d}$, then $\dfrac{a+b}{b} = \dfrac{c+d}{d}$ (componendo).

### 4. Area of a Triangle
- Area of a triangle = $\dfrac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
- Triangles on the same base and between the same parallels have equal areas.

### 5. Properties of Quadrilaterals
- Trapezium: one pair of opposite sides parallel.
- Parallelogram: opposite sides parallel and equal.

## Quick Diagnostic Checks

1. In $\Delta ABC$, if $DE \parallel BC$, name a pair of equal corresponding angles.
2. State the SSS congruence rule.
3. If $\dfrac{x}{3} = \dfrac{y}{5}$, find $\dfrac{x+y}{y}$.
4. The sides of a triangle are in the ratio $2:3:4$. If the shortest side is $6$ cm, find the other two sides.
5. Name the quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides.

## Common Gaps to Address

- Confusing similarity with congruence.
- Writing similarity with incorrect vertex correspondence.
- Incorrectly pairing corresponding sides when ratios are given.
- Difficulty in identifying equal angles in diagrams involving parallel lines.
