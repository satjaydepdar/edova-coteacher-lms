**A5.** (a)

**A6.** If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

**A7.** $\dfrac{PE}{QE} = \dfrac{PF}{RF} = \dfrac{8}{9}$, so by Theorem 6.2, $EF \parallel QR$.

**A8.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By SSS similarity, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$. Therefore $\angle P = \angle C = 180° - 80° - 60° = 40°$.

**A9.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{3.8}{7.6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By SSS similarity, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$.

**A10.** Distance walked = $4.8$ m. Let shadow be $x$ m. Then $\dfrac{4.8+x}{x} = \dfrac{3.6}{0.9} = 4$, so $x = 1.6$ m.

**A11.** See [basic-proportionality-theorem.md](../handbook/concepts/basic-proportionality-theorem.md).

**A12.** By AA similarity, $\Delta AOB \sim \Delta COD$, so $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**A13.** $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR} \Rightarrow ST \parallel QR \Rightarrow \angle PST = \angle PQR$. Since $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$, $\angle PQR = \angle PRQ$, so $PQ = PR$.

**A14.** Height of tower = $42$ m.

**A15.** By AA similarity, $\Delta ADC \sim \Delta BAC$. Hence $\dfrac{CA}{CB} = \dfrac{CD}{CA}$, giving $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.

**A16.** $4.8$ m.

**A17.** $1.6$ m.

**A18.** Both triangles are right-angled and share the angle of elevation of the sun (AA similarity).

**A19.** $42$ m.
