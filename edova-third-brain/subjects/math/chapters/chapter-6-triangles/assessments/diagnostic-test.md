**Q1.** Which of the following pairs is always similar?

(a) Two rectangles
(b) Two rhombuses
(c) Two equilateral triangles
(d) Two isosceles triangles

**Q2.** In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on sides $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 4$ cm, $QE = 4.5$ cm, $PF = 8$ cm and $RF = 9$ cm. Then:

(a) $EF \parallel QR$
(b) $EF$ is perpendicular to $QR$
(c) $EF$ intersects $QR$
(d) none of these

(a) perpendicular to the third side
(b) parallel to the third side
(c) equal to the third side
(d) none of these
