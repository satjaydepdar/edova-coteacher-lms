# Assessment Rubrics

## Chapter 6: Triangles

---

## Rubric for Geometric Proofs

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Given/To Prove** | Clearly stated | Mostly clear | Partially stated | Missing |
| **Theorem/Criterion Use** | Correct theorem cited at each step | Minor omissions | Some incorrect citations | Theorems not cited |
| **Logical Flow** | All steps clear and logical | Most steps clear | Some jumps in reasoning | Reasoning unclear |
| **Vertex Correspondence** | Correct throughout | Minor error | Some errors | Incorrect |
| **Conclusion** | Clear and precise | Mostly clear | Incomplete | Missing |

---

## Rubric for Numerical Problems

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Diagram/Identification** | Correct diagram and identification | Minor error | Partial identification | Missing |
| **Formula/Theorem Choice** | Appropriate theorem selected | Appropriate but not best | Some difficulty | Inappropriate |
| **Working Steps** | Clear and logical | Mostly clear | Some steps missing | Unclear |
| **Calculation** | Correct | Minor arithmetic error | One major error | Incorrect |
| **Final Answer/Units** | Correct with units/context | Mostly correct | Incomplete | Missing |
