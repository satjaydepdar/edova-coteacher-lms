### Section B: Short Answer (2 × 5 = 10 marks)

**Q6.** State the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

**Q7.** In $\Delta PQR$, $PE = 4$ cm, $QE = 4.5$ cm, $PF = 8$ cm and $RF = 9$ cm. Show that $EF \parallel QR$.

**Q8.** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta RQP$, $AB = 3.8$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 3$ cm, $RQ = 7.6$ cm, $QP = 12$ cm and $PR = 6$ cm. If $\angle A = 80°$ and $\angle B = 60°$, show that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$ and find $\angle P$.

**Q9.** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta RQP$, $AB = 3.8$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 3$ cm, $RQ = 7.6$ cm, $QP = 12$ cm and $PR = 6$ cm. Show that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$.

**Q10.** All congruent figures are similar. Is the converse true? Justify.

---

### Section C: Word Problem (5 × 1 = 5 marks)

**Q11.** A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long. Find the height of the tower.

---

## Answer Key

**A1.** (b)
**A2.** (b)
**A3.** (b)
**A4.** (c)
**A5.** (b)

**A6.** If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

**A7.** $\dfrac{PE}{QE} = \dfrac{4}{4.5} = \dfrac{8}{9}$ and $\dfrac{PF}{RF} = \dfrac{8}{9}$. Since the ratios are equal, by Theorem 6.2, $EF \parallel QR$.

**A8.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By SSS similarity, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$. Therefore $\angle P = \angle C = 180° - 80° - 60° = 40°$.

**A9.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{3.8}{7.6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By SSS similarity, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$.

**A10.** The converse is not true. Similar figures have the same shape but not necessarily the same size. Congruent figures must also have the same size.

**A11.** By AA similarity, $\dfrac{h}{6} = \dfrac{28}{4} = 7$, so $h = 42$ m.
