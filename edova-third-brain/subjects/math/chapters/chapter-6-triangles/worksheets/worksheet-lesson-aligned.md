## Based on Lesson 3: Similarity Criteria (Section 6.4)

**Q5.** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta RQP$, $AB = 3.8$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 3$ cm, $RQ = 7.6$ cm, $QP = 12$ cm and $PR = 6$ cm. If $\angle A = 80°$ and $\angle B = 60°$, show that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$ and find $\angle P$.

**Q6.** Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a trapezium $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel DC$ intersect each other at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**Q7.** A girl of height $90$ cm is walking away from a lamp-post of height $3.6$ m at $1.2$ m/s. Find the length of her shadow after $4$ seconds.

**Q1.**
(i) similar
(ii) equal, proportional

**Q2.** Sample: Two equilateral triangles of different sizes are similar; a square and a rectangle are not similar.

**Q3.** $\dfrac{PE}{QE} = \dfrac{4}{4.5} = \dfrac{8}{9}$ and $\dfrac{PF}{RF} = \dfrac{8}{9}$. Since the ratios are equal, by Theorem 6.2, $EF \parallel QR$.

**Q4.** Since $D$ and $E$ are mid-points, $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = 1 = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$. By Theorem 6.2, $DE \parallel BC$.

**Q5.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{3.8}{7.6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By SSS similarity, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$. Hence $\angle P = \angle C = 180° - 80° - 60° = 40°$.

**Q6.** In $\Delta AOB$ and $\Delta COD$, $\angle OAB = \angle OCD$ and $\angle OBA = \angle ODC$ (alternate angles). By AA similarity, $\Delta AOB \sim \Delta COD$, so $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**Q7.** Let shadow length be $x$ m. Distance walked = $4.8$ m.

By AA similarity:

$$\frac{4.8 + x}{x} = \frac{3.6}{0.9} = 4$$

$$x = 1.6 \text{ m}$$
