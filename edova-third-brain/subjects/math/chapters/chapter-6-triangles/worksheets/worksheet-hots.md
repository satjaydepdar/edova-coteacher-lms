## Section A: Proofs Involving Medians (NCERT Example 8)

**Q1.** $CM$ and $RN$ are respectively the medians of $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta PQR$. If $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, prove that $\Delta AMC \sim \Delta PNR$ and $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.

---

## Section B: Ratio and Product Relationships (NCERT Exercise 6.3)

**Q2.** $D$ is a point on the side $BC$ of $\Delta ABC$ such that $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$. Show that $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.

**Q3.** Sides $AB$ and $BC$ and median $AD$ of $\Delta ABC$ are respectively proportional to sides $PQ$ and $QR$ and median $PM$ of $\Delta PQR$. Show that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$.

---

## Section C: Indirect Measurement

**Q4.** A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long. Find the height of the tower.

**Q5.** If $AD$ and $PM$ are medians of triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$ respectively, where $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, prove that $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{AD}{PM}$.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** Since $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{CA}{RP}$ and $\angle A = \angle P$. As $CM$ and $RN$ are medians, $AM = \dfrac{1}{2}AB$ and $PN = \dfrac{1}{2}PQ$, so $\dfrac{AM}{PN} = \dfrac{CA}{RP}$. By SAS similarity, $\Delta AMC \sim \Delta PNR$. Hence $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{CA}{RP} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.

**Q2.** In $\Delta ADC$ and $\Delta BAC$, $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$ (given) and $\angle C = \angle C$ (common). By AA similarity, $\Delta ADC \sim \Delta BAC$. Therefore:

$$\frac{CA}{BC} = \frac{CD}{CA}$$

$$CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$$

**Q3.** Given $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{BC}{QR} = \dfrac{AD}{PM}$. Since $AD$ and $PM$ are medians, $BD = \dfrac{1}{2}BC$ and $QM = \dfrac{1}{2}QR$, so $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{BD}{QM}$. Also $\angle B = \angle Q$ (derived from SSS congruence of the smaller triangles formed by the median). Hence $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$.

**Q4.** By AA similarity, $\dfrac{h}{6} = \dfrac{28}{4} = 7$, so $h = 42$ m.

**Q5.** This follows from the median result: $\Delta ABD \sim \Delta PQM$ by SAS, giving $\dfrac{AD}{PM} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.

</details>
