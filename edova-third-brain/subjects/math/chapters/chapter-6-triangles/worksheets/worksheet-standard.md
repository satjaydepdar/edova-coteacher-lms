## Section A: BPT Proofs (NCERT Exercise 6.2)

**Q1.** Using Theorem 6.1, prove that a line drawn through the mid-point of one side of a triangle parallel to another side bisects the third side.

**Q2.** Using Theorem 6.2, prove that the line joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side.

**Q3.** $ABCD$ is a trapezium in which $AB \parallel DC$ and its diagonals intersect each other at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{AO}{CO} = \dfrac{BO}{DO}$.

## Section B: Similarity Criteria (NCERT Exercise 6.3)

**Q4.** $S$ and $T$ are points on sides $PR$ and $QR$ of $\Delta PQR$ such that $\angle P = \angle RTS$. Show that $\Delta RPQ \sim \Delta RTS$.

**Q5.** $E$ is a point on the side $AD$ produced of a parallelogram $ABCD$ and $BE$ intersects $CD$ at $F$. Show that $\Delta ABE \sim \Delta CFB$.

**Q6.** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta RQP$, $AB = 3.8$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 3$ cm, $RQ = 7.6$ cm, $QP = 12$ cm and $PR = 6$ cm. If $\angle A = 80°$ and $\angle B = 60°$, show that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$ and find $\angle P$.

---

## Section C: Application

**Q7.** A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long. Find the height of the tower.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** Let $D$ be the mid-point of $AB$ and $DE \parallel BC$. By Theorem 6.1, $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$. Since $AD = DB$, we get $AE = EC$. Hence $E$ is the mid-point of $AC$.

**Q2.** Let $D$ and $E$ be the mid-points of $AB$ and $AC$. Then $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = 1 = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$. By Theorem 6.2, $DE \parallel BC$.

**Q3.** In $\Delta AOB$ and $\Delta COD$, $\angle OAB = \angle OCD$ and $\angle OBA = \angle ODC$ (alternate angles). By AA similarity, $\Delta AOB \sim \Delta COD$, so $\dfrac{AO}{CO} = \dfrac{BO}{DO}$.

**Q4.** In $\Delta RPQ$ and $\Delta RTS$, $\angle R = \angle R$ (common) and $\angle P = \angle RTS$ (given). By AA similarity, $\Delta RPQ \sim \Delta RTS$.

**Q5.** In $\Delta ABE$ and $\Delta CFB$, $\angle A = \angle C$ (opposite angles of parallelogram) and $\angle ABE = \angle CFB$ (alternate angles, $AB \parallel DC$). By AA similarity, $\Delta ABE \sim \Delta CFB$.

**Q6.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{3.8}{7.6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. By SSS similarity, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$. Therefore $\angle C = \angle P = 180° - 80° - 60° = 40°$.

**Q7.** By AA similarity of the triangles formed by the pole/tower and their shadows:

$$\frac{h}{6} = \frac{28}{4} = 7$$

$$h = 42 \text{ m}$$

The height of the tower is $42$ m.
