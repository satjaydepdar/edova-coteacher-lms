**Q2.** Sample answers:
(i) Two photographs of the same monument in different sizes; two circles of different radii.
(ii) A circle and a square; a square and a rectangle.

**Q3.**
(i) $\dfrac{PE}{EQ} = \dfrac{3.9}{3} = 1.3$; $\dfrac{PF}{FR} = \dfrac{3.6}{2.4} = 1.5$. Ratios are not equal, so $EF$ is **not** parallel to $QR$.

(ii) $\dfrac{PE}{QE} = \dfrac{4}{4.5} = \dfrac{8}{9}$; $\dfrac{PF}{RF} = \dfrac{8}{9}$. Ratios are equal, so $EF \parallel QR$.

(iii) $EQ = PQ - PE = 1.10$ cm; $FR = PR - PF = 2.20$ cm.

$\dfrac{PE}{EQ} = \dfrac{0.18}{1.10} = \dfrac{9}{55}$; $\dfrac{PF}{FR} = \dfrac{0.36}{2.20} = \dfrac{9}{55}$. Ratios are equal, so $EF \parallel QR$.

**Q4.** Let $D$ and $E$ be the mid-points of $AB$ and $AC$ respectively. Then $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = 1 = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$. By Theorem 6.2, $DE \parallel BC$.
