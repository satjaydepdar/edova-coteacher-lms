# Lesson 1

## Chapter 6: Triangles
### Topic: Introduction and Similar Figures (NCERT Sections 6.1 and 6.2)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Distinguish between congruent and similar figures.
- State the definition of similar polygons.
- Use the scale factor to compare corresponding sides.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Congruent figures: same shape and same size.
- Similar figures: same shape, not necessarily same size.
- Two polygons are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Show two photographs of the same monument in different sizes.
- **Recall:** Congruency criteria from Class IX.
- **Define:** Introduce similar figures.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Discussion:** All circles are similar; all squares are similar; all equilateral triangles are similar.
- **Counter-examples:** Square and rectangle (equal angles but sides not proportional); square and rhombus (proportional sides but angles not equal).
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 6.1 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the two conditions for similarity of polygons.
- Assign remaining questions from Exercise 6.1 as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Give two different examples of pairs of similar figures and two pairs of non-similar figures.
