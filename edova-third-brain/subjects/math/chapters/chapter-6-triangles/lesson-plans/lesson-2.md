# Lesson 2

## Chapter 6: Triangles
### Topic: Similarity of Triangles and Basic Proportionality Theorem (NCERT Section 6.3)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Define similar triangles and equiangular triangles.
- State and apply the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Theorem 6.1).
- State and apply the converse of BPT (Theorem 6.2).

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Similar triangles: equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides.
- Basic Proportionality Theorem: $DE \parallel BC \Rightarrow \dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$.
- Converse of BPT: $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC} \Rightarrow DE \parallel BC$.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Draw a triangle and a line parallel to one side. Ask students to predict a relationship among the segments.
- **Recall:** Area of triangles on the same base and between the same parallels.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Theorem 6.1:** Prove BPT using area ratios.
- **Example 1:** Prove $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}$ when $DE \parallel BC$.
- **Theorem 6.2:** Outline the converse proof.
- **Examples 2 and 3:** Trapezium and isosceles triangle problems.
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 6.2 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise BPT and its converse.
- Assign remaining Exercise 6.2 questions as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 3.9$ cm, $EQ = 3$ cm, $PF = 3.6$ cm and $FR = 2.4$ cm. Is $EF \parallel QR$? Justify.
