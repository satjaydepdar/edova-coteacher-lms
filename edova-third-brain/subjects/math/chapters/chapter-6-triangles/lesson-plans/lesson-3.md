# Lesson 3

## Chapter 6: Triangles
### Topic: Criteria for Similarity of Triangles (NCERT Section 6.4)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- State the AAA/AA, SSS, and SAS similarity criteria.
- Choose and apply the correct criterion to prove triangle similarity.
- Recognise the RHS similarity criterion from the "Note to the Reader".

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Symbolic notation: $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$.
- AAA/AA criterion (Theorem 6.3).
- SSS criterion (Theorem 6.4).
- SAS criterion (Theorem 6.5).
- RHS criterion (right triangles only; Note to the Reader).

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** "Do we always need to check all six parts (three angles and three sides) to decide similarity?"
- **Discuss:** In congruency, three parts were enough; perhaps similarity also has short cuts.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Theorem 6.3 (AAA/AA):** Explain why AA is sufficient.
- **Theorem 6.4 (SSS):** Sides proportional implies angles equal.
- **Theorem 6.5 (SAS):** One equal included angle and proportional including sides.
- **Examples 4–8:** Work through each, highlighting the criterion used.
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 6.3 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise all similarity criteria.
- Mention the RHS similarity criterion from the "Note to the Reader".
- Assign remaining Exercise 6.3 questions as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta DEF$, $\angle A = \angle D = 50°$, $AB = 4$ cm, $AC = 6$ cm, $DE = 6$ cm, $DF = 9$ cm. Are the triangles similar? If yes, by which criterion?
