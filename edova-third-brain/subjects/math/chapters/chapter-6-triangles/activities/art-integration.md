# Art Integration

## Chapter 6: Triangles

---

## Activity 1: Scale Drawing of the Taj Mahal

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual or pairs

Create scale drawings of the Taj Mahal in two different sizes and explain why they are similar.

---

## Activity 2: Similarity Collage

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual

Make a collage using cut-outs of similar and non-similar figures. Label each pair with the reason.

---

## Activity 3: Criterion Poster

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual

Design a poster for one similarity criterion (AA, SSS, SAS, RHS) with a diagram and a textbook example.

---

## Activity 4: Shadow Scene

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Draw a scene showing a person and a lamp-post with shadows. Label the similar triangles and write the ratio equation.
