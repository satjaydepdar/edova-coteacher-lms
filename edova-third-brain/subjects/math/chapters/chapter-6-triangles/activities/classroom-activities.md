# Classroom Activities

## Chapter 6: Triangles

---

## Activity 1: Similar or Not?

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Prepare cards with pairs of figures. Students sort them into "Similar", "Congruent", or "Neither" and justify using the definition of similarity.

**Sample Cards:**
- Two circles of different radii.
- A square and a rectangle.
- A square and a rhombus.
- Two equilateral triangles of different sizes.
- Two photographs of the same monument in different sizes.

---

## Activity 2: Discover BPT

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Students draw any triangle, mark a point on one side, draw a line parallel to another side, and measure the four segments. They verify Theorem 6.1.

---

## Activity 3: Similarity Criteria Card Sort

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Teams of 4

Match cards describing triangle pairs to the correct criterion: AA, SSS, SAS, or RHS.

---

## Activity 4: Shadow Measurement

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Measure the height of a lamp-post or tree using shadows and AA similarity.
