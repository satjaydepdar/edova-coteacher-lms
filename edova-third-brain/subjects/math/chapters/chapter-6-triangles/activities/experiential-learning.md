# Experiential Learning

## Chapter 6: Triangles

---

## Activity 1: Outdoor Shadow Measurement

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Use a metre stick and measuring tape to find the height of a tree or building using similar triangles formed by shadows.

---

## Activity 2: Enlargement on Grid Paper

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Draw a polygon and enlarge it by a scale factor of $2$ or $3$. Verify that corresponding angles are equal and sides are proportional.

---

## Activity 3: Paper Triangle Similarity

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Cut out two triangles with equal angles but different sizes. Measure sides and confirm proportionality.

---

## Activity 4: Light and Shadow Model

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Replicate the textbook bulb-shadow activity. Place a polygon cut-out between a light source and a screen and observe the enlarged similar shadow.
