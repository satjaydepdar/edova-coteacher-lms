# Chapter 6 — Triangles

## Overview

This chapter introduces **similar figures** and, in particular, the **similarity of triangles**. It covers the Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT), its converse, and the criteria for similarity of triangles (AAA/AA, SSS, SAS). The chapter concludes with the RHS similarity criterion mentioned in the "Note to the Reader". All content is drawn from the NCERT/CBSE Class 10 textbook PDF.

---

## Chapter Topics (based on NCERT Chapter 6)

- 6.1 Introduction
- 6.2 Similar Figures
- 6.3 Similarity of Triangles (Basic Proportionality Theorem and converse)
- 6.4 Criteria for Similarity of Triangles (AA/AAA, SSS, SAS)
- 6.5 Summary
- Note to the Reader: RHS Similarity Criterion
