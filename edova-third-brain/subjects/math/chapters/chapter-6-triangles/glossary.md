# Glossary – Triangles

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Similar Figures** | Figures having the same shape but not necessarily the same size. |
| **Congruent Figures** | Figures having the same shape and the same size. All congruent figures are similar, but similar figures need not be congruent. |
| **Scale Factor** | The ratio of corresponding sides of two similar polygons; also called the Representative Fraction. |
| **Corresponding Angles** | Angles in two figures that occupy the same relative position. |
| **Corresponding Sides** | Sides in two figures that occupy the same relative position. |
| **Equiangular Triangles** | Triangles in which corresponding angles are equal. |
| **Similar Triangles** | Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio. Symbolically, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$. |
| **Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)** | A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio. |
| **Thales Theorem** | Another name for the Basic Proportionality Theorem. |
| **AAA Similarity Criterion** | If in two triangles, corresponding angles are equal, then their corresponding sides are proportional and the triangles are similar (Theorem 6.3). |
| **AA Similarity Criterion** | If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the two triangles are similar. |
| **SSS Similarity Criterion** | If in two triangles, the sides of one triangle are proportional to the sides of the other triangle, then their corresponding angles are equal and the triangles are similar (Theorem 6.4). |
| **SAS Similarity Criterion** | If one angle of a triangle is equal to one angle of another triangle and the sides including these angles are proportional, then the two triangles are similar (Theorem 6.5). |
| **RHS Similarity Criterion** | If in two right triangles, the hypotenuse and one side of one triangle are proportional to the hypotenuse and one side of the other triangle, then the two triangles are similar. |
| **Median** | A line segment joining a vertex of a triangle to the mid-point of the opposite side. |
