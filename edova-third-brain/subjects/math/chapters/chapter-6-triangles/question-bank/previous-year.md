# Previous Year Questions

**Q1.** State and prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

**Q2.** In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 3.9$ cm, $EQ = 3$ cm, $PF = 3.6$ cm and $FR = 2.4$ cm. Is $EF \parallel QR$? Justify.

**Q3.** Prove that the line joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side.

**Q4.** Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a trapezium $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel DC$ intersect each other at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**Q5.** A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long. Find the height of the tower.

**Q6.** If $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$ and $CM$, $RN$ are medians, prove that $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.

**Q7.** In $\Delta PQR$, $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR}$ and $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$. Prove that $\Delta PQR$ is an isosceles triangle.

**Q8.** $D$ is a point on the side $BC$ of $\Delta ABC$ such that $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$. Show that $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.

---

## Answers

**A1.** See [basic-proportionality-theorem.md](../handbook/concepts/basic-proportionality-theorem.md).

**A2.** $\dfrac{PE}{EQ} = 1.3$ and $\dfrac{PF}{FR} = 1.5$. Since the ratios are not equal, $EF$ is not parallel to $QR$.

**A3.** Let $D$ and $E$ be mid-points of $AB$ and $AC$. Then $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = 1 = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$. By Theorem 6.2, $DE \parallel BC$.

**A4.** By AA similarity, $\Delta AOB \sim \Delta COD$, so $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**A5.** Height of tower = $42$ m.

**A6.** See Example 8 in [hots.md](../handbook/examples/hots.md).

**A7.** $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR} \Rightarrow ST \parallel QR \Rightarrow \angle PST = \angle PQR$. Since $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$, we get $\angle PQR = \angle PRQ$, so $PQ = PR$.

**A8.** By AA similarity, $\Delta ADC \sim \Delta BAC$. Hence $\dfrac{CA}{CB} = \dfrac{CD}{CA}$, giving $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.
