# Challenge Problems

**Q1.** Sides $AB$ and $AC$ and median $AD$ of $\Delta ABC$ are respectively proportional to sides $PQ$ and $PR$ and median $PM$ of $\Delta PQR$. Show that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$.

**Q2.** $D$ is a point on the side $BC$ of $\Delta ABC$ such that $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$. Show that $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.

**Q3.** $CM$ and $RN$ are respectively the medians of $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta PQR$. If $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, prove that:
(i) $\Delta AMC \sim \Delta PNR$
(ii) $\Delta CMB \sim \Delta RNQ$

**Q4.** $ABCD$ is a trapezium in which $AB \parallel DC$ and its diagonals intersect at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**Q5.** Prove that the line joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side. Also, find the ratio in which this line divides the third side if extended.

**Q6.** $E$ is a point on side $CB$ produced of an isosceles triangle $ABC$ with $AB = AC$. If $AD \perp BC$ and $EF \perp AC$, prove that $\Delta ABD \sim \Delta ECF$.

---

## Answers

**A1.** Given $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{AC}{PR} = \dfrac{AD}{PM}$. Since $AD$ and $PM$ are medians, $BD = \dfrac{1}{2}BC$ and $QM = \dfrac{1}{2}QR$. This implies $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{BD}{QM}$. Also $\angle B = \angle Q$ (by SSS congruence of $\Delta ABD$ and $\Delta PQM$). Hence $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$ by SAS similarity.

**A2.** By AA similarity, $\Delta ADC \sim \Delta BAC$. Therefore $\dfrac{CA}{CB} = \dfrac{CD}{CA}$, so $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.

**A3.** See Example 8 in [hots.md](../handbook/examples/hots.md).

**A4.** By AA similarity, $\Delta AOB \sim \Delta COD$, so the ratios of corresponding sides are equal.

**A5.** The line joining the mid-points is parallel to the third side by Theorem 6.2. The line divides the third side in the ratio $1:1$.

**A6.** In $\Delta ABD$ and $\Delta ECF$, $\angle ADB = \angle EFC = 90°$ and $\angle ABD = \angle ECF$ (angles opposite equal sides $AB$ and $AC$ in isosceles $\Delta ABC$). By AA similarity, $\Delta ABD \sim \Delta ECF$.
