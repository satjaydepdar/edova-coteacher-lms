# Competency-Based Questions

**Q1.** A student writes $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF$ when the correct correspondence is $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DFE$. Explain the error and write the correct similarity statement.

**Q2.** Design a real-life problem that can be solved using the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

**Q3.** A student claims that all isosceles triangles are similar. Is the claim correct? Justify.

**Q4.** Compare the AA and AAA similarity criteria. When would you prefer one over the other?

**Q5.** The sides of $\Delta ABC$ are $3.8$ cm, $6$ cm and $3$ cm. The sides of $\Delta RQP$ are $7.6$ cm, $12$ cm and $6$ cm. Analyse whether the triangles are similar. If yes, state the criterion.

---

## Answers

**A1.** The error is incorrect vertex correspondence. Vertices must be matched so that equal angles correspond. If $\angle A = \angle D$, $\angle B = \angle F$, and $\angle C = \angle E$, the correct statement is $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DFE$.

**A2.** Sample (based on Exercise 6.2): "In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on sides $PQ$ and $PR$ respectively such that $PE = 4$ cm, $QE = 4.5$ cm, $PF = 8$ cm and $RF = 9$ cm. Show that $EF \parallel QR$." Since $\dfrac{PE}{QE} = \dfrac{PF}{RF} = \dfrac{8}{9}$, by Theorem 6.2, $EF \parallel QR$.

**A3.** The claim is incorrect. Isosceles triangles have two equal sides and two equal base angles, but the vertex angle can differ. Corresponding angles need not be equal, so they need not be similar.

**A4.** AA and AAA are equivalent in triangles. AA is preferred because checking two pairs of equal angles is enough; the third pair follows from the angle sum property. AAA is the formal statement of Theorem 6.3.

**A5.** $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{3.8}{7.6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Since the corresponding sides are proportional, by the SSS similarity criterion, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$.
