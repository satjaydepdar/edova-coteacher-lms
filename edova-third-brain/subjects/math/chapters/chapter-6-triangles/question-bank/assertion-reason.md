# Assertion-Reason Questions

In each question, two statements are given:

- **Assertion (A)**
- **Reason (R)**

Choose the correct option:

(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true but R is false.
(d) A is false but R is true.

---

**Q1.** **Assertion (A):** All equilateral triangles are similar.

**Reason (R):** Equilateral triangles have equal corresponding angles.

**Q2.** **Assertion (A):** In $\Delta ABC$, if $DE \parallel BC$, then $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$.

**Reason (R):** Triangles on the same base and between the same parallels have equal areas.

**Q3.** **Assertion (A):** AA criterion is sufficient to prove similarity of two triangles.

**Reason (R):** The sum of the angles of a triangle is $180°$.

**Q4.** **Assertion (A):** SSA is a valid similarity criterion.

**Reason (R):** If two sides and a non-included angle of one triangle are proportional/equal to those of another triangle, the triangles are always similar.

**Q5.** **Assertion (A):** The RHS similarity criterion is proved in Section 6.4.

**Reason (R):** The RHS similarity criterion is mentioned in the "Note to the Reader" at the end of the chapter.

---

## Answers

1. (a) Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A.
2. (a) Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A (the area equality is the basis of the BPT proof).
3. (a) Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A.
4. (d) A is false (SSA is not a valid similarity criterion) but R is also false (SSA does not guarantee similarity).
5. (d) A is false (RHS is not proved in Section 6.4) but R is true.
