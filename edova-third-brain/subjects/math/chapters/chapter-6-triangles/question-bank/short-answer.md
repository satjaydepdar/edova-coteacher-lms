# Short Answer Questions

**Q1.** Define similar figures.

**Q2.** State the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

**Q3.** State the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

**Q4.** In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 3.9$ cm, $EQ = 3$ cm, $PF = 3.6$ cm and $FR = 2.4$ cm. Is $EF \parallel QR$? Justify.

**Q5.** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta RQP$, $AB = 3.8$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 3$ cm, $RQ = 7.6$ cm, $QP = 12$ cm and $PR = 6$ cm. If $\angle A = 80°$ and $\angle B = 60°$, find $\angle P$.

**Q6.** Two equilateral triangles have sides $3$ cm and $6$ cm. What is the scale factor of the first triangle to the second?

**Q7.** State the AA similarity criterion.

**Q8.** In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 4$ cm, $QE = 4.5$ cm, $PF = 8$ cm and $RF = 9$ cm. Show that $EF \parallel QR$.

**Q9.** Are all squares similar? Why?

**Q10.** To which type of triangles does the RHS similarity criterion apply?

---

## Answers

**A1.** Similar figures are figures having the same shape but not necessarily the same size.

**A2.** If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

**A3.** If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

**A4.** $\dfrac{PE}{EQ} = \dfrac{3.9}{3} = 1.3$ and $\dfrac{PF}{FR} = \dfrac{3.6}{2.4} = 1.5$. Since the ratios are not equal, $EF$ is **not** parallel to $QR$.

**A5.** Since $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, by SSS similarity $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta RQP$. Therefore $\angle P = \angle C = 180° - 80° - 60° = 40°$.

**A6.** Scale factor = $\dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

**A7.** If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the two triangles are similar.

**A8.** $\dfrac{PE}{QE} = \dfrac{4}{4.5} = \dfrac{8}{9}$ and $\dfrac{PF}{RF} = \dfrac{8}{9}$. Since the ratios are equal, by Theorem 6.2, $EF \parallel QR$.

**A9.** Yes, all squares are similar because their corresponding angles are equal ($90°$ each) and their corresponding sides are in the same ratio.

**A10.** The RHS similarity criterion applies only to right triangles.
