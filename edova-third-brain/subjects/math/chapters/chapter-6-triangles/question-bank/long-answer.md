# Long Answer Questions

**Q1.** State and prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Theorem 6.1).

**Q2.** Prove the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Theorem 6.2).

**Q3.** Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a trapezium $ABCD$ with $AB \parallel DC$ intersect each other at $O$. Show that $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**Q4.** In $\Delta PQR$, $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR}$ and $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$. Prove that $\Delta PQR$ is an isosceles triangle.

**Q5.** In the figure, $PQ \parallel RS$. Prove that $\Delta POQ \sim \Delta SOR$.

**Q6.** A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long on the ground. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long. Find the height of the tower.

**Q7.** $CM$ and $RN$ are respectively the medians of $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta PQR$. If $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, prove that $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.

**Q8.** $D$ is a point on the side $BC$ of $\Delta ABC$ such that $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$. Show that $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.

## Answers

**A1.** See [basic-proportionality-theorem.md](../handbook/concepts/basic-proportionality-theorem.md).

**A2.** See [basic-proportionality-theorem.md](../handbook/concepts/basic-proportionality-theorem.md).

**A3.** In $\Delta AOB$ and $\Delta COD$, $\angle OAB = \angle OCD$ and $\angle OBA = \angle ODC$ (alternate angles). By AA similarity, $\Delta AOB \sim \Delta COD$, so $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD}$.

**A4.** Given $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR}$, by Theorem 6.2, $ST \parallel QR$. Therefore $\angle PST = \angle PQR$ (corresponding angles). Since $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$ (given), $\angle PQR = \angle PRQ$. Hence $PQ = PR$, so $\Delta PQR$ is isosceles.

**A5.** $PQ \parallel RS$ gives $\angle P = \angle S$ and $\angle Q = \angle R$ (alternate angles). Also $\angle POQ = \angle SOR$ (vertically opposite angles). By AAA similarity, $\Delta POQ \sim \Delta SOR$.

**A6.** By AA similarity, $\dfrac{\text{height of tower}}{6} = \dfrac{28}{4} = 7$. Height of tower = $42$ m.

**A7.** Since $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta PQR$, $\angle A = \angle P$ and $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{CA}{RP}$. As $CM$ and $RN$ are medians, $AM = \dfrac{1}{2}AB$ and $PN = \dfrac{1}{2}PQ$. Thus $\dfrac{AM}{PN} = \dfrac{CA}{RP}$. By SAS similarity, $\Delta AMC \sim \Delta PNR$. Hence $\dfrac{CM}{RN} = \dfrac{CA}{RP} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$.

**A8.** In $\Delta ADC$ and $\Delta BAC$, $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$ (given) and $\angle C = \angle C$ (common). By AA similarity, $\Delta ADC \sim \Delta BAC$. Therefore $\dfrac{CA}{CB} = \dfrac{CD}{CA}$, giving $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.
