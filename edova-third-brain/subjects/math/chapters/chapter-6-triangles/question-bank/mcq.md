## Questions

**Q1.** Which of the following pairs of figures are always similar?

(a) Two squares
(b) Two rectangles
(c) Two rhombuses
(d) Two isosceles triangles

**Q2.** In $\Delta PQR$, $E$ and $F$ are points on sides $PQ$ and $PR$ such that $PE = 4$ cm, $QE = 4.5$ cm, $PF = 8$ cm and $RF = 9$ cm. Then:

(a) $EF \parallel QR$
(b) $EF$ is perpendicular to $QR$
(c) $EF$ intersects $QR$
(d) none of these

**Q3.** If a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is:

(a) perpendicular to the third side
(b) parallel to the third side
(c) equal to the third side
(d) none of these

**Q4.** The AAA similarity criterion is stated as Theorem:

(a) 6.1
(b) 6.2
(c) 6.3
(d) 6.4

**Q5.** If in two triangles, $\dfrac{AB}{DE} = \dfrac{BC}{EF} = \dfrac{CA}{FD}$, then the triangles are similar by:

(a) AA
(b) SAS
(c) SSS
(d) RHS

**Q6.** Which of the following is **not** a valid similarity criterion?

(a) AA
(b) SSS
(c) SAS
(d) SSA

**Q7.** The RHS similarity criterion applies to:

(a) all triangles
(b) equilateral triangles
(c) right triangles
(d) isosceles triangles

**Q8.** All congruent figures are:

(a) similar
(b) not similar
(c) equal in area only
(d) none of these

**Q9.** In Example 7 of the textbook, the length of the girl's shadow after $4$ seconds is:

(a) $0.9$ m
(b) $1.2$ m
(c) $1.6$ m
(d) $4.8$ m

**Q10.** In $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta RQP$, $AB = 3.8$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 3$ cm, $RQ = 7.6$ cm, $QP = 12$ cm and $PR = 6$ cm. The triangles are similar by:

(a) AA criterion
(b) SAS criterion
(c) SSS criterion
(d) RHS criterion

## Answers

1. (a)  2. (a)  3. (b)  4. (c)  5. (c)  6. (d)  7. (c)  8. (a)  9. (c)  10. (c)
