# Case Study Questions

## Case Study 1: Girl and Lamp-post (NCERT Example 7)

A girl of height $90$ cm is walking away from the base of a lamp-post at a speed of $1.2$ m/s. The lamp is $3.6$ m above the ground.

**Q1.** How far does the girl walk in $4$ seconds?

**Q2.** Which two triangles are similar? By which criterion?

**Q3.** Find the length of her shadow after $4$ seconds.

---

## Case Study 2: Pole and Tower (NCERT Exercise 6.3 Q15)

A vertical pole of length $6$ m casts a shadow $4$ m long on the ground. At the same time a tower casts a shadow $28$ m long.

**Q4.** Why are the triangles formed by the pole and the tower with their shadows similar?

**Q5.** Find the height of the tower.

---

## Case Study 3: Trapezium with Parallel Line (NCERT Example 2)

$ABCD$ is a trapezium with $AB \parallel DC$. $E$ and $F$ are points on non-parallel sides $AD$ and $BC$ respectively such that $EF \parallel AB$.

**Q6.** What can you say about $EF$ and $DC$? Why?

**Q7.** Prove that $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{BF}{FC}$.

---

## Answers

### Case Study 1

**A1.** $BD = 1.2 \times 4 = 4.8$ m.

**A2.** $\Delta ABE \sim \Delta CDE$ by AA criterion (right angles and common angle).

**A3.** Let shadow length be $x$ m. Then $\dfrac{4.8 + x}{x} = \dfrac{3.6}{0.9} = 4$, giving $x = 1.6$ m.

### Case Study 2

**A4.** Both triangles are right-angled and share the angle of elevation of the sun, so they are similar by AA criterion.

**A5.** $\dfrac{\text{height of tower}}{6} = \dfrac{28}{4} = 7$. Height of tower = $42$ m.

### Case Study 3

**A6.** $EF \parallel DC$ because both are parallel to $AB$.

**A7.** Join $AC$ intersecting $EF$ at $G$. In $\Delta ADC$, $EG \parallel DC$, so by Theorem 6.1, $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{AG}{GC}$. In $\Delta CAB$, $FG \parallel AB$, so $\dfrac{AG}{GC} = \dfrac{BF}{FC}$. Hence $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{BF}{FC}$.
