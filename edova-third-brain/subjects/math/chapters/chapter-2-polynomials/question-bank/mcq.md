# Multiple Choice Questions

**Q1.** Which of the following is a polynomial?

(a) $\frac{1}{x} + 2$
(b) $\sqrt{x} - 3$
(c) $4x^3 - 2x + 1$
(d) $x^{-2} + 5$

**Q2.** The degree of the polynomial $7x^5 - 3x^2 + x - 8$ is:

(a) 2
(b) 3
(c) 5
(d) 8

**Q3.** A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

(a) linear
(b) quadratic
(c) cubic
(d) biquadratic

**Q4.** The zero of the polynomial $3x - 9$ is:

(a) 3
(b) -3
(c) 9
(d) $\frac{1}{3}$

**Q5.** If $p(x) = x^2 - 5x + 6$, then $p(2)$ is:

(a) 0
(b) 6
(c) 12
(d) 20

**Q6.** The graph of a quadratic polynomial can intersect the x-axis at most at:

(a) 1 point
(b) 2 points
(c) 3 points
(d) 4 points

**Q7.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$, then $\alpha + \beta$ is:

(a) 5
(b) -5
(c) 6
(d) -6

**Q8.** The product of the zeroes of $2x^2 - 7x + 3$ is:

(a) $\frac{7}{2}$
(b) $\frac{3}{2}$
(c) $-\frac{7}{2}$
(d) $-\frac{3}{2}$

**Q9.** A cubic polynomial can have at most:

(a) 1 zero
(b) 2 zeroes
(c) 3 zeroes
(d) 4 zeroes

**Q10.** Which of the following is a linear polynomial?

(a) $x^2 + 1$
(b) $3x + 4$
(c) $x^3 - x$
(d) $x + \frac{1}{x}$

---

## Answers

**A1.** (c) $4x^3 - 2x + 1$

**A2.** (c) 5

**A3.** (c) cubic

**A4.** (a) 3

**A5.** (a) 0

**A6.** (b) 2 points

**A7.** (a) 5

**A8.** (b) $\frac{3}{2}$

**A9.** (c) 3 zeroes

**A10.** (b) $3x + 4$
