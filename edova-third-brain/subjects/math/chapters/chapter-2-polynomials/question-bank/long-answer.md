# Long Answer Questions

**Q1.** Find the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial $x^2 - 5x + 6$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

**Q2.** Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $-3$ and $4$.

**Q3.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $2x^2 - 7x + 3$, find the value of $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$.

**Q4.** Verify that $3$, $-1$, and $-\frac{1}{3}$ are the zeroes of the cubic polynomial $p(x) = 3x^3 - 5x^2 - 11x - 3$, and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

**Q5.** Divide $p(x) = 3x^2 + x - 1$ by $g(x) = x + 1$ and verify the division algorithm.

**Q6.** Prove that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

**Q7.** Find the values of $a$ and $b$ if the sum and product of the zeroes of the polynomial $ax^2 + bx + 6$ are $-3$ and $2$ respectively.

**Q8.** Draw the graph of $y = x^2 - 3x - 4$ and find its zeroes from the graph.

---

## Answers

**A1.** $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$. Zeroes are $2$ and $3$.

Sum = $2 + 3 = 5 = -\frac{-5}{1}$ ✓

Product = $2 \times 3 = 6 = \frac{6}{1}$ ✓

**A2.** Sum $= -3 + 4 = 1$, Product $= -3 \times 4 = -12$.

Polynomial: $x^2 - x - 12$.

**A3.** $\alpha + \beta = \frac{7}{2}$ and $\alpha\beta = \frac{3}{2}$.

$\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = \frac{49}{4} - 3 = \frac{37}{4}$.

**A4.** See [relationship-zeroes-coefficients.md](../handbook/concepts/relationship-zeroes-coefficients.md).

**A5.** $3x^2 + x - 1 = (x + 1)(3x - 2) + 1$. Quotient $= 3x - 2$, Remainder $= 1$.

**A6.** Assume $\sqrt{2} = \frac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms. Then $2q^2 = p^2$, so $p$ is even. Let $p = 2k$. Then $2q^2 = 4k^2$, so $q$ is even. Contradiction. Hence $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

**A7.** $-\frac{b}{a} = -3 \Rightarrow b = 3a$. Also $\frac{6}{a} = 2 \Rightarrow a = 3$. Therefore $b = 9$.

**A8.** Graph is a parabola cutting the x-axis at $-1$ and $4$.
