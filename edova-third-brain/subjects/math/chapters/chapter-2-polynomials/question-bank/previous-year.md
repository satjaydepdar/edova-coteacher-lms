# Previous Year Questions

**Q1.** Find the zeroes of the polynomial $x^2 - 3x - 10$ and verify the relationship between the zeroes and coefficients. (CBSE 2023)

**Q2.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of the polynomial $2x^2 - 5x + 3$, find the value of $\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta}$. (CBSE 2022)

**Q3.** Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $2$ and $-5$. (CBSE 2021)

**Q4.** How many zeroes can a polynomial of degree 4 have? (CBSE 2020)

**Q5.** If the product of the zeroes of the polynomial $ax^2 - 6x - 6$ is $4$, find the value of $a$. (CBSE 2019)

---

## Answers

**A1.** $x^2 - 3x - 10 = (x - 5)(x + 2)$. Zeroes: $5$ and $-2$. Sum $= 3 = -\frac{-3}{1}$, Product $= -10 = \frac{-10}{1}$.

**A2.** $\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta} = \frac{\alpha + \beta}{\alpha\beta} = \frac{5/2}{3/2} = \frac{5}{3}$.

**A3.** Sum $= 2 + (-5) = -3$, Product $= -10$. Polynomial: $x^2 + 3x - 10$.

**A4.** At most 4 zeroes.

**A5.** Product $= \frac{-6}{a} = 4 \Rightarrow a = -\frac{3}{2}$.
