# Case Study Questions

## Case Study 1

A rectangular park has length $(x + 5)$ metres and breadth $(x - 2)$ metres. The area of the park is represented by the polynomial $x^2 + 3x - 10$.

**Q1.** What is the degree of the area polynomial?

**Q2.** If $x = 7$, find the length and breadth of the park.

**Q3.** Find the value of the area polynomial at $x = 7$.

---

## Answers

**A1.** Degree 2.

**A2.** Length $= 7 + 5 = 12$ m, Breadth $= 7 - 2 = 5$ m.

**A3.** Area $= 12 \times 5 = 60$ square metres.

## Case Study 2

The path of a ball thrown upwards is modelled by $h(t) = -t^2 + 4t$, where $h$ is height in metres and $t$ is time in seconds.

**Q1.** What is the degree of the polynomial $h(t)$?

**Q2.** At what times does the ball hit the ground? (Hint: find zeroes of $h(t)$)

**Q3.** What is the maximum time the ball is in the air?

---

## Answers

**A1.** Degree 2.

**A2.** $-t^2 + 4t = -t(t - 4) = 0$, so $t = 0$ and $t = 4$ seconds.

**A3.** 4 seconds.
