# Short Answer Questions

**Q1.** Define the degree of a polynomial.

**Q2.** Find the zero of the polynomial $5x + 10$.

**Q3.** If $p(x) = x^2 - 3x - 4$, find $p(0)$ and $p(-1)$.

**Q4.** Name the type of polynomial: $2x^3 - 5x^2 + x - 1$.

**Q5.** Find the zeroes of $x^2 - 9$.

**Q6.** How many zeroes can a polynomial of degree $n$ have?

**Q7.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$, find $\alpha + \beta$ and $\alpha\beta$.

**Q8.** Give an example of a quadratic polynomial with no real zeroes.

**Q9.** Find the value of $k$ if $x = 2$ is a zero of $p(x) = x^2 - kx + 4$.

**Q10.** What is the maximum number of zeroes of a cubic polynomial?

---

## Answers

**A1.** The degree of a polynomial is the highest power of the variable in the polynomial.

**A2.** $5x + 10 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -2$.

**A3.** $p(0) = -4$ and $p(-1) = 0$.

**A4.** Cubic polynomial.

**A5.** $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$, so zeroes are $3$ and $-3$.

**A6.** At most $n$ zeroes.

**A7.** $\alpha + \beta = -7$ and $\alpha\beta = 10$.

**A8.** $x^2 + 1$ has no real zeroes.

**A9.** $p(2) = 4 - 2k + 4 = 0 \Rightarrow k = 4$.

**A10.** 3.
