# Competency-Based Questions

**Q1.** A polynomial $p(x)$ of degree 2 has zeroes at $-2$ and $5$. Without expanding fully, find $p(1)$.

**Q2.** The graph of $y = p(x)$ is given below. How many zeroes does $p(x)$ have? What is the sign of the leading coefficient?

*(Description: Parabola opening downwards, crossing x-axis at two points)*

**Q3.** Design a real-life situation that can be modelled by a quadratic polynomial. Identify what the zeroes represent in your situation.

**Q4.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are zeroes of $x^2 - 6x + 8$, form a new quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $\alpha + 1$ and $\beta + 1$.

---

## Answers

**A1.** $p(x) = k(x + 2)(x - 5)$. Taking $k = 1$, $p(1) = (3)(-4) = -12$.

**A2.** Two zeroes. Leading coefficient is negative because parabola opens downwards.

**A3.** Example: Profit $P(x) = -x^2 + 10x - 21$, where $x$ is price. Zeroes represent break-even prices.

**A4.** $\alpha + \beta = 6$, $\alpha\beta = 8$. New sum $= (\alpha + 1) + (\beta + 1) = 8$. New product $= (\alpha + 1)(\beta + 1) = \alpha\beta + \alpha + \beta + 1 = 8 + 6 + 1 = 15$. New polynomial: $x^2 - 8x + 15$.
