## Case Study 1: Rectangular Park

A rectangular park has length $(x + 5)$ metres and breadth $(x - 2)$ metres. The area of the park is represented by the polynomial $A(x) = x^2 + 3x - 10$.

**Q1.** What is the degree of the area polynomial?

**Q2.** If $x = 7$, find the length and breadth of the park.

**Q3.** Find the area of the park when $x = 7$.

**Q4.** For what value of $x$ does the breadth become zero? What does this mean physically?

---

## Case Study 2: Ball Thrown Upwards

The path of a ball thrown upwards is modelled by $h(t) = -t^2 + 4t$, where $h$ is the height in metres and $t$ is the time in seconds.

**Q5.** What is the degree of the polynomial $h(t)$?

**Q6.** At what times does the ball hit the ground?

**Q7.** What is the maximum time the ball is in the air?

**Q8.** What is the height of the ball at $t = 2$ seconds?

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Case Study 1**

**A1.** Degree 2.

**A2.** Length $= 7 + 5 = 12$ m, Breadth $= 7 - 2 = 5$ m.

**A3.** Area $= 12 \times 5 = 60$ m².

**A4.** $x - 2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 2$. At this value the breadth becomes zero, so the park collapses to a line; $x$ must be greater than $2$ for a valid rectangle.

**Case Study 2**

**A5.** Degree 2.

**A6.** $-t^2 + 4t = -t(t - 4) = 0$. So $t = 0$ and $t = 4$ seconds.

**A7.** $4$ seconds.

**A8.** $h(2) = -(2)^2 + 4(2) = -4 + 8 = 4$ metres.
