## Based on Lesson 3: Relationship Between Zeroes and Coefficients (NCERT Section 2.3)

**Q6.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$, find $\alpha + \beta$ and $\alpha\beta$.

**Q7.** Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $2$ and $-5$.

**Q8.** If $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ are the zeroes of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$, find $\alpha + \beta + \gamma$ and $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha$.

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** A polynomial in one variable is an expression of the form $a_n x^n + \dots + a_0$, where the powers of $x$ are non-negative integers. Examples: $x^2 + 3x + 2$, $5x^3 - x$.

**Q2.**
(i) Degree 3, cubic
(ii) Degree 2, quadratic
(iii) Degree 1, linear
(iv) Degree 0, constant

**Q3.** Zeroes are the values of $x$ where $p(x) = 0$. A quadratic polynomial can have at most two zeroes.

**Q4.** The polynomial has two equal (repeated) real zeroes.

**Q5.** $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$. Zeroes are $3$ and $-3$.

**Q6.** $\alpha + \beta = -7$, $\alpha\beta = 10$.

**Q7.** Sum $= 2 + (-5) = -3$, Product $= -10$. Polynomial: $x^2 + 3x - 10$.

**Q8.** $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 6$, $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = 11$.

</details>
