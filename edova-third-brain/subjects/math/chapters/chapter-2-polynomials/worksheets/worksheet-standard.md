**Q6.** Divide $p(x) = 3x^2 + x - 1$ by $g(x) = x + 1$ and verify the division algorithm.

**Q7.** Check whether $x - 2$ is a factor of $x^3 - 3x^2 + 4x - 4$.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Answer Key</strong></summary>

**Q1.** Two zeroes; approximately $-1$ and $4$.

**Q2.** Graph is a parabola cutting the x-axis at $-1$ and $4$.

**Q3.** $x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3)$. Zeroes are $2$ and $3$.

Sum $= 2 + 3 = 5 = -\frac{(-5)}{1}$ ✓

Product $= 2 \times 3 = 6 = \frac{6}{1}$ ✓

**Q4.** Sum $= -3 + 4 = 1$, Product $= -3 \times 4 = -12$.

Polynomial: $x^2 - x - 12$.

**Q5.** $\alpha + \beta = \frac{7}{2}$ and $\alpha\beta = \frac{3}{2}$.

$\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = \frac{49}{4} - 3 = \frac{37}{4}$.

**Q6.** $3x^2 + x - 1 = (x + 1)(3x - 2) + 1$.

Quotient $= 3x - 2$, Remainder $= 1$.

Verification: $(x + 1)(3x - 2) + 1 = 3x^2 + x - 2 + 1 = 3x^2 + x - 1$.

**Q7.** Remainder on dividing by $x - 2$ is $p(2) = 8 - 12 + 8 - 4 = 0$. Hence $x - 2$ is a factor.
