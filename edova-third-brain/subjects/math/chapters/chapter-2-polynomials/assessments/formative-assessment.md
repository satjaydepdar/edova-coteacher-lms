### Section A: MCQs (1 × 5 = 5 marks)

**Q1.** Which of the following is a polynomial in one variable?

(a) $\frac{1}{x} + 2$

(b) $\sqrt{x} - 3$

(c) $4x^3 - 2x + 1$

(d) $x^{-2} + 5$

**Q2.** The degree of $2x^3 - 5x^2 + x - 1$ is:

(a) 1

(b) 2

(c) 3

(d) 4

**Q3.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$, then $\alpha\beta$ is:

(a) 5

(b) -5

(c) 6

(d) -6

**Q4.** A quadratic polynomial can have at most:

(a) 1 zero

(b) 2 zeroes

(c) 3 zeroes

(d) 4 zeroes

**Q5.** When $p(x) = x^2 - 5x + 6$ is divided by $x - 2$, the remainder is:

(a) 0

(b) 1

(c) 2

(d) 3

### Section B: Short Answer (2 × 5 = 10 marks)

**Q6.** Define the degree of a polynomial.

**Q7.** Find the zero of $5x + 10$.

**Q8.** If $p(x) = x^2 - 3x - 4$, find $p(0)$ and $p(-1)$.

**Q9.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$, find $\alpha + \beta$ and $\alpha\beta$.

**Q10.** Divide $p(x) = 3x^2 + x - 1$ by $g(x) = x + 1$ and write the quotient and remainder.

---

### Section C: Word Problem (5 × 1 = 5 marks)

**Q11.** A ball thrown upwards follows the path $h(t) = -t^2 + 4t$, where $h$ is height in metres and $t$ is time in seconds. After how many seconds does the ball hit the ground? Explain what the zeroes represent in this situation.

---

## Answer Key

**A1.** (c)
**A2.** (c)
**A3.** (c)
**A4.** (b)
**A5.** (a)

**A6.** The degree of a polynomial is the highest power of the variable in the polynomial.

**A7.** $5x + 10 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -2$.

**A8.** $p(0) = -4$ and $p(-1) = 0$.

**A9.** $\alpha + \beta = -7$ and $\alpha\beta = 10$.

**A10.** $3x^2 + x - 1 = (x + 1)(3x - 2) + 1$. Quotient $= 3x - 2$, Remainder $= 1$.

**A11.** $h(t) = -t^2 + 4t = -t(t - 4) = 0$, so $t = 0$ and $t = 4$. The ball hits the ground after $4$ seconds. The zeroes represent the times when the ball is at ground level.
