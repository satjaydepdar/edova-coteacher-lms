## Section D: Case Study (5 × 2 = 10)

### Case Study 1: Rectangular Park

A rectangular park has length $(x + 5)$ metres and breadth $(x - 2)$ metres. The area of the park is represented by the polynomial $A(x) = x^2 + 3x - 10$.

**Q16.** What is the degree of the area polynomial?

**Q17.** If $x = 7$, find the length, breadth, and area of the park.

### Case Study 2: Ball Thrown Upwards

The path of a ball thrown upwards is modelled by $h(t) = -t^2 + 4t$, where $h$ is height in metres and $t$ is time in seconds.

**Q18.** At what times does the ball hit the ground?

**Q19.** What is the maximum time the ball is in the air? What do the zeroes represent?
