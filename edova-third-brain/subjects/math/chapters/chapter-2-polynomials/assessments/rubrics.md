# Assessment Rubrics

## Chapter 2: Polynomials

---

## Rubric for Proofs and Verifications

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Given/To Prove** | Clearly stated | Mostly clear | Partially stated | Missing |
| **Formula/Theorem Use** | Correct formula cited at each step | Minor omissions | Some incorrect citations | Formulas not cited |
| **Logical Flow** | All steps clear and logical | Most steps clear | Some jumps in reasoning | Reasoning unclear |
| **Algebraic Manipulation** | Correct throughout | Minor error | Some errors | Incorrect |
| **Conclusion** | Clear and precise | Mostly clear | Incomplete | Missing |

---

## Rubric for Numerical Problems

| Criteria | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Satisfactory (2) | Needs Improvement (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Identification** | Correctly identifies what is given and required | Minor error | Partial identification | Missing |
| **Formula Choice** | Appropriate formula selected | Appropriate but not best | Some difficulty | Inappropriate |
| **Working Steps** | Clear and logical | Mostly clear | Some steps missing | Unclear |
| **Calculation** | Correct | Minor arithmetic error | One major error | Incorrect |
| **Final Answer/Units** | Correct with units/context | Mostly correct | Incomplete | Missing |

## Overall Chapter Performance Levels

| Level | Marks Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced | 85–100% | Masters polynomial identification, zeroes, zeroes-coefficients relationship, and division algorithm |
| Proficient | 70–84% | Solves most problems with minor errors |
| Developing | 50–69% | Understands basics but needs practice |
| Beginning | Below 50% | Requires significant remediation |
