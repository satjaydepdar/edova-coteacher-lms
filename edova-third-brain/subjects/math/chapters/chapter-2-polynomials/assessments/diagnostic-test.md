**Q1.** Which of the following is a polynomial?

(a) $\frac{1}{x} + 2$

(b) $\sqrt{x} - 3$

(c) $4x^3 - 2x + 1$

(d) $x^{-2} + 5$

**Q2.** The degree of the polynomial $7x^5 - 3x^2 + x - 8$ is:

(a) 2

(b) 3

(c) 5

(d) 8

**Q3.** A polynomial of degree 3 is called:

(a) linear

(b) quadratic

(c) cubic

(d) biquadratic

**Q4.** The zero of the polynomial $3x - 9$ is:

(a) 3

(b) -3

(c) 9

(d) $\frac{1}{3}$

**Q5.** The graph of a quadratic polynomial can intersect the x-axis at most at:

(a) 1 point

(b) 2 points

(c) 3 points

(d) 4 points

**Q6.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$, then $\alpha + \beta$ is:

(a) 5

(b) -5

(c) 6

(d) -6

**Q7.** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $ax^2 + bx + c$, then $\alpha\beta$ equals:

(a) $\frac{b}{a}$

(b) $-\frac{b}{a}$

(c) $\frac{c}{a}$

(d) $-\frac{c}{a}$

**Q8.** A quadratic polynomial with no real zeroes is:

(a) $x^2 - 4$

(b) $x^2 + 1$

(c) $x^2 - x$

(d) $x^2 - 5x + 6$

**Q9.** When $p(x) = 3x^2 + x - 1$ is divided by $x + 1$, the remainder is:

(a) 0

(b) 1

(c) -1

(d) 2

**Q10.** A polynomial of degree $n$ can have at most:

(a) $n - 1$ zeroes

(b) $n$ zeroes

(c) $n + 1$ zeroes

(d) $2n$ zeroes
