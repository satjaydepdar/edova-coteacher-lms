# Chapter 2 – Polynomials

## Overview

This directory contains curated resources for teaching and learning **Polynomials** in CBSE Class 10 Mathematics.

## Contents

- `handbook/` – Teacher handbook with learning objectives, concepts, examples, and pedagogy notes
- `lesson-plans/` – Ready-to-use lesson plans
- `question-bank/` – Categorized question bank (MCQ, short, long, assertion-reason, case study, etc.)
- `assessments/` – Formative, summative, diagnostic assessments and rubrics
- `activities/` – Classroom, experiential, art, and ICT integration activities
- `worksheets/` – Basic, standard, lesson-aligned, HOTS, and case-study worksheets
- `chapter-2-polynomials.pdf` – NCERT textbook chapter PDF
- `metadata.yaml` – Chapter metadata
