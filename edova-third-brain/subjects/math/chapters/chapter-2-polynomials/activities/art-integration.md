# Art Integration

## Chapter 2: Polynomials

---

## Activity 1: Polynomial Curve Collage

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual or pairs

Students draw the graphs of different polynomials (linear, quadratic, cubic) on coloured paper, cut them out, and arrange them as a collage. Each curve is labelled with its degree, number of zeroes, and leading coefficient sign.

---

## Activity 2: Zeroes and Roots Poster

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Individual

Design a poster showing a quadratic polynomial, its factorisation, its graph, and the relationship between zeroes and coefficients. Include a real-life example such as a ball thrown upwards.

---

## Activity 3: Geometric Meaning of Zeroes

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Create a flipbook or comic strip showing how the graph of a quadratic polynomial moves from cutting the x-axis at two points, to touching it at one point, to not cutting it at all. Label each case with the discriminant condition.

---

## Activity 4: Division Algorithm Storyboard

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Make a storyboard that illustrates the division algorithm for polynomials using characters such as Dividend, Divisor, Quotient, and Remainder. Include a worked example.
