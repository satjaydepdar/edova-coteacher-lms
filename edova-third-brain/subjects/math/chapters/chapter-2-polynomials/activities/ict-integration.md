# ICT Integration

## Chapter 2: Polynomials

---

## Tool 1: GeoGebra / Desmos

**Activity:** Plot polynomials of different degrees and drag the coefficients to observe how the graph changes.

**Tasks:**
- Change the constant term and observe the vertical shift.
- Change the leading coefficient and observe whether the parabola opens upwards or downwards.
- Identify the number of zeroes from the graph.

---

## Tool 2: Interactive Quizzes

**Platforms:** Kahoot, Quizizz, Google Forms

**Quiz Topics:**
- Identifying polynomials and their degrees.
- Finding zeroes from graphs.
- Using the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.
- Applying the division algorithm.

---

## Tool 3: Polynomial Root Finder Apps

**Activity:** Use online polynomial root calculators to verify the zeroes obtained algebraically. Compare exact values with decimal approximations and discuss why exact forms are preferred in examinations.
