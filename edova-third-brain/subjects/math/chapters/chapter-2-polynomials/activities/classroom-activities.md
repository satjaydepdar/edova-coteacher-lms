# Classroom Activities

## Chapter 2: Polynomials

---

## Activity 1: Polynomial Classification Race

**Time:** 15 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Give students a set of algebraic expressions. Ask them to classify each as a polynomial or not, then state the degree and type (linear, quadratic, cubic, etc.).

**Sample Cards:**
- $4x^3 - 2x + 1$
- $\frac{1}{x} + 2$
- $y^2 - 5y + 6$
- $\sqrt{x} + 3$
- $7$ (constant polynomial)
- $x^{-2} + 5$

---

## Activity 2: Graph Matching

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Provide students with graphs of different polynomials. Ask them to match each graph with the number and approximate values of its zeroes, and discuss why some graphs do not cut the x-axis at all.

---

## Activity 3: Zeroes from Coefficients

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Give students the sum and product of zeroes of a quadratic polynomial. Ask them to write possible quadratic polynomials and verify by factorising.

**Example:** Sum $= 7$, Product $= 12$ → polynomial $x^2 - 7x + 12$.

---

## Activity 4: Division Algorithm Verification

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

Students divide a given polynomial by a linear factor using long division and verify the result using the division algorithm:

$$
\text{Dividend} = \text{Divisor} \times \text{Quotient} + \text{Remainder}
$$

**Example:** Divide $3x^2 + x - 1$ by $x + 1$.
