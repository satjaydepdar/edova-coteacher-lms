# Experiential Learning

## Chapter 2: Polynomials

---

## Activity 1: Throwing a Ball

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 3

A ball thrown upwards follows a quadratic path. Students record the height of the ball at different times, plot the points, and model the motion using a quadratic polynomial $h(t) = -at^2 + bt + c$.

**Discussion:** What do the zeroes of $h(t)$ represent? When does the ball hit the ground?

---

## Activity 2: Profit and Loss Polynomial

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

Students create a profit polynomial $P(x) = -x^2 + 10x - 21$, where $x$ is the selling price of an item. They find the break-even prices (zeroes) and decide the price range that gives a profit.

---

## Activity 3: Area of a Rectangular Garden

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Pairs

The area of a rectangular garden is given by $A(x) = x^2 + 3x - 10$, where length is $(x + 5)$ m and breadth is $(x - 2)$ m. Students find valid values of $x$, sketch possible dimensions, and verify the area.

---

## Activity 4: Number Pattern Hunt

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Group Size:** Groups of 4

Students generate number patterns that follow a polynomial rule. For example, the sequence of square numbers $1, 4, 9, 16, \dots$ follows $n^2$. They identify whether a given pattern can be described by a polynomial and, if so, predict the next terms.
