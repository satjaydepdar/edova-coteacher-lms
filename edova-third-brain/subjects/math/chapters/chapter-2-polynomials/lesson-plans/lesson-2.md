# Lesson 2

## Chapter 2: Polynomials
### Topic: Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes (NCERT Section 2.2)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Interpret the zeroes of a polynomial as x-intercepts of its graph.
- Identify the number of zeroes from a given polynomial graph.
- Distinguish between the three cases for quadratic polynomial zeroes.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Graph of $y = p(x)$.
- Zeroes are x-coordinates where the graph cuts the x-axis.
- Linear polynomial: 1 zero.
- Quadratic polynomial: 2, 1, or 0 real zeroes.
- Cubic polynomial: at most 3 zeroes.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Sketch a parabola cutting the x-axis at two points. Ask what the x-coordinates represent.
- **Recall:** Graphs of linear equations from earlier classes.
- **Define:** Geometrical meaning of zeroes.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Linear Graphs:** Show $y = 2x + 3$ and identify the zero.
- **Quadratic Cases:** Draw three parabolas representing 2 distinct zeroes, 1 repeated zero, and no real zeroes.
- **Cubic Examples:** Discuss $y = x^3 - 4x$ and identify zeroes from a table of values.
- **Student Practice:** Complete NCERT Exercise 2.1 graph-based questions in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise: degree $ightarrow$ maximum number of zeroes.
- Assign remaining graph interpretation questions as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** A quadratic graph touches the x-axis at exactly one point. How many zeroes does it have? A cubic graph crosses the x-axis at two points only. Explain.
