# Lesson 1

## Chapter 2: Polynomials
### Topic: Introduction to Polynomials (NCERT Section 2.1)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Define a polynomial in one variable and identify its degree.
- Classify polynomials as linear, quadratic, or cubic.
- Find the value of a polynomial at a given point.
- Identify the zeroes of a polynomial.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- Polynomial: $a_n x^n + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \dots + a_0$, where $n$ is a non-negative integer.
- Degree: highest power of the variable.
- Linear, quadratic, and cubic polynomials.
- Value of $p(x)$ at $x = k$ is denoted $p(k)$.
- Zero of a polynomial: $p(k) = 0$.

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** Write expressions like $3x^2 - 2x + 1$, $\frac{1}{x} + 2$, $\sqrt{x}$ on the board. Ask which are algebraic expressions and which are polynomials.
- **Recall:** Algebraic expressions and exponents from Class IX.
- **Define:** Introduce the formal definition of a polynomial.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Classification:** Give students a mix of polynomials and non-polynomials to classify by degree and type.
- **Value Practice:** Find $p(0)$, $p(1)$, $p(-1)$ for simple quadratics.
- **Zeroes Practice:** Find zeroes of linear polynomials and verify by substitution.
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 2.1 introductory discussion in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise degree, type, value, and zeroes.
- Assign classification and evaluation problems as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** Find the degree and zeroes of $4x - 12$. Is $\frac{1}{x} + 3$ a polynomial? Why or why not?
