# Lesson 3

## Chapter 2: Polynomials
### Topic: Relationship Between Zeroes and Coefficients (NCERT Section 2.3)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Target Audience:** Class X Students

---

### 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- State the relationship between zeroes and coefficients for quadratic polynomials.
- Verify the relationship for a given quadratic polynomial.
- Form a quadratic polynomial when the sum and product of zeroes are given.
- State the extension of the relationship to cubic polynomials.

---

### 2. Key Concepts

- For $ax^2 + bx + c$ with zeroes $\alpha$ and $\beta$:
  - $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$
  - $\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$
- For $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$ with zeroes $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$:
  - $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = -\frac{b}{a}$
  - $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \frac{c}{a}$
  - $\alpha\beta\gamma = -\frac{d}{a}$

---

### 3. Teaching-Learning Process

#### Introduction (10 mins)
- **Hook:** "If I tell you the zeroes of a quadratic are 2 and 3, can you write the polynomial?"
- **Recall:** Factorisation of quadratics by splitting the middle term.
- **Derive:** Show that if $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are zeroes, then $x^2 - (\alpha + \beta)x + \alpha\beta$ is a polynomial with those zeroes.

#### Main Activity (25 mins)
- **Derive Formulas:** Compare $a(x - \alpha)(x - \beta)$ with $ax^2 + bx + c$.
- **Example 1:** Find zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$ and verify the relationship.
- **Example 2:** Find a quadratic polynomial with sum $-3$ and product $2$.
- **Extension:** Briefly introduce cubic relationships with a worked example.
- **Student Practice:** Begin NCERT Exercise 2.2 in class.

#### Conclusion (10 mins)
- Summarise the quadratic zeroes-coefficients formulas.
- Assign remaining Exercise 2.2 questions as homework.

---

### 4. Assessment

- **Exit Ticket:** If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are zeroes of $2x^2 - 5x + 3$, find $\alpha + \beta$ and $\alpha\beta$ without finding the zeroes.
