# Chapter 2 — Chapter Overview

## What this chapter covers

Polynomials builds on Class IX algebra by formalising the study of polynomials in one variable, their degrees, zeroes, graphical meaning, and the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.

## Main sections

| Section | Topic | Core Idea | Key Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Introduction to Polynomials | Degree, types, value, zeroes | Identifying and evaluating polynomials |
| 2.2 | Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes | Zeroes as x-intercepts | Reading number of zeroes from a graph |
| 2.3 | Zeroes and Coefficients | Sum/product relationships | Forming polynomials from zeroes |
| 2.4 | Summary | Consolidation of formulas | Revision and exam preparation |

## What is NOT in this chapter (common confusion)

- **Division algorithm** is not in the current NCERT PDF but is included as a supplementary extension in many curricula.
- **Equations of polynomials in two variables** are not covered.
- **Remainder theorem** is assumed known from Class IX; it is not re-taught here.

## Suggested time allocation (8 periods)

| Period | Topic | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction, degree, types of polynomials | Quick classification quiz |
| 2 | Value and zeroes of polynomials | Worked examples on board |
| 3 | Geometrical meaning — linear and quadratic | Graph sketching discussion |
| 4 | Cases of quadratic zeroes | Group problem-solving |
| 5 | Relationship for quadratic polynomials | Derivation and examples |
| 6 | Cubic polynomial relationships | Verification exercises |
| 7 | Division algorithm (supplementary) | Practice problems |
| 8 | Revision and assessment | Quiz / board-style practice |

## Connection to other chapters

- **Chapter 4 (Quadratic Equations)** — zeroes of quadratics reappear as roots.
- **Chapter 3 (Linear Equations)** — linear polynomials are a special case.
