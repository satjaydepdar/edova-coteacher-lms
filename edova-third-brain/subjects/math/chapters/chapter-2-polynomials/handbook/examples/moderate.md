# Moderate Worked Examples

## Example 1

Find the zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$ and verify the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.

**Solution:**

$$x^2 + 7x + 10 = (x + 2)(x + 5)$$

Zeroes: $-2$ and $-5$.

- Sum: $-2 + (-5) = -7 = -\frac{7}{1}$ ✓
- Product: $(-2)(-5) = 10 = \frac{10}{1}$ ✓

## Example 2

Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes have sum $-3$ and product $2$.

**Solution:** Polynomial is $x^2 - (-3)x + 2 = x^2 + 3x + 2$.

## Example 3

How many zeroes does the polynomial whose graph is completely above the x-axis have?

**Solution:** Zero real zeroes.
