# Easy Worked Examples

## Example 1

Find the degree of $5x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 7$.

**Solution:** Highest power is 3, so degree = 3.

## Example 2

Find the value of $p(x) = x^2 - 5x + 6$ at $x = 2$.

**Solution:** $p(2) = 4 - 10 + 6 = 0$. So $2$ is a zero.

## Example 3

Find the zero of $3x - 9$.

**Solution:** $3x - 9 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 3$.
