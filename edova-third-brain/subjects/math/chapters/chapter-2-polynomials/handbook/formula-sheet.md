# Chapter 2 — Formula Sheet

## General Form

Linear: $ax + b$  
Quadratic: $ax^2 + bx + c$  
Cubic: $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$

## Zero of a Linear Polynomial

For $p(x) = ax + b$:

$$x = -\frac{b}{a}$$

## Quadratic Polynomial

If zeroes are $\alpha$ and $\beta$:

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Sum of zeroes | $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$ |
| Product of zeroes | $\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$ |
| Polynomial from zeroes | $x^2 - (\alpha + \beta)x + \alpha\beta$ |

## Cubic Polynomial

If zeroes are $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$:

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Sum of zeroes | $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = -\frac{b}{a}$ |
| Sum of products two at a time | $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \frac{c}{a}$ |
| Product of zeroes | $\alpha\beta\gamma = -\frac{d}{a}$ |

## Division Algorithm

$$p(x) = g(x) \cdot q(x) + r(x)$$

If $r(x) = 0$, then $g(x)$ is a factor of $p(x)$.
