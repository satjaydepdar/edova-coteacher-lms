# Chapter 2 — Revision Notes

## Most Important Concepts

1. **Degree determines the type:**
   - 1 → Linear
   - 2 → Quadratic
   - 3 → Cubic

2. **Zeroes and coefficients are directly related.** For a quadratic, memorise:
   - Sum = $-\frac{b}{a}$
   - Product = $\frac{c}{a}$

3. **Graphical interpretation:** The number of times $y = p(x)$ crosses the x-axis equals the number of real zeroes.

## Common Board Questions

1. Find the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$ and verify the relationship.
2. Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $3$ and $-2$.
3. If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are zeroes of $2x^2 - 5x + 3$, find $\alpha + \beta + \alpha\beta$.
4. How many zeroes can a cubic polynomial have? Explain with graphs.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Confusing the sign in $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$.
- Forgetting that a quadratic can have no real zeroes.
- Assuming every algebraic expression is a polynomial (check exponents).
