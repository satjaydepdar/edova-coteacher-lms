# Chapter 2 — Chapter Summary

## Key Points

1. A polynomial in one variable has the form $a_n x^n + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \dots + a_0$, where $n$ is a non-negative integer.
2. Degree 1, 2, and 3 polynomials are called linear, quadratic, and cubic respectively.
3. A real number $k$ is a zero of $p(x)$ if $p(k) = 0$.
4. Geometrically, zeroes are the x-coordinates where the graph of $y = p(x)$ cuts the x-axis.
5. A polynomial of degree $n$ has at most $n$ zeroes.

## Formulas

### Quadratic Polynomial $ax^2 + bx + c$

$$\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}, \quad \alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$$

### Cubic Polynomial $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$

$$\alpha + \beta + \gamma = -\frac{b}{a}$$
$$\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \frac{c}{a}$$
$$\alpha\beta\gamma = -\frac{d}{a}$$

### Division Algorithm

$$p(x) = g(x) \cdot q(x) + r(x)$$

where degree of $r(x)$ < degree of $g(x)$, or $r(x) = 0$.
