# Chapter 2 — Visual Learning

## Graphs of Polynomials

### Linear Polynomial
- Shape: Straight line
- Zeroes: Exactly 1 (where line crosses x-axis)

### Quadratic Polynomial
- Shape: Parabola
- Zeroes: 2, 1, or 0 depending on where the parabola intersects the x-axis

### Cubic Polynomial
- Shape: S-shaped curve
- Zeroes: Up to 3

## Suggested Visual Aids

1. **Graph chart:** Show the three cases for quadratic polynomials side by side.
2. **Number line:** Mark zeroes of linear and quadratic polynomials.
3. **Factor tree:** Illustrate how zeroes relate to factors $(x - \alpha)$, $(x - \beta)$.
4. **Animated graph:** Use Desmos / GeoGebra to show how changing coefficients moves the parabola.
