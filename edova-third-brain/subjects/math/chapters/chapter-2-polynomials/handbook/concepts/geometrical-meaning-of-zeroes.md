# Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes

The zeroes of $p(x)$ are the x-coordinates where the graph of $y = p(x)$ intersects the x-axis.

## Linear Polynomial

Graph is a straight line; exactly one zero.

## Quadratic Polynomial

Graph is a parabola. Three cases:
1. Two distinct zeroes — cuts x-axis at two points.
2. One repeated zero — touches x-axis at one point.
3. No real zeroes — completely above or below x-axis.

## Cubic Polynomial

Graph is S-shaped; at most three zeroes.

## General Result

A polynomial of degree $n$ has at most $n$ zeroes.
