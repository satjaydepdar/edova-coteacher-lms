# Introduction to Polynomials

A polynomial in one variable $x$ is an expression of the form:

$$p(x) = a_n x^n + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \dots + a_1 x + a_0$$

where $a_n \neq 0$ and $n$ is a non-negative integer.

## Degree

The highest power of the variable is the degree.

## Types

- Linear: degree 1
- Quadratic: degree 2
- Cubic: degree 3

## Value and Zeroes

- $p(k)$ is the value at $x = k$.
- $k$ is a zero if $p(k) = 0$.

## Example

For $p(x) = x^2 - 3x - 4$:
- $p(2) = -6$
- Zeroes are $-1$ and $4$ because $p(-1) = 0$ and $p(4) = 0$.
