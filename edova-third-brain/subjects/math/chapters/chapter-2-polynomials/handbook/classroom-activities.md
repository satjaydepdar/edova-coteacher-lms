# Chapter 2 — Classroom Activities

## Activity 1: Polynomial Classification Race

Give students a set of expressions. Ask them to classify each as polynomial / not polynomial, and identify the degree and type.

**Examples:**
- $4x^3 - 2x + 1$
- $\frac{1}{x} + 2$
- $y^2 - 5y + 6$
- $\sqrt{x} + 3$

## Activity 2: Graph Matching

Provide students with graphs of polynomials and ask them to match each graph with the number and approximate values of zeroes.

## Activity 3: Zeroes from Coefficients

Give students the sum and product of zeroes. Ask them to write possible quadratic polynomials and verify by factorising.

## Activity 4: Factor Verification

Students divide a given polynomial by a linear factor using long division and verify using the division algorithm.
