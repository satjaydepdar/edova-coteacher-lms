# Chapter 2 — Teacher Reflection

After teaching this chapter, consider:

1. Could students confidently identify the degree and type of any polynomial?
2. Did students understand the graphical meaning of zeroes?
3. Were students able to derive and apply the zeroes-coefficients relationship?
4. Which part needed more time — factorisation, graph interpretation, or cubic relationships?
5. What real-life examples helped students connect with the concept?

## Areas to Reinforce

- Sign conventions in sum/product formulas.
- Difference between zeroes and factors.
- Interpretation of graphs that do not cut the x-axis.
