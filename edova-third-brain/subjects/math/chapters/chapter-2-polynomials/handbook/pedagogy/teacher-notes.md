# Teacher Notes

## Teaching Strategies

1. **Start with Class IX revision:** Review algebraic expressions and factorisation before introducing formal definitions.
2. **Use graphs:** Show how zeroes relate to x-intercepts; this builds visual intuition.
3. **Derive formulas:** Derive sum/product relationships by comparing coefficients rather than asking students to memorise.
4. **Use real examples:** Polynomials appear in area, profit, and motion problems.

## Common Misconceptions

- All algebraic expressions are polynomials. (Not true — check exponents.)
- A quadratic always has two zeroes. (It can have 2, 1, or 0 real zeroes.)
- The zero of a polynomial is the same as the coefficient. (Clarify with examples.)

## Pacing

- Spend extra time on graph interpretation and the sign in sum/product formulas.
