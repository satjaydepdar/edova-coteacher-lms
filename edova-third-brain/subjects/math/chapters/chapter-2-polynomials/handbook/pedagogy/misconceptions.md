# Common Misconceptions

## 1. "Every algebraic expression is a polynomial."

**Correction:** Polynomials require non-negative integer exponents. $\frac{1}{x}$ and $\sqrt{x}$ are not polynomials.

## 2. "A quadratic polynomial always has two zeroes."

**Correction:** It has at most two real zeroes. It may have one repeated zero or no real zeroes.

## 3. Sign error in sum of zeroes

Students often write $\alpha + \beta = \frac{b}{a}$ instead of $-\frac{b}{a}$.

**Fix:** Derive from $a(x - \alpha)(x - \beta)$ and compare coefficients.

## 4. Confusing zero with factor

A zero is a number; a factor is an expression.

**Example:** $2$ is a zero of $x - 2$; $x - 2$ is a factor of $x^2 - 4$.
