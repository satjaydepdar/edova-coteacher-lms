# Chapter 2 — Learning Objectives

## Knowledge (Remember)

- Define a polynomial in one variable and identify its degree.
- Recall the names of polynomials based on degree: linear, quadratic, cubic.
- State the relationship between zeroes and coefficients for quadratic and cubic polynomials.

## Understanding (Comprehend)

- Explain why expressions with negative or fractional powers are not polynomials.
- Interpret the geometrical meaning of zeroes as x-intercepts of $y = p(x)$.
- Describe the possible number of zeroes for linear, quadratic, and cubic polynomials.

## Application (Apply)

- Find the value and zeroes of a polynomial.
- Use the sum and product of zeroes to find a quadratic polynomial.
- Verify the relationship between zeroes and coefficients for given polynomials.
- Apply the division algorithm to divide polynomials and check factors.

## Analysis (Analyse)

- Predict the number of zeroes from the graph of a polynomial.
- Compare polynomials to determine their type and degree.
- Analyse whether a given linear expression is a factor of a polynomial.

## Evaluation (Evaluate)

- Judge the correctness of a factorisation by verifying the division algorithm.
- Select the most efficient method to find zeroes of a quadratic polynomial.

## Creation (Create)

- Construct a quadratic or cubic polynomial given its zeroes.
- Design graphs of polynomials that satisfy given zero conditions.
