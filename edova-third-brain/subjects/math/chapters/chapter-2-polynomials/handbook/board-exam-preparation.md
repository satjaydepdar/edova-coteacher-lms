# Chapter 2 — Board Exam Preparation

## Common Question Types

### 1-mark questions
- Define degree of a polynomial.
- Give an example of a cubic polynomial.
- What is the maximum number of zeroes of a quadratic polynomial?

### 2-mark questions
- Find the zeroes of a given quadratic polynomial.
- Find the value of a polynomial at a given point.

### 3-mark questions
- Verify the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.
- Find a quadratic polynomial with given sum and product of zeroes.

### 5-mark questions
- Prove the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.
- Solve a word problem involving polynomials.

## Preparation Tips

1. Practise all NCERT examples and exercises.
2. Memorise the quadratic zeroes-coefficients formulas.
3. Learn to split the middle term quickly.
4. Practise verifying answers by substitution.
