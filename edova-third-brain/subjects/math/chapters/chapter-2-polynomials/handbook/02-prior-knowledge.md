# Chapter 2 — Prior Knowledge

Before starting this chapter, students should be comfortable with:

## From Class IX

1. **Algebraic expressions** — identifying terms, coefficients, and variables.
2. **Laws of exponents** — especially $x^m \cdot x^n = x^{m+n}$ and $(x^m)^n = x^{mn}$.
3. **Factorisation of quadratic expressions** by splitting the middle term.
4. **Linear equations in one variable** and their solutions.
5. **Basic coordinate geometry** — plotting points on the Cartesian plane.
6. **Graphs of linear equations** — recognising that $y = mx + c$ is a straight line.

## Key Vocabulary to Review

- **Variable:** A symbol (usually $x$, $y$, etc.) that can take different values.
- **Coefficient:** The numerical factor of a term.
- **Constant:** A term without a variable.
- **Factor:** An expression that divides another expression exactly.
- **Zero of a polynomial:** A value of the variable that makes the polynomial equal to zero.

## Diagnostic Check

Ask students to:
1. Identify the degree of $5x^3 - 2x + 7$.
2. Factorise $x^2 + 5x + 6$.
3. Solve $2x + 5 = 0$.
4. Plot the points $(2, 0)$ and $(-1, 0)$ on a graph.
