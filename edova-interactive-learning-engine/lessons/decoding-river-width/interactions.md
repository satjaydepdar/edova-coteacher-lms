# Interactions — Decoding River Width

This file specifies **only the interaction mechanics** (placement, type,
pause/resume behaviour, purpose, timing). Full question content, options,
and answer keys live in `questions.md`. Each interaction below references
its matching question by ID where one exists.

---

## int1 — Prediction

- **Timestamp:** `01:40`
- **Concept:** `c2`
- **Interaction Type:** Prediction
- **Pause Required:** Yes
- **Resume Condition:** Learner submits a free-text or short-choice
  prediction (or taps "Skip" after one attempt).
- **Purpose:** Prime learners to anticipate that an angle alone is
  insufficient — a length is also needed — before the lesson reveals the
  baseline concept in `c3`.
- **Estimated Time:** 20 seconds
- **Related Question:** `q1`

## int2 — Drag and Drop

- **Timestamp:** `02:50`
- **Concept:** `c3`
- **Interaction Type:** Drag Drop
- **Pause Required:** Yes
- **Resume Condition:** Learner drags all three labels (River Width,
  Baseline, Angle of Sight) onto the correct triangle elements and taps
  "Check"; resumes automatically 1.5 seconds after a correct submission,
  or after a manual "Continue" tap following feedback on an incorrect one.
- **Purpose:** Reinforce correct triangle labelling immediately after it is
  introduced, before the tangent ratio depends on students knowing which
  side is which.
- **Estimated Time:** 30 seconds
- **Related Question:** `q2`

## int3 — Fill in the Blank

- **Timestamp:** `04:10`
- **Concept:** `c4`
- **Interaction Type:** Fill Blank
- **Pause Required:** Yes
- **Resume Condition:** Learner types or selects the missing term
  ("tangent" / "tan") and submits; resumes on correct submission or after
  two incorrect attempts (hint is shown after the first incorrect attempt).
- **Purpose:** Check immediate recall of the tangent ratio's structure
  before it is used in a numeric calculation.
- **Estimated Time:** 15 seconds
- **Related Question:** `q3`

## int4 — Multiple Choice Checkpoint

- **Timestamp:** `05:30`
- **Concept:** `c5`
- **Interaction Type:** MCQ
- **Pause Required:** Yes
- **Resume Condition:** Learner selects one of four options; resumes
  automatically after feedback is shown (correct or incorrect).
- **Purpose:** Confirm learners can reason about how changing the measured
  angle affects the calculated width, without needing to compute an exact
  value.
- **Estimated Time:** 25 seconds
- **Related Question:** `q4`

## int5 — Reflection

- **Timestamp:** `06:52`
- **Concept:** `c6`
- **Interaction Type:** Reflection
- **Pause Required:** Yes
- **Resume Condition:** Learner enters a free-text response of at least
  one sentence, or taps "Skip"; no correctness check is applied.
- **Purpose:** Encourage metacognition about measurement error and why
  angle precision matters more than baseline precision at a short baseline.
- **Estimated Time:** 30 seconds
- **Related Question:** `q5`

## int6 — Summary

- **Timestamp:** `07:42`
- **Concept:** `c1`–`c6` (whole-lesson recap)
- **Interaction Type:** Summary
- **Pause Required:** Yes
- **Resume Condition:** Learner orders four summary steps correctly (drag
  to reorder) or taps "Continue" after two attempts; video resumes for the
  10-second outro afterward.
- **Purpose:** Consolidate the full four-step method (frame → measure →
  calculate → verify) as a single memorable sequence before the lesson
  ends.
- **Estimated Time:** 40 seconds
- **Related Question:** `q8`
