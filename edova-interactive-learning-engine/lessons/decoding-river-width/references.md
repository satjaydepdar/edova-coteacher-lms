# References — Decoding River Width

## Concept Sources

- NCERT. *Mathematics — Textbook for Class X*, Chapter 9: "Some
  Applications of Trigonometry." National Council of Educational Research
  and Training, India.
- NCERT. *Mathematics — Textbook for Class X*, Chapter 8: "Introduction to
  Trigonometry" (prerequisite ratio definitions and standard-angle table).
- Survey of India. *Elementary Surveying: Principles and Practice*
  (background reference for baseline-and-angle field survey methodology
  referenced in the lesson's real-world framing).

## Suggested Reading

- NCERT Exemplar Problems — Mathematics, Class X, Chapter 9, for additional
  worked examples at varying difficulty.
- Survey of India educational resources on classical field-surveying
  techniques (baseline measurement, triangulation), for teachers wanting
  deeper real-world context beyond the lesson's scope.
- Any standard Class 10 trigonometric ratio table (sine, cosine, tangent
  for 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°), as a required companion reference for
  students working through `questions.md` independently.
